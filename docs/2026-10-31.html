SpeakerTurn: Target-Speaker Semantic Turn Detection in Multi-Speaker Environments

作者：Nan Li, Hetao Shan, Jintao Liang, Jiahao Yan, Longbiao Wang, Tubin Huang, Min Xu

语音识别评分 20speechspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出SpeakerTurn框架，在多人环境中基于目标说话人参考联合预测目标转录和五种轮次状态，实现目标说话人语义轮次检测。

解决了什么问题

多说话人环境中，仅凭语义完整性无法判断助手何时回应：完整话语可能来自干扰说话人，而目标用户可能仍在说话、确认或请求时间。

核心方法

基于CocktailASR-1的联合预测框架，从语音混合和独立目标说话人参考中同时预测目标转录和五状态轮次（完整、不完整、附和、等待、目标缺失），并包含单独的打断任务；还研究了结构化思维链（CoT）监督。

主要创新点

首次研究以说话人身份为条件的语义轮次推断；提出区分五种轮次状态及打断任务的联合监督机制；引入结构化CoT监督探索决策改进。

实验结论

在合成多说话人测试集上，五状态宏F1达0.811，目标转录字符错误率（CER）为4.20%，正确解决所有十个参考交换打断对；联合格式转录与原生接口识别表现不同，CoT提升验证转录但未提升轮次宏F1。

可能局限

联合格式转录与原生接口识别行为不同，CoT未改善轮次宏F1，表明模型在原生接口上的性能或CoT对轮次决策的增益有限。

适合谁读

语音识别、说话人分离、对话系统、人机交互研究者。

和已有工作的关系

基于CocktailASR-1；涉及语音语言模型在说话人条件交互中的适应。

对研究的启发

揭示了在语音语言模型中需要分别评估目标归属、语义轮次区分和识别保持，为多说话人交互系统的设计与评估提供思路。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为引入了说话人条件轮次检测的新任务，并展示了联合训练中识别与轮次决策的行为差异。

TOP 2

Discovering Interpretable Acoustic Features in Autoregressive Voice-Cloning TTS Models

作者：Anuj Diwan, David Harwath, Eunsol Choi

语音合成评分 14speechvoiceacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出基于特征的可解释性框架，分析自回归语音克隆TTS模型中的声学特征表示与可操控性，发现模型编码音高、能量、噪声和口音等概念但探测与操控并不总是一致。

解决了什么问题

语音克隆TTS模型从简短语音提示合成未见说话人声音，但其内部表示和使用的声学属性不透明，难以解释。

核心方法

利用稀疏自编码器（SAE）、PCA和随机方向提取特征，通过稀疏探测、操控和定量自动可解释性进行分析；以Qwen3-TTS为主，并扩展探测和操控至5个额外模型以测试跨架构泛化。

主要创新点

首次对自回归语音克隆TTS模型进行系统性可解释性研究；系统比较SAE、PCA和随机方向；自动发现684个新概念特征。

实验结论

模型编码音高、能量、噪声和口音等概念，但稀疏预测和操控仅对部分概念有效；SAE在稀疏探测和新概念发现上表现好，PCA在密集探测和操控上具有竞争力；自动可解释性在Qwen3-TTS中发现684个有效SAE特征，涵盖说话人簇、说话风格和录音条件。

可能局限

稀疏预测和操控仅对某些概念有效，强探测不一定转化为操控，反之亦然；主要分析基于Qwen3-TTS，其他模型仅扩展了探测和操控。

适合谁读

语音合成、可解释性机器学习、音频表征研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中未明确说明。

对研究的启发

提示可解释性分析中探测与操控的不一致性，需多角度评估特征重要性；PCA等传统方法在密集分析中仍有竞争力。

是否值得精读

值得深读，提供了对语音克隆TTS内部表征的全面分析和跨模型比较，对理解模型行为有重要价值。

TOP 3

Bridging Neural Representations and Symbolic Semantics in Audio Classification

作者：Avishek Das, Pascal Hitzler

其他评分 14audiomusicacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出模型无关的神经符号框架，通过符号概念归纳将音频分类器隐藏神经元与人类可理解的声学概念关联，实现超越输入级归因的可解释性。

解决了什么问题

深度音频分类器隐藏表示难以解释，现有方法多关注输入级归因（波形段、频谱图区域、声学特征），对隐藏神经元编码内容提供的洞察有限。

核心方法

对最后隐藏层的32个神经元应用符号概念归纳，将神经元激活与符号声学概念关联，并用Mann-Whitney U检验进行统计验证；在GTZAN音乐流派分类和IRMAS乐器分类数据集上评估。

主要创新点

提出模型无关的神经符号框架，直接解释隐藏神经元而非输入级特征；结合统计检验验证神经元-概念关联的显著性。

实验结论

在音乐流派和乐器分类任务上，若干隐藏神经元与有意义的概念关联，大多数确认的神经元-概念对通过Mann-Whitney U检验得到统计支持。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音频分类、可解释AI、符号推理、音乐信息检索研究者。

和已有工作的关系

对比现有音频可解释性方法，这些方法通常聚焦于输入级归因，如波形段、频谱图区域或声学特征。

对研究的启发

表明符号概念归纳是解释潜在音频表示的有前景方向，可扩展到其他音频模型和任务。

是否值得精读

值得深读，提供了从隐藏神经元层面解释音频模型的新思路，并有统计检验支持。

Reading-Level Verification of Kanji Words in Japanese Financial Speech Synthesis

作者：Cheng Zhang

语音识别评分 13speechaudiovoicespeakerspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出基于CTC平假名识别器的阅读级别检查方法，用于验证日语金融语音合成中多读法汉字词的发音正确性，显著优于传统基于ASR转录的质量门。

解决了什么问题

少样本语音克隆合成日语金融评论时，多读法汉字词（如“市场”“大势”）常被误读；而常用质量门（ASR转录后假名匹配）不可靠：误读时ASR语言模型可能写回预期汉字隐藏错误，正确时普通识别错误触发不必要的重新生成。

核心方法

使用连接时序分类（CTC）平假名识别器对预期读音及其易混淆替代读音打分，当替代读音能更好地解释音频时标记该词；与基于转录的基线方法进行对比。

主要创新点

提出针对多读法汉字的阅读级别验证方法，避免ASR语言模型纠错掩盖误读；通过读音打分实现高检测率和低误报率。

实验结论

在540个测试发音上，传统转录门检测率78.9%、误报率44.4%；所提方法检测率91.7%、误报率5.0%（AUC 0.990）；在自然汉字输入上捕获了转录门漏掉的前端错误，并将重新生成请求从38.9%降至6.1%。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音合成、语音识别、日语语音处理、金融科技应用研究者。

和已有工作的关系

对比了基于ASR转录后假名匹配的常用质量门。

对研究的启发

展示了针对特定语言现象设计轻量级验证模块的价值，可推广到其他易混淆发音或多读法场景。

是否值得精读

值得深读，提供了实际生产系统中解决语音合成质量问题的有效方案，并有明确的性能对比数据。

Toward Complete Speaker Anonymization: a Streaming Cascaded System for Voice Privacy and PII Removal

作者：Stanisław Kacprzak, Thomas Thebaud, Najim Dehak

语音合成评分 12speechvoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出级联流式语音隐私系统，整合ASR、NLP、TTS模块实现说话人匿名化和PII移除，达到与离线系统相当的性能。

解决了什么问题

需要实现完整的说话人匿名化，同时能够检测和移除语音中的个人身份信息（PII），并支持流式处理以满足实时应用需求。

核心方法

采用ASR、NLP、TTS级联的流式系统，利用各模块最新进展实现语音隐私和内容保护；在Librispeech上评估内容保留和PII检测能力，并测试对抗半知情攻击者的隐私性能。

主要创新点

实现流式处理，与最先进离线语音隐私系统性能可比；在语音匿名化之外提供可控的PII移除能力。

实验结论

端到端延迟约717ms（发声开始），Librispeech上WER为11%，PII检测SLU F1为69%，对半知情攻击者的等错误率（EER）最高达47.66%。

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音隐私、语音处理、流式系统、安全领域研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与最先进离线语音隐私系统进行比较。

对研究的启发

展示了级联系统在流式场景下实现全面语音隐私的可行性，可进一步探索端到端优化和隐私-效用平衡。

SpeakerTurn: Target-Speaker Semantic Turn Detection in Multi-Speaker Environments2610.39828v1语音识别
展开摘要

Semantic completeness alone cannot determine when an assistant should respond in a multi-speaker environment: a completed utterance may belong to an interfering speaker, while the designated user is still speaking, acknowledging, or requesting time. We therefore study semantic turn inference conditioned on speaker identity. We propose SpeakerTurn, a framework that jointly predicts the target transcript and turn state from a speech mixture and an independent target-speaker reference. Built on CocktailASR-1, it links semantic decisions to the corresponding target content through joint supervision, distinguishing complete, incomplete, backchannel, wait, and target-absent states, with a separate interruption task. We also examine whether structured chain-of-thought (CoT) supervision improves decisions by representing speaker presence and semantic cues. On a synthetic multi-speaker test set, SpeakerTurn achieves five-state macro F1 of 0.811 and target-transcription character error rate (CER) of 4.20%, correctly resolving all ten reference-swapped interruption pairs. Joint-format transcription and native-interface recognition nevertheless behave differently, and CoT improves validation transcription without improving turn macro F1. These results show why target attribution, semantic turn discrimination, and recognition retention require separate assessment when adapting speech language models for speaker-conditioned interaction.

Discovering Interpretable Acoustic Features in Autoregressive Voice-Cloning TTS Models2610.40505v1语音合成
展开摘要

Voice-cloning TTS models synthesize an unseen speaker's voice from brief speech prompts, but it remains unclear which acoustic properties they represent and use. We introduce a feature-based interpretability framework for autoregressive voice-cloning TTS models, analyzing features from sparse autoencoders (SAEs), PCA, and random directions via sparse probing, steering, and quantitative auto-interpretability. We focus on Qwen3-TTS and extend probing and steering to $5$ additional models to test generalization across architectures. These models encode concepts like pitch, energy, noise, and accent, but sparse prediction and steering are effective only for some concepts. Strong probing does not always translate to steering or vice-versa. SAEs are useful for sparse probing of human-labelled concepts and novel-concept discovery, while PCA is competitive for dense probing and steering. Auto-interpretability finds $684$ automatically validated SAE features in Qwen3-TTS covering speaker clusters, speaking style, and recording conditions beyond known concepts. Overall, we perform a comprehensive interpretability study of autoregressive voice-cloning TTS models.

Bridging Neural Representations and Symbolic Semantics in Audio Classification2610.40414v1其他
展开摘要

Deep audio classifiers can achieve strong predictive performance, but their hidden representations remain difficult to interpret. Existing audio explainability methods often focus on input-level attribution, such as waveform segments, spectrogram regions, or acoustic features, and provide limited insight into what hidden neurons encode. Interpreting hidden neurons at the concept level can provide a more meaningful view of what an audio model learns internally. This paper investigates whether hidden neurons in audio classifiers can be associated with human-understandable symbolic acoustic concepts. A model-agnostic neurosymbolic framework is presented for analyzing the final hidden layer of audio classifiers, containing 32 neurons, through symbolic concept induction. The framework is evaluated on two audio classification settings: music genre classification using GTZAN dataset and musical instrument classification using IRMAS dataset. Results show that several hidden neurons can be associated with meaningful induced concepts, and most confirmed neuron-concept pairs are statistically supported using the Mann-Whitney U test. These findings suggest that symbolic concept induction is a promising direction for explaining latent audio representations beyond input-level attribution.

Reading-Level Verification of Kanji Words in Japanese Financial Speech Synthesis2610.39168v1语音识别
展开摘要

Few-shot voice cloning makes it cheap to produce daily Japanese financial commentary with several synthetic speakers, but kanji words with more than one reading, such as the word for "market" (shijo vs. ichiba) or "overall trend" (taisei vs. oze), are still misread. A common quality gate transcribes each synthesized sentence with automatic speech recognition (ASR), converts both the input and the transcript to kana, and regenerates on mismatch. We show that this gate is unreliable in both directions: when the audio contains a misreading, the ASR language model frequently writes the expected kanji back, hiding the error, and when the audio is correct, ordinary recognition errors trigger needless regeneration. We propose a reading-level check that scores the expected reading and its confusable alternatives with a connectionist temporal classification (CTC) hiragana recognizer and flags a word when an alternative explains the audio better. On 540 utterances from three voice-cloned production voices covering 30 financial terms, with misreadings injected under controlled conditions, the transcript-based gate detected 78.9% of misreadings at a 44.4% false-alarm rate, whereas the proposed check detected 91.7% at 5.0% (AUC 0.990). On natural kanji input, it caught a front-end error that the transcript-based gate missed for one voice, and reduced regeneration requests from 38.9% to 6.1% of sentences.

Toward Complete Speaker Anonymization: a Streaming Cascaded System for Voice Privacy and PII Removal2610.39754v1语音合成
展开摘要

We present a cascade streaming voice privacy system, consisting of ASR, NLP, and TTS modules. By leveraging recent improvements in all three aspects of speech and language processing, we show that such a system is capable of achieving a result comparable to state-of-the-art offline voice privacy systems, while providing controllable capabilities of content protection beyond voice. With an estimated utterance-onset end-to-end latency of 717 ms, the presented system preserves speech content with 11% WER on Librispeech, while detecting PII with a 69% SLU F1 score and achieving up to 47.66% EER against a semi-informed attacker.

What Makes a Good Audio Prompt for Zero-Shot TTS? Learning Prompt Selection from Synthesis Outcomes2610.40372v1语音合成
展开摘要

Zero-shot text-to-speech (TTS) models clone a target voice from a short reference clip, commonly referred to as the prompt. In practice, the prompt has to be cut from a longer recording of the speaker, so which part to use must be chosen. To understand what makes a good prompt, we analyze hundreds of thousands of outputs synthesized by four TTS models from candidate prompts of YouTube recordings. The choice of prompt substantially changes intelligibility, naturalness and speaker similarity. Standard predicted-quality scores capture mainly the naturalness part but miss most of the intelligibility gain. We find that the models largely agree on which prompt yields the highest measured speaker similarity, but much less on which prompt yields intelligible speech. We propose a small network that learns from the outputs a TTS model produced to rank a recording's candidate prompts from their audio alone. Without synthesizing anything at selection time, it picks prompts that lower the word error rate on held-out recordings by a third (Qwen3-TTS) to two fifths (F5-TTS) relative to a randomly chosen prompt, more than predicted quality achieves and nearly as much as synthesizing a dozen test sentences from every candidate first.

A learning-based method of automatic music transcription2610.39255v1音乐生成
展开摘要

Automatic Music Transcription (AMT) seeks to convert acoustic music recordings into symbolic musical notation, a process that remains remarkably difficult for polyphonic piano due to concurrent polyphony, harmonic partial interference, expressive timing, and severe pitch activation sparsity. In this paper, we formulate polyphonic piano transcription as a frame-level multi-label classification problem across the standard 88 piano pitches (MIDI notes 21 to 108).
