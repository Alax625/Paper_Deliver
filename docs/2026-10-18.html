How Much Physics Can Vision Learn from Audio? Probing and Distilling Damping Structure

作者：Zainab Afolabi, Vadim Selyutin, Zhao Gao, Babs Khalidson, Mohamed Ghoneim

其他评分 9audioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

探测音频与视觉编码器对物体阻尼品质因子Q的排序能力，并将音频知识蒸馏至视觉模型，发现多帧视觉输入可部分恢复音频优势。

解决了什么问题

视觉模型难以从图像推断物体阻尼等物理属性，音频直接编码此类信息；该工作探究视觉能从音频中学习多少阻尼结构知识。

核心方法

用冻结音频和视觉编码器，以Spearman秩相关ρ评估对品质因子Q的排序；将冻结音频教师通过训练投影头蒸馏到冻结CLIP主干；采用留一材料外协议，对比单帧与四帧输入。

主要创新点

提出音频到视觉的阻尼结构蒸馏方法；引入留一材料外协议避免类别身份干扰；发现多帧视觉输入捕获可见动力学且无需额外声学成本。

实验结论

音频嵌入ρ=0.73，单帧CLIP ρ=0.28；在留一材料外协议下，四帧蒸馏将相关性从0.234提升至0.338（+0.053），且多帧输入提供一半改进。

EVA-Cap: Optimizing Audiovisual Video Captioning via Event-Centric Alignment

作者：Jiapeng Shi, Qiuxia Hou, Jiaqi Leng, Junke Wang, Yanhao Zhang, Haonan Lu, Ziyi Ye, Zuxuan Wu

其他评分 7audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出EVA-Cap框架，将视听视频字幕分解为原子事件并以事件中心对齐优化，显著提升事件中心评估性能。

解决了什么问题

现有视听视频字幕方法将生成视为非结构化文本，忽略视频语义结构，导致细粒度视听对齐不佳。

核心方法

将字幕分解为原子视听事件，构建事件中心对齐；在自建数据集EVA-Data上通过SFT和GRPO训练；GRPO阶段优化全局事件完整性、事件内归因、事件间同步。

主要创新点

提出事件中心对齐的视听字幕框架；将非结构化文本监督转化为结构化事件对齐；设计三个事件中心对齐目标。

实验结论

在事件中心评估上平均增益+9.0%（ChronusAV），通用基准上+3.3%（WorldSense），优于现有方法。

How Much Physics Can Vision Learn from Audio? Probing and Distilling Damping Structure2610.22221v1其他
展开摘要

Physical understanding is crucial for decision making, yet vision models struggle to infer properties such as damping that govern how materials behave. Audio encodes such properties directly, because an object's acoustic response to impact reveals its damping through measurable decay. We probe frozen encoders for the quality factor $Q$, a dimensionless measure of how many oscillations an object completes before its ring dies away, and report how well each embedding orders impacts by $Q$ using the Spearman rank correlation $ρ$, for which $ρ=1$ is a perfect ordering and $ρ=0$ an unrelated one. Audio embeddings reach $ρ=0.73$ and retain most of that advantage on materials withheld from training, while a single video frame taken at the moment of impact, encoded by CLIP, supports only $ρ=0.28$. Having established where the signal lives, we ask how much of that gap vision can recover. We distill a frozen audio teacher into a frozen CLIP backbone through a trained projection head and find that this recovers a useful fraction of the audio advantage. Because pooled correlations on this task are dominated in practice by category identity, we also evaluate under a leave-one-material-out protocol in which the held-out material is withheld from the projection head as well as the probe. Distillation with a four-frame input improves the correlation there from 0.234 to 0.338 (+0.053 over the matched four-frame baseline, 14 of 17 materials), a gain that no category-level strategy can produce. Widening the visual input to four frames spanning the impact supplies half of that improvement at no acoustic cost, which indicates that part of what a single frame misses is visible dynamics.

EVA-Cap: Optimizing Audiovisual Video Captioning via Event-Centric Alignment2610.22186v1其他
展开摘要

Recent advances in Omni Language Models (OLMs) provide a unified solution for audiovisual video captioning. Nevertheless, existing approaches predominantly treat captioning as an unstructured text generation process, neglecting the inherent semantic structure of videos and leading to suboptimal fine-grained audiovisual alignment. To bridge this gap, we propose EVA-Cap, a novel framework that decomposes captions into atomic audiovisual events and transforms unstructured text supervision into event-centric alignment. We train EVA-Cap via Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on a self-curated dataset, EVA-Data, which is built with event-centric quality control. During the GRPO phase, the model is explicitly optimized against three event-centric alignment targets: global event completeness, intra-event attribution, and inter-event synchronization. Extensive experiments demonstrate that EVA-Cap significantly outperforms existing methods in event-centric evaluation (e.g., +9.0% average gain on ChronusAV), while achieving competitive performance on general audiovisual captioning benchmarks (e.g., +3.3% on WorldSense).

Backward Compatibility in Zero-Bit Neural Audio Watermarking2610.22296v1其他
展开摘要

Neural watermarking systems are typically trained as tightly coupled embedder-detector pairs and implicitly assume static deployment. In real-world deployments, embedders and detectors are frequently updated and selectively decoupled for security and versioning purposes, which can break detection in previously embedded content and, more critically, increase false-positive rates on embedders that a given detector is intentionally not intended to recognize. In this paper the concept of backward compatibility in zero-bit audio watermarking is formalized. A training framework is proposed that pairs a new detector with a sequence of frozen legacy embedders (its legacy chain), using a performance-weighted sampling strategy so that the detector retains accuracy across all earlier versions. This coupling, while necessary for backward compatibility, is shown to induce an embedder-collapse effect that catastrophically degrades the true negative rate (TNR) against out-of-chain embedders. To counteract this, a Angular Diversity Loss (ADL) is introduced that explicitly forces each new embedder to occupy a distinct region of the output space. The combined approach restores near-perfect TNR with no measurable cost to detection accuracy or perceptual audio quality.

CLUE: Uncovering Social Context for Pragmatic Understanding2610.22167v1其他
展开摘要

Understanding a conversation requires connecting what is said to the speakers' relationships, expectations, and earlier exchanges. Prior work shows that supplying relevant context can improve models' social judgments, but much of this information may already be present in the conversation. This motivates training models to discover the cues that matter and use them to support an interpretation. We propose CLUE, a two-stage training pipeline that combines supervised evidence reasoning with GRPO guided by Grounded Information Utilization ($\mathrm{IU}_{\mathrm{G}}$). This reward credits new, evidence-supported social inferences while accounting for reasoning effort. We train on a small set of conversational examples to assess effectiveness and generalization across tasks, languages, and genres. At inference time, the trained model reasons over the supplied interaction in a self-contained manner. Compared with base Qwen, CLUE achieves relative gains of 17.3% in-domain and 9.3% out-of-domain in average weighted F1, with higher F1 on four of five out-of-domain evaluations. These gains are accompanied by over 80% fewer reasoning tokens on average in both domains. Human evaluation further finds that CLUE more often grounds its explanations in specific conversational content, with closer alignment between its quoted evidence and human-selected context.
