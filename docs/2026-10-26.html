Noisy Target Training Meets Drifting for Weakly-Supervised Speech Enhancement

作者：Yoshiki Masuyama, Dominik Klement, Christoph Boeddeker, Julius Richter, Kohei Saijo, Gordon Wichern, Takahiro Edo, Jonathan Le Roux

语音增强评分 17speechvoicespeech enhancement查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

本文提出在噪声目标训练（NyTT）中引入Sinkhorn drifting loss，通过匹配增强语音与未配对干净语音的分布来减少残余噪声。

解决了什么问题

标准监督语音增强需要配对数据且存在域不匹配，而NyTT虽缓解了域不匹配但可能学习在增强输出中留下残余噪声。

核心方法

在NyTT框架中引入Sinkhorn drifting loss，该损失在潜在空间中吸引增强语音靠近未配对干净语音分布的高密度区域，同时排斥远离当前增强语音分布。

主要创新点

将Sinkhorn drifting loss与NyTT结合用于弱监督语音增强；该损失仅使用未配对干净语音，保持了弱监督性质；在潜在空间中进行分布匹配。

实验结论

在VoiceBank-DEMAND和CHiME-3真实录音上的实验结果表明了所提组合方法的有效性。

相关工作包括标准监督语音增强训练、噪声目标训练（NyTT）以及分布匹配方法如Sinkhorn损失。

该工作提示在弱监督设置下，可通过分布匹配损失引导模型输出更接近干净语音分布，减少对配对数据的依赖。

CoupleFlow: Null-Calibrated Auditory Attention Decoding With Time-Locked Brain-Speech Coupling and Flow-Matching Likelihood Ratios

作者：Ali Alavi, Donald S. Williamson

其他评分 13speechaudioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

本文提出CoupleFlow，通过神经和声学嵌入的时间耦合及条件流匹配似然比进行听觉注意力解码，在多个数据集上优于基线且空测试保持在随机水平。

解决了什么问题

现有EEG听觉注意力解码器报告高准确度但不能推广到未见语音，因为模型利用试验泄漏、眼动凝视和声学线索等捷径，而非语音诱发的神经活动。

核心方法

CoupleFlow通过神经和声学嵌入的时间耦合进行解码；使用条件流匹配头实现基于脑和语音逐点互信息的似然比决策规则；当脑信号无信息耦合时预测保持随机。

主要创新点

时间锁定脑-语音耦合；条件流匹配似然比决策规则；空校准设计防止捷径；证据积累定律预测准确性与窗口长度关系。

实验结论

在九个EEG和MEG数据集上，CoupleFlow优于线性和深度基线，所有空测试保持在随机水平；证据积累定律表明记录信噪比是解码速度和可靠性的限制。

SEMAC: Spherical Energy Matching Ambisonics Codec for Efficient Higher-Order Ambisonics Compression

作者：Christoph Hold, Jan Buethe, Jean-Marc Valin, Jan Skoglund, Toni Hirvonen, Nils Peters

其他评分 3audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

本文提出SEMAC编解码器，通过量化的全阶方向能量包络和较少CELT编码传输通道来高效压缩高阶Ambisonics，MUSHRA测试表明其感知质量优于Opus CMF~3。

解决了什么问题

高阶Ambisonics高通道数和方向性特性导致编码挑战，现有Opus编码所有通道导致比特率随球谐阶数二次增长。

核心方法

SEMAC用量化的全阶方向能量包络和较少的CELT编码传输通道表示Ambisonics信号；解码器重新施加方向能量包络恢复全阶方向能量分布。

主要创新点

将CELT的能量包络分离原理推广到方向信号；通过减少传输通道并用方向包络补偿，避免编码所有HOA通道；实现高效HOA压缩。

实验结论

MUSHRA测试表明，在512和768 kbit/s下，SEMAC（使用16/36传输通道）的评级高于全阶CMF~3；在传输通道数相等时高于阶截断，感知上优于低精度编码所有HOA通道。

作者：Ankit Gupta, Akshat Pandya, Christoph Adami, Emily Dolson

自监督学习评分 2self-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

本文通过稀疏像素优化在空白画布上编辑，发现现代视觉编码器在远离自然图像流形处存在共享的离流形失效模式，稀疏编辑可进入目标语义盆地。

解决了什么问题

现代视觉编码器在远离自然图像流形时，嵌入相似性与人类可见语义之间的关系可能变得不清楚，其表示几何存在离流形失效模式。

核心方法

在空白画布上应用稀疏像素编辑，使用像素级爬山过程进行黑盒优化，评估编辑后的画布在多模态对比编码器和自监督视觉编码器中检索目标的能力。

主要创新点

提出稀疏空白画布优化作为表示几何的简单探针；在两种类型编码器上发现共享的离流形失效模式；证明稀疏编辑可进入目标语义盆地。

实验结论

在ImageNet和Flickr30k上，针对OpenCLIP类提示优化的输入实现了强top-1和top-5定向检索性能；针对DINOv3类原型优化的输入实现了高目标对齐和接近顶部的原型排名。

Noisy Target Training Meets Drifting for Weakly-Supervised Speech Enhancement2610.32870v1语音增强
展开摘要

In speech enhancement, standard supervised training requires pairs of noisy and clean speech. Such pairs are typically created by artificially degrading clean speech, leading to a domain mismatch between synthetic training data and real recordings. Noisy target training (NyTT) mitigates this limitation by training an enhancement model to predict a noisy recording from a noisier version generated by adding extra noise. However, since the training target is noisy, NyTT might learn to leave residual noise in the enhanced output. In this paper, we address this issue in NyTT by incorporating a Sinkhorn drifting loss that matches the distribution of the enhanced speech to that of unpaired clean speech. The Sinkhorn drifting loss attracts enhanced speech toward high-density regions of the clean-speech distribution while repelling it from the current enhanced-speech distribution in latent space. This loss uses only unpaired clean speech rather than the clean counterpart of each degraded recording, preserving the weakly-supervised nature of NyTT. Experimental results on VoiceBank-DEMAND and CHiME-3 real recordings demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed combination.

CoupleFlow: Null-Calibrated Auditory Attention Decoding With Time-Locked Brain-Speech Coupling and Flow-Matching Likelihood Ratios2610.32950v1其他
展开摘要

Auditory attention decoding (AAD) seeks to identify the attended talker from brief noninvasive brain recordings by comparing neural responses with competing speech streams. Recent electroencephalography (EEG) decoders report nearly perfect accuracy from one second of data, yet we show that this performance fails to generalize to unseen speech because models exploit trial leakage, eye gaze, and acoustic cues instead of speech evoked neural activity. We introduce CoupleFlow, which decodes attention through temporal coupling between neural and acoustic embeddings. Its predictions remain at chance when the brain signal contains no informative coupling, preventing shortcuts based on speech content or talker identity. A conditional flow matching head implements a likelihood ratio decision rule based on pointwise mutual information between brain and speech. This rule is Bayes optimal when the recording depends only on the attended talker and removes preferences for speech that is likely a priori. We evaluate CoupleFlow with content disjoint splits recovered from the audio and null tests that distinguish genuine temporal coupling from trial recognition. Across nine EEG and magnetoencephalography (MEG) datasets processed through a common pipeline, spanning four languages, high density EEG recordings, listeners with hearing impairment, and a room with multiple talkers, CoupleFlow outperforms linear and deep baselines while all null tests remain at chance. Finally, we derive an evidence accumulation law that predicts accuracy across window lengths from a single parameter per dataset and predicts that parameter from the strength of neural speech tracking. This law identifies recording signal to noise ratio as a limit on decoding speed and reliability and estimates the window length required to reach a target accuracy.

SEMAC: Spherical Energy Matching Ambisonics Codec for Efficient Higher-Order Ambisonics Compression2610.32981v1其他
展开摘要

Higher-order Ambisonics (HOA) is an attractive format for immersive spatial audio, but its high channel count and directional properties present significant audio coding challenges. The widely deployed Opus audio codec supports HOA through Channel Mapping Families 2 and 3 (RFC 8486), which code and transmit every Ambisonics channel, so the bitrate grows quadratically with the spherical harmonic order. We introduce SEMAC, which instead represents an Ambisonics signal by a quantized full-order directional energy envelope together with fewer CELT-coded transport channels. CELT separates a per-band energy envelope from the signal shape and restores it after quantization, and SEMAC generalizes this principle to directional signals. Since the reduced transport spans only a lower-dimensional subspace, the decoder re-imposes the envelope observed at the encoder input, restoring the directional energy distribution at full order. A MUSHRA test on fifth-order material compares SEMAC against Opus CMF~3 in matched configurations at 512 and 768 kbit/s. SEMAC with 16 of 36 transport channels is rated above full-order CMF~3 at both rates, and at equal transport channel count above order truncation. The proposed SEMAC strategy of coding fewer transport channels with the directional envelope is found perceptually preferable to coding all HOA channels at low precision.

SPOOK: Sparse Pixel Optimization for Off-Manifold Knowledge in Vision Encoders2610.32835v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Modern vision encoders are widely used as semantic representations, but the relationship between embedding similarity and human-visible semantics can become unclear far from the natural-image manifold. We probe this regime in a black-box setting by applying sparse pixel edits to blank canvases with a simple pixel-level hill-climbing procedure. Across ImageNet and Flickr30k, we evaluate whether these edited blank canvases can retrieve chosen targets in multimodal contrastive encoders and self-supervised visual encoders. On OpenCLIP, inputs optimized toward class prompts achieve strong top-1 and top-5 targeted retrieval performance; on DINOv3, inputs optimized toward class prototypes achieve high target alignment and near-top prototype ranks. These results show that sparse edits on otherwise blank, visually non-semantic inputs can enter target semantic basins in both language-aligned and self-supervised visual representation spaces. Our findings identify a shared off-manifold failure mode of modern vision encoders and suggest sparse blank-canvas optimization as a simple probe of representation geometry.
