Towards Trustworthy Mobile Speech Interaction: Ensuring Voice Authenticity and Transmission Integrity

作者：Guodong Liu, Lei Wang, Dongxu Liu, Qianran Qiao, Haipeng Dai, He Huang, Guihai Chen

语音合成评分 18speechvoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

MoBiMark 在智能手机上实时发射超声波并提取声道回声特征，与语音内容嵌入融合生成双层动态水印，用于移动语音交互中的声源认证和传播篡改检测。

解决了什么问题

生成式语音模型使声纹认证易受攻击，现有语音水印无法在采集时验证实时语音是否来自真实说话人。

核心方法

利用商用手机发射多频超声波，从声道回声提取特征并与内容相关语音嵌入融合，生成包含持久用户标识UID和每句语音标识SID的双层动态水印，联合训练嵌入与检测器并做INT8量化部署。

主要创新点

将超声波发音特征与语音内容绑定，实现采集时实时双层水印；支持声源认证和传播时间篡改检测；系统级INT8量化可端侧运行。

实验结论

在50名用户、4款智能手机上，对声源和传播攻击的平均检测准确率为99.79%，对语音质量和可懂度影响可忽略。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

移动语音安全、声纹认证、语音水印、端侧语音处理研究者。

和已有工作的关系

现有语音水印方法侧重于离线保护或为合成语音加水印，无法在采集时验证真实说话人。

对研究的启发

可将生物特征与内容水印结合、通过超声波硬件实现实时端侧认证，为语音安全提供新的感知维度。

是否值得精读

是，提供了实时端侧生物语音水印的完整实现与用户实验，但需关注跨设备与真实攻击场景的泛化。

TOP 2

Textraction: Unified Text-Guided Speaker Extraction and Enhancement for Single- and Multi-Channel Audio

作者：Aviad Eisenberg, Sharon Gannot, Shlomo E. Chazan

语音增强评分 15speechaudioacousticspeakerspeech enhancement查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

Textraction 是一个统一的文本引导说话人提取框架，支持单通道和多通道输入，并可根据语义或空间描述直接从混合音频中提取目标声源。

解决了什么问题

现有文本引导说话人提取通常依赖昂贵的大语言模型，或缺乏对单/多通道和空间描述的统一下支持；需要在混合音频中仅凭自然语言描述选择性提取或移除目标声源。

核心方法

单通道与多通道编码器分别编码后送入共享时频Transformer块，每个块通过交叉注意力以提示文本和录音配置为条件，直接估计目标源；文本编码器由紧凑文本检索模型对比微调得到。

主要创新点

统一支持单/多通道、语义描述与空间描述；无需分离或声源选择阶段，单次前向估计；可提取任意子集；轻量文本编码器对keep/remove极性和改写稳健。

实验结论

在基于属性提取、空间引导提取和语音增强实验中，方法能有效隔离目标实体，并达到或超过专用空间提取与增强基线，同时提供更大灵活性。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

说话人提取、语音增强、多通道音频处理、文本引导声源分离研究者。

和已有工作的关系

先前工作常使用计算开销较大的大语言模型，或专用空间提取/增强模型。

对研究的启发

文本提示与声学配置在时频Transformer中的统一条件设计，以及对比微调紧凑文本编码器的思路，可迁移到其他可控音频生成/增强任务。

是否值得精读

是，统一单/多通道文本引导提取并在多个设定下验证，灵活性和效率值得参考。

TOP 3

REAL: Reward Modeling through Evidence-Linked Analysis of Lifelikeness in Generated Speech

作者：Yaodong Song, Jie Lian, Xusheng Yang, Genliang Zhao, Hongjie Chen, Jian Kang, Zhen-Hua Ling, Jie Li, Yongxiang Li

语音合成评分 14speechaudioacoustictext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

REAL 通过证据链接分析生成语音的类人程度，监督成对偏好和结构化证据报告，从而提升语音奖励模型对细微差异的判断能力。

解决了什么问题

现有语音奖励模型仅在语句或配对层面监督，无法显式连接局部声学现象与最终偏好，难以精细评估合成语音的类人程度。

核心方法

REAL 框架同时监督成对偏好和结构化证据报告，由 REAL-Jury 用时间戳对齐、多模型分析和争议导向审议构建证据链，REAL-Judge 从配对音频生成这些报告；REAL-Eval 分两级评估偏好预测。

主要创新点

引入证据链接的奖励建模，显式关联局部音频片段与感知现象、受影响维度和整体判断；提供两级评估更贴近强TTS系统间的细微差异。

实验结论

以 MiMo-Audio 实例化，REAL-Judge 在两个评估层级上较其骨干模型分别提高16.01和11.41个百分点，并优于开源语音奖励模型 SpeechJudge 和 UniSRM。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音合成评估、奖励建模、可解释AI、TTS开发与数据筛选研究者。

和已有工作的关系

现有语音奖励模型可预测分数或成对偏好并生成自然语言理由，但通常在语句或配对层面监督，未显式关联局部声学证据。

对研究的启发

证据链接与结构化推理可作为自动化评估的可解释性增强路径，也可用于其他生成任务的奖励模型。

是否值得精读

是，在偏好预测之外引入证据链监督和两级评估，对TTS评估有实际价值。

TOP 4

Text Grouping Changes Completion-Time Comparisons in Long-Form Simultaneous Speech Translation

作者：Hieu Minh Truong, Dung D. Le

语音合成评分 12speechaudiovoicespeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该研究考察文本分组方式对长形式同声传译完成时间比较的影响，发现不同合成器下分组可产生相反效果，语音计时比较需明确文本进入合成的方式。

解决了什么问题

在长形式同声传译中，翻译更新在合成前的文本分组方式是否会改变不同系统完成时间的比较结果。

核心方法

固定译文文本和可用时间，仅改变送入语音合成的文本分组方式，对比 StreamAtt 与固定音频历史基线在十场英德演讲及二十场面板上的完成时间；还观察 CosyVoice 3 连续会话中的表现。

主要创新点

揭示文本分组策略对同声传译完成时间比较的显著影响，强调语音计时比较需明确文本进入合成的方式，而不只是指定合成器。

实验结论

四秒分组在 XTTS-v2 下使 StreamAtt 平均完成领先从106.6秒增至229.1秒，但在 Piper 下从169.5秒降至146.5秒；CosyVoice 3 连续会话中分组使20个块中19个完成延迟；语音计时比较需说明文本如何进入合成。

可能局限

摘要指出识别和自动词计时描述输出，但不建立翻译准确度或可懂度；完成时间结论可能受特定合成器和文本分组设置影响。

适合谁读

同声传译、语音合成集成、语音翻译评估与系统比较研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与固定音频历史基线对比，使用 XTTS-v2、Piper 和 CosyVoice 3 等合成器。

对研究的启发

语音翻译系统的计时评估应控制文本分组等前端因素；评估指标需区分完成时间与翻译质量/可懂度。

是否值得精读

是，提出了同声传译系统比较中易被忽略的文本分组变量，但对翻译质量未作判断，适合关注评测方法者。

LAION-Audio-XL: A Web-Scale Corpus and Scaling Studies for Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining

作者：Gijs Wijngaard, Felix Förster, Ross Wightman, Marianna Nezhurina, Christoph Schuhmann, Jenia Jitsev, A. Sophia Koepke

其他评分 12audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

LAION-Audio-XL 构建了包含14.8亿音频样本的网络级语料，并基于 NaFlex 架构研究对比音频-语言模型的扩展行为，训练出 CLAP2 模型。

解决了什么问题

对比音频-语言模型的性能受训练数据规模与质量限制，需要构建更大规模网络级音频数据集并理解模型与数据的扩展行为。

核心方法

从学术数据集、互联网数据、网络采集、Common Crawl 和 YouTube 收集14.8亿音频样本，经多阶段清洗，并为缺字幕样本生成短/长字幕；基于 NaFlex 音频编码器研究对比音频-语言模型的扩展规律，训练 CLAP2 模型。

主要创新点

构建并发布大规模音频-文本语料；生成缺失字幕；在固定计算预算下分析模型大小与训练数据的权衡；开源数据、字幕、模型与代码。

实验结论

在 MAEB 和 HEAR 基准上，CLAP2 优于同等规模的前代音频-文本模型；实验识别了给定计算预算下模型规模与训练数据之间的权衡。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音频-语言对比学习、大规模数据集构建、音频表示学习、多模态预训练研究者。

和已有工作的关系

此前对比音频-语言模型进展主要归因于训练数据规模与质量，已有音频-文本模型为比较对象。

对研究的启发

大规模数据工程与缩放实验可为多模态对比预训练提供资源和分析框架；开源语料与模型有助于复现与扩展。

是否值得精读

是，数据集规模与开放资源对音频-语言预训练社区具有参考价值，但需关注数据来源版权与清洗细节。

Towards Trustworthy Mobile Speech Interaction: Ensuring Voice Authenticity and Transmission Integrity2611.11777v1语音合成
展开摘要

The rapid progress of generative speech models has made high-fidelity voice cloning and editing cheap, fast, and widely accessible, weakening the security of voiceprint-based authentication in mobile speech interactions. Existing speech watermarking techniques focus on offline protection or watermarking synthetic speech, and thus cannot verify whether a live utterance originates from the genuine speaker at capture time. We present \textit{MoBiMark}, a real-time \textbf{mo}bile \textbf{bi}ologic speech water\textbf{mark}ing system that binds ultrasonic articulatory signatures to speech content and embeds a dynamic watermark during capture. MoBiMark emits a multi-frequency ultrasonic probe on commodity smartphones, extracts echo features from the vocal tract, and fuses them with a content-dependent speech embedding to produce a dual-layer dynamic watermark combining a persistent user identifier (UID) and a per-utterance speech identifier (SID), enabling both source authentication and propagation-time tamper detection. A jointly trained embedder and detector injects the watermark while preserving perceptual quality, and system-level optimization with INT8 quantization enables lightweight on-device deployment. Evaluated on 50 users across four smartphone models, MoBiMark achieves an average 99.79% accuracy in source and propagation attacks, with negligible impact on speech quality and intelligibility.

Textraction: Unified Text-Guided Speaker Extraction and Enhancement for Single- and Multi-Channel Audio2611.12493v1语音增强
展开摘要

We present Textraction, a text-guided speaker extraction framework that enables the selective extraction of desired speakers or noise sources from an audio mixture using only natural-language descriptions. The proposed method operates within a single unified model that supports both single-channel and multi-channel inputs and it allows conditioning on either semantic textual descriptions of the target source or on its spatial description. Dedicated single- and multi-channel encoders feed a shared stack of time--frequency transformer blocks, which is conditioned on the prompt and on the recording configuration in every block through cross-attention and estimates the target directly from the mixture in one forward pass, without a separation stage or a source-selection stage. The system can extract an arbitrary subset of entities from a mixture, assuming up to two simultaneously active speakers and two noise sources, and interprets prompts specifying which entities to keep, which to remove, or both. In addition, we show that the framework naturally applies to a speech-enhancement setting, in which a single target speaker is present alongside background noise. To circumvent the application of computationally costly large language models used in prior work, we introduce a lightweight, task-adapted text encoder obtained by contrastively fine-tuning a compact text-retrieval model, which learns to be sensitive to instruction polarity (e.g., keep vs. remove) while remaining robust to paraphrasing. Experiments across attribute-based extraction, spatially guided extraction, and speech enhancement show that the proposed approach effectively isolates the desired entities under various acoustic and spatial conditions, and matches or surpasses dedicated spatial-extraction and enhancement baselines while offering substantially greater flexibility.

REAL: Reward Modeling through Evidence-Linked Analysis of Lifelikeness in Generated Speech2611.12406v1语音合成
展开摘要

As modern text-to-speech systems become increasingly intelligible and natural, evaluation is shifting from detecting gross synthesis failures to identifying subtle departures from lifelike human delivery. Because large-scale listening tests are costly and slow, speech reward models can serve as scalable automatic judges for comparing generated utterances, curating data, and supporting iterative TTS development. Existing speech reward models can predict scores or pairwise preferences and may generate natural-language rationales, but their supervision is typically defined at the utterance or pair level. The connection between local acoustic phenomena and the final preference therefore remains implicit. We introduce REAL, a framework for Reward modeling through Evidence-linked Analysis of Lifelikeness in generated speech. Its central idea is to supervise not only the final pairwise preference but also a structured account of its supporting observations, linking local audio spans to perceptual phenomena, affected dimensions, and the overall judgment. Specifically, REAL-Jury constructs these evidence-linked reports through timestamp alignment, multi-model analysis, and dispute-oriented deliberation. REAL-Judge learns to generate them from paired audio inputs. REAL-Eval assesses preference prediction in two tiers, from salient perceptual differences to subtle distinctions among strong TTS systems. Instantiated with MiMo-Audio, REAL-Judge improves pairwise preference accuracy over its backbone by 16.01 and 11.41 percentage points on the two tiers, respectively, and outperforms the open-source speech reward models SpeechJudge and UniSRM on both tiers.

Text Grouping Changes Completion-Time Comparisons in Long-Form Simultaneous Speech Translation2611.12501v1语音合成
展开摘要

Does grouping translation updates before speech synthesis change the measured completion gap between two translators? We compare StreamAtt with its fixed-audio-history baseline on ten English-German talks. For each translation, we keep the intended text and its availability times fixed and vary how updates are grouped for synthesis. Four-second grouping increases StreamAtt's mean completion lead from 106.6 to 229.1 seconds with XTTS-v2, but reduces it from 169.5 to 146.5 seconds with Piper. Both produce shorter audio; different changes in playback idle account for the opposite effects. These directions recur on a separately selected twenty-talk panel. In continuing CosyVoice 3 sessions, grouping instead leaves paired audio unchanged and delays completion on nineteen of twenty blocks. A speech-timing comparison therefore needs to specify how text enters synthesis, not just which synthesizer is used. Recognition and automatic word timing describe the outputs, but do not establish translation accuracy or intelligibility.

LAION-Audio-XL: A Web-Scale Corpus and Scaling Studies for Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining2611.11592v1其他
展开摘要

Recent progress on contrastive audio-language models can be attributed mainly to the scale and quality of their training data. Larger training datasets expose models to a broader range of sounds and corresponding audio captions. To exploit this, we collect 1.48 billion audio samples from academic datasets, internet datasets, web collections, Common Crawl, and YouTube, and process them with a multi-stage curation pipeline. Furthermore, we generate short and long captions for samples in our corpus that lack them. We use our dataset to study the scaling behavior of contrastive audio-language models based on the NaFlex architecture for the audio encoder. Our experiments identify trade-offs between model size and training data for a given compute budget. Moreover, our resulting models, CLAP2, outperform prior audio-text models of comparable size on the MAEB and HEAR benchmarks. We release our dataset, captions, model checkpoints, and code.

AudioCodex: Streaming Autoencoders for Live Audio2611.12375v1其他
展开摘要

We introduce AudioCodex, a fully causal autoencoder for live generative audio applications that compresses stereo audio into compact continuous latent sequences at ~10 Hz. It combines a Mean Flow magnitude autoencoder with latent hypersphere projection, bounded-noise augmentation, representation alignment, and adversarial phase recovery to learn representations that support both efficient downstream generation and high-quality reconstruction. Through extensive ablations, we show that AudioCodex outperforms the state-of-the-art in terms of generation-reconstruction trade-off, whilst remaining strictly causal in both encoding and decoding. This work paves the way towards truly real-time interactive and streaming generative audio applications.

Discriminator-free adversarial domain adaptation for robust speech classification2611.12119v1其他
展开摘要

The performance of speech classification systems such as speech emotion recognition or pathological speech detection can degrade substantially when there is a domain shift between training and test data. This shift can arise due to e.g., differences in recording setup, acoustic environment, speaker, language, or speaking style. Adversarial domain adaptation addresses this problem by encouraging representations that are less sensitive to domain-specific differences. However, many adversarial approaches rely on training a separate domain discriminator, which can be unstable and difficult to train. In this paper, we propose to use a discriminator-free adversarial learning network (DALN) for unsupervised domain adaptation in speech classification tasks. DALN reuses the task classifier as an implicit discriminator and aligns domains through the nuclear-norm Wasserstein discrepancy on classifier outputs. We extensively evaluate the performance of DALN on intra-lingual, cross-lingual, and noisy adaptations for speech emotion recognition and pathological speech detection. Extensive experimental results show that DALN generally achieves the best performance across the considered tasks and domain shifts, outperforming discriminator-based domain adaptation approaches.

展开摘要

Talking-head generation models, which synthesize realistic facial animations from audio, are increasingly vulnerable to misuse in multimodal deepfake scenarios. Protecting such systems remains challenging, as talking-head models fundamentally rely on precise audio-visual alignment, while effective audio protection methods remain largely underexplored. In this work, we propose MUTE, an audio protection framework that uncouples the underlying audio-visual alignment through a multi-level strategy. Specifically, MUTE combines (1) Alignment-level Disruption, which directly perturbs cross-modal attention to disrupt structural alignment, and (2) Representation-level Degradation, which perturbs temporal audio embeddings to degrade their representations. An optional speaker-level objective further mitigates potential bypass via TTS-based resynthesis. Extensive experiments demonstrate that MUTE consistently degrades lip synchronization across both white-box and black-box talking-head models while preserving perceptual audio quality. The proposed method remains effective under real-world transformations and can be combined with image-based approaches to provide stronger multimodal protection. The source code and project page are available at https://github.com/jindl465/MUTE.

Occupancy-based controls regulate lighting and HVAC according to whether a space is in use. Passive infrared (PIR) sensors, the most common choice, respond to motion and can report an occupied room as vacant when its occupants remain still. Sound offers an alternative cue, since seated occupants type, turn pages and cough, but microphones raise privacy concerns. We therefore study a two-stage design: an on-device encoder maps each second of audio to a short embedding, and a server-side temporal model turns the stream of embeddings into an occupancy state. To our knowledge, no publicly available dataset pairs office audio with occupancy labels, so we evaluate the two stages separately on public data. The encoder is trained on synthetic scenes that mix empty-room recordings, room impulse responses, office sounds and read speech, and tested on unseen rooms, recordings and speakers for whether a second contains a sound made by a person. Noise-floor normalization with adversarial training, an 8-dimensional embedding, or sending only class probabilities lowers this to 38 to 55% without hurting detection. On HPDmobile, which pairs home occupancy labels with an amplitude envelope of the audio, a causal temporal model pretrained with a hierarchical contrastive loss matches hand-crafted features (AUC 0.75) and does better when only one labelled day per home is available. These results make the two-stage design a practical basis for privacy-aware acoustic occupancy sensing, with speaker identity as the one leak still to close.

Audio-text models such as Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining (CLAP) typically pool frame and token sequences uniformly into global embeddings. This work proposes WaveGen, a fully end-to-end diffusion-based text-to-waveform framework based on Spectral Forcing, which jointly learns an internal spectral trajectory and the target waveform trajectory within a single model, enabling direct end-to-end text-to-waveform generation.
