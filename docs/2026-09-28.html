TinyAudio: Compact and Efficient Text-to-Audio Generation for Low-Resource Deployment

作者：Junxi Liu, Xiquan Li, Wenhao Guan, Yifan Duan, Zhikang Niu, Yanru Huo, Ziyang Ma, Xie Chen

音频生成评分 19audioaudio generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出TinyAudio，一个总参数量87M的紧凑流匹配文本到音频生成模型，包含TA-DiT、TA-CLAP和TA-VAE，并引入TinyAudio-MF以在四核CPU配额下实现实时生成。

解决了什么问题

代表性文本到音频生成系统通常参数量约十亿，限制在资源受限设备上部署。

核心方法

TinyAudio核心为35M单流流匹配Transformer TA-DiT，配合32M音频对齐文本编码器TA-CLAP和20M解码器TA-VAE，总参数量87M，峰值GPU内存0.48GB；TinyAudio-MF采用MeanFlow加速。

主要创新点

通过紧凑架构将总参数量降低超过90%，并引入MeanFlow加速实现四核CPU实时生成，在低资源约束下取得竞争生成质量。

实验结论

在AudioCaps和TTA-Bench上达到竞争生成质量；TinyAudio-MF在四核CPU配额下实现实时生成，展示质量与占用之间的实际权衡。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

关注低资源音频生成、边缘计算部署和高效生成模型的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及代表性系统参数量约十亿且部署受限，但未列出具体工作。

对研究的启发

可从紧凑流匹配架构、音频对齐文本编码器和MeanFlow加速中获得低资源生成模型的启发。

是否值得精读

SAID: Semantic Acoustic Imaging Detector for Sound Event Localization and Detection

作者：Runbang Wang, Zining Liang, Yin Cao, Qiuqiang Kong

其他评分 15speechaudiomusicacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出SAID，一种语义声学成像检测器，为每个活动声源预测带标签的声学图（区域、能量和类别），并在DCASE2026 Task 3 Track A上排名第一。

解决了什么问题

现有模型可识别声音类别并估计每个声源的方向，但方向无法描述声源区域和能量，且需区分邻近方向声源并处理时变的活跃声源数量和区域。

核心方法

SAID首先通过无类别标签的方向声能估计预训练音频编码器Audio2Sph，然后联合训练预测每个活动声源的区域、能量和类别；配套流程生成模拟录音用于预训练并支持真实录音微调。

主要创新点

提出语义声学成像任务，为每个声源生成单独带标签的声学图；利用声能估计预训练编码器；构建模拟到真实的训练流程。

实验结论

在DCASE2026 Task 3 Track A官方评估集上，提交系统以0.1080 Macro mAP和0.3962 Macro Pearson r排名第一。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

声音事件定位与检测、声学成像、机器人感知和增强现实方向的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及现有模型能识别声类并估计方向，但未列出具体工作。

对研究的启发

将声源定位从方向估计扩展为带能量和类别的声学图，并采用能量估计预训练，可为相关任务提供新思路。

是否值得精读

对声音事件定位与检测及声学成像任务有参考价值，特别是在DCASE任务表现上。

Learning to Stop without Learning to Stop: Self-Supervised Confidence Training Improves Reasoning Efficiency

作者：Parsa Hosseini, Akasha Tigalappanavara, Sumit Nawathe, Chenrui Fan, Sourya Basu, Genta Indra Winata, Anirban Das, Soheil Feizi, Nima Chitsazan

自监督学习评分 5self-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一种自监督置信度微调方法，仅使用600个训练问题，在不显式优化长度的情况下使推理模型生成令牌减少高达25%。

解决了什么问题

推理模型通常生成很长的推理轨迹，导致推理计算昂贵；现有方法依赖推理时早停或显式鼓励短推理（如带长度惩罚的强化学习）。

核心方法

自监督程序微调推理模型，在自身推理轨迹的中间点预测对答案的置信度；置信度仅作为训练目标，损失不含长度、效率或停止目标；推理时使用标准生成过程，无置信度引出或早停机制。

主要创新点

展示效率提升可从元认知置信度信号中涌现，不需要直接优化长度；仅用600个训练问题；跨多个模型和基准验证。

实验结论

在Gemma、Qwen、Nemotron和GPT-OSS模型上，数学、科学和编码推理基准中，生成令牌减少高达25%（匹配精度），效率提升与显式优化短推理的方法相当；分析显示大部分保留高层推理组成。

适合谁读

推理模型效率优化、自监督学习和推理过程研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及推理时早停机制和强化学习长度惩罚等现有方法，未列出具体工作。

对研究的启发

自监督置信度微调可作为提高推理效率的新路径，提示元认知信号可能间接带来效率增益。

RePlay: Retrieval-Based Voice Playback for Multi-Turn spoken dialogue

作者：Sathvik Udupa, Naveen Kumar, Ryan Folmsbee

语音识别评分 5speechvoice查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出RePlay，一种基于检索的语音播放系统，通过中间隐藏状态检索预录制台词，实现中位延迟383 ms的多轮口语对话。

解决了什么问题

许多语音交互应用需要精确控制响应内容和传递方式；现有全双工模型低延迟但无法保证精确内容或复现特定录制表现，级联系统可限制到预定义响应但增加延迟。

核心方法

RePlay基于PersonaPlex，通过探测识别即将到来的响应可恢复的层和帧，使用该隐藏状态作为检索查询；仅保留到该点的层，用轻量级轮次和检索头替代文本和语音生成，从预录制台词中检索播放。

主要创新点

利用隐藏状态检索预录制台词，仅保留部分层以降低生成复杂度；在低延迟下实现多轮检索式对话。

实验结论

模拟多轮访谈中，RePlay中位延迟383 ms，比对话质量相当的ASR-LLM级联低3到7倍，但精确台词准确率较低；用户研究中，63%评分偏好RePlay，对比12%偏好带小LLM的快速级联（p=0.008）；相比带强LLM的较慢级联偏好非显著（46% vs 21%）。

可能局限

精确台词准确率较低（摘要明确提及）。

适合谁读

语音交互、低延迟对话系统和预录制响应应用的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及全双工模型和级联系统，并以PersonaPlex为基础，未列出其他具体工作。

对研究的启发

使用中间隐藏状态作为检索查询，结合轻量级头，可在低延迟和精确内容控制之间取得折中。

是否值得精读

对需要低延迟且可接受预录制响应语音对话场景的研究者具有参考价值。

作者：Christelle Clervilsson, Yanzhu Guo

一句话总结

解决了什么问题

大语言模型在高资源与低资源语言之间存在显著性能差异，而且往往未能捕捉代表性不足社区的文化规范与价值观；海地克里奥尔语数字资源严重不足。

主要创新点

实验结论

结果揭示海地克里奥尔语与较高资源法语之间存在明显差距，海地表现更不均衡且更受法语语言干扰；故事生成中反复出现通过艰难与韧性刻画海地人物，表明即使正面刻画也可能编码刻板叙事。

TinyAudio: Compact and Efficient Text-to-Audio Generation for Low-Resource Deployment2609.31525v1音频生成
展开摘要

Text-to-audio (TTA) generation has advanced rapidly in generation quality and instruction following. However, representative systems often require around a billion parameters, limiting deployment on resource-constrained devices. This paper introduces TinyAudio, a compact flow-matching-based TTA model for low-resource deployment. At its core, TinyAudio uses TA-DiT, a 35M single-stream flow-matching Transformer. TinyAudio also includes TA-CLAP, a 32M audio-aligned text encoder, and TA-VAE, whose 20M decoder reconstructs 44.1 kHz audio from compressed latents. TinyAudio has only 87M parameters in total, over 90% fewer than representative billion-parameter pipelines, and uses 0.48 GB peak GPU memory. TinyAudio achieves competitive generation quality on AudioCaps and TTA-Bench. We further introduce TinyAudio-MF, a MeanFlow-accelerated model that enables real-time generation with a four-core CPU quota. Our results demonstrate a practical quality-footprint trade-off for low-resource TTA deployment.

SAID: Semantic Acoustic Imaging Detector for Sound Event Localization and Detection2609.31492v1其他
展开摘要

In daily life, people hear speech, footsteps, and music around them. We can often recognize these sounds and judge where they come from. Each sound source can be shown on a separate acoustic map, a rectangular image covering $360^{\circ}$ horizontally and $180^{\circ}$ vertically. The map shows the directions occupied by the source as a region and the sound energy within that region. A class label identifies the sound. Predicting these labeled acoustic maps from audio is called semantic acoustic imaging. Such maps could help robots perceive their surroundings and allow augmented reality displays to show sound regions and classes over the real world. Existing models can recognize sound classes and estimate a direction for each source. However, a direction alone does not describe the source region or its energy. Acoustic imaging must also distinguish sound sources in nearby directions, while the number of active sources and the regions they occupy can change over time. We therefore propose the Semantic Acoustic Imaging Detector (SAID), which predicts a separate labeled acoustic map for each active source from audio. First, we pretrain Audio2Sph, SAID's audio encoder, through sound energy estimation across directions without class labels. Then, we train the complete SAID model to predict source regions, energy, and classes together. We also develop a pipeline that generates simulated recordings for pretraining and supports fine-tuning on real recordings. On the official DCASE2026 Task 3 Track A evaluation set, our submitted system ranks first with 0.1080 macro-averaged mean average precision (Macro mAP) and 0.3962 Macro Pearson $r$. Demos and code are provided at https://github.com/IN03X/SAID.

Learning to Stop without Learning to Stop: Self-Supervised Confidence Training Improves Reasoning Efficiency2609.31619v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Reasoning models often generate very long reasoning traces, making inference computationally expensive. Existing approaches typically improve efficiency either through inference-time early-stopping mechanisms or by explicitly encouraging shorter reasoning during training, for example through reinforcement learning with length penalties. We show that substantial efficiency gains can instead emerge from a different kind of supervision: \textit{confidence}. Using a self-supervised procedure, we fine-tune reasoning models to predict their confidence in the answer at intermediate points along their own reasoning trajectories using only 600 training problems. Confidence is used only as a training target: the loss contains no objective for reasoning length, efficiency, or stopping. At inference, the fine-tuned models use the standard generation procedure, with no confidence elicitation or early-stopping mechanism. Despite this, self-supervised confidence fine-tuning makes reasoning more efficient, reducing generated tokens by up to 25\% at matched accuracy across Gemma, Qwen, Nemotron, and GPT-OSS models on mathematical, scientific, and coding reasoning benchmarks, with efficiency gains comparable to methods that explicitly optimize for shorter reasoning. Analysis of reasoning episodes further shows that confidence supervision largely preserves the base models' high-level reasoning composition rather than selectively suppressing particular behaviors. Our results suggest that efficient reasoning may emerge as a downstream consequence of learning metacognitive signals, without being directly optimized.

RePlay: Retrieval-Based Voice Playback for Multi-Turn spoken dialogue2609.31588v1语音识别
展开摘要

Many voice interaction applications require exact control over both the content and delivery of responses, typically using pre-recorded lines. Recent full-duplex models respond with low latency but cannot guarantee exact content or reproduce a specific recorded performance, while cascaded systems can be constrained to predefined responses at the cost of additional latency. We propose RePlay, a spoken dialogue system adapted from PersonaPlex that handles multi-turn conversations by retrieving and playing pre-recorded lines. Using probing, we identify the layer and frame at which the upcoming response becomes recoverable, and use this hidden state as the retrieval query. RePlay retains only the layers up to that point and replaces text and speech generation with lightweight turn-taking and retrieval heads. In simulated multi-turn interviews, RePlay reaches a median latency of 383 ms, 3 to 7 times lower than ASR-LLM cascades of comparable dialogue quality, at the cost of lower exact-line accuracy. In a user study, participants preferred RePlay in 63% of ratings versus 12% for a fast cascade with a small LLM (p = 0.008), and showed a non-significant preference (46% vs. 21%) over a slower cascade with a stronger LLM.

Evaluating Cultural Awareness of LLMs for Haitian Creole2609.31506v1其他
展开摘要

Large language models (LLMs) exhibit substantial performance disparities between high- and low-resource languages. Beyond lower task performance, they often fail to capture the cultural norms and values of underrepresented communities. In this work, we present the first systematic evaluation of cultural awareness in LLMs for Haitian Creole, a language spoken by millions but severely underrepresented in digital resources. We assess cultural awareness along four complementary dimensions---specificity, bias, diversity, and variation---using a benchmark of culturally salient prompts curated by native speakers in a text infilling setting. Our results reveal a clear gap between cultural awareness in Haitian Creole and higher-resource French, with Haitian performance being more uneven across domains and more affected by French linguistic interference. Story generation further reveals recurring portrayals of Haitian characters through hardship and resilience, showing that even positive characterizations can encode stereotypical narratives. Our code, benchmark, and evaluation framework are publicly available.
