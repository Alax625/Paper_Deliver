The Content Leash: Mitigating Degenerate Solutions in Invertible Speaker Anonymization

作者：Nirjon Mobarak Talukder, Rafid Ahmed, Mohammad Ishrak Abedin, Abu Raihan Mostofa Kamal

说话人评分 27speechvoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出内容保持训练约束和验证器泛化策略，用于缓解可逆说话人匿名化中的退化解，在未见说话者和验证器上提升匿名化效果。

解决了什么问题

可逆语音匿名化中，现有方法可能通过降低语音质量实现表面匿名化，且效果依赖于训练时使用的说话人验证表示，导致对未见验证器鲁棒性不足。

核心方法

引入内容保持训练约束防止通过语音退化实现匿名化，并采用验证器泛化策略，在训练中整合更强的说话人验证表示。

主要创新点

内容保持约束与验证器泛化策略结合，提升隐私-效用权衡，在保持可懂语音和可恢复性的同时增强匿名化鲁棒性。

实验结论

在38个未见说话者上，攻击者等错误率从2.8%提高至26.3%，在保留验证器上为27.9%，关键词恢复时词错误率11.8%，说话人相似度94.9%。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

语音隐私、说话人匿名化、生物特征保护相关研究者。

和已有工作的关系

可逆语音匿名化、说话人验证、隐私保护语音处理。

对研究的启发

通过训练约束和验证器泛化提升匿名化鲁棒性，可为其他隐私保护任务提供思路。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因提出具体策略并给出量化结果，对可逆匿名化的隐私-效用权衡有明确改进。

TAC2: Timestamped Audio Captioning as composable functions

作者：Swapnil Bhosale, Prem Seetharaman, Ke Chen, Haonan Chen, Justin Salamon, Zeyu Jin

音频生成评分 19speechaudioacousticspeakeraudio generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出TAC2模型，将时间戳音频字幕视为可组合函数，从自然音频中构建细粒度、带说话人属性的声学时间线。

解决了什么问题

现有大型音频语言模型倾向于生成自由形式描述，混淆声学感知与高级推理，缺乏精确时间定位。

核心方法

通过时间注册IoU匹配融合弱教师预测，以指令-输入-输出（IIO）完成格式建模多粒度时间戳事件、说话人轮次和细粒度语音属性，并支持函数组合调用；扩展TAC2-S使用参数高效专家提炼副语言能力。

主要创新点

将时间戳音频字幕重新定义为可组合函数，实现单次调用解码完整描述及组合调用条件生成；TAC2-S利用专家蒸馏统一副语言属性。

实验结论

在两种互补设置中验证：与冻结文本LLMs配合产生强口语理解；作为文本到音频生成的配对监督，支持语义和时间基础重构及零样本控制事件时间、说话人属性和词级强调。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

音频-语言模型、音频字幕、时序定位、文本到音频生成研究者。

和已有工作的关系

大型音频语言模型、时间戳音频字幕、文本到音频生成、参数高效微调。

对研究的启发

将复杂任务分解为可组合函数调用，可提高模型灵活性和可控性，为多模态序列建模提供新视角。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因其提出新的表征框架并在两个应用中验证，但缺少量化指标，可关注后续实现细节。

From Voiceprints to User Entities: Online Speaker-Memory Construction for Multi-User Spoken Agents

作者：Yucong Zhang, Cancan Li, Bo Xiao, Ke Zeng, Ming Li

其他评分 18voiceacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出EvoSpeakerMem训练免费层，用于多用户口语代理的在线说话人记忆构建，将声纹视为身份观测并维持持久用户实体。

解决了什么问题

个性化口语代理需要在不确定身份下构建用户记忆，现有基准假设正确的说话人归属，且声纹是身份观测而非身份本身。

核心方法

使用因果说话人路由、选择性声学状态更新和身份分区记忆读写，通过可替换文本记忆后端实现训练免费的在线说话人记忆构建；并构建LoCoMo-ID基准。

主要创新点

将声纹重新定义为身份观测，提出在线说话人记忆构建框架，无需训练即可集成现有系统，引入新的基准测试隐藏身份和混淆。

实验结论

在纵向SMIIP-TV上，冻结近注册阈值下的真实探针拒绝率在约一年内从29.8%升至55.6%；在LoCoMo-ID说话人敏感查询上，门控更新在干净和偏移条件下分别比固定表示提高3.86和11.62个百分点，无错误用户授权，优势在四说话人测试中保持；但作者指出QA改善可与不正确跨用户链接共存，暴露碎片化-污染权衡。

可能局限

摘要指出存在碎片化-污染权衡，QA改善可能伴随不正确跨用户链接。

适合谁读

说话人识别、个性化对话系统、记忆增强代理研究者。

和已有工作的关系

说话人验证、用户记忆管理、语音代理个性化。

对研究的启发

将身份建模为基于观测的动态实体，而非静态特征，为多用户交互系统设计提供新思路。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因提出训练免费方法并构建新基准，同时指出关键权衡，对实际部署有参考价值。

AudioRSI: Recursively Self-Improving Audio Reasoners

作者：Jiajun Fan, Roger Ren, Jingyuan Li, Rahul Pandey, Prashanth Gurunath Shivakumar, Ankur Gandhe, Yile Gu, Ge Liu, Ivan Bulyko

其他评分 17audioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出AudioRSI框架，使音频大语言模型通过递归自我改进，无需外部奖励即可提升音频推理能力。

解决了什么问题

音频大语言模型的强化学习奖励通常来自外部固定来源，无法随模型改进而更新，限制持续进步。

核心方法

单一模型扮演演员和评论家双重角色：演员生成推理路径，评论家进行成对比较产生过程级奖励，且评论家定期由当前策略更新，形成递归循环。

主要创新点

将奖励来源内部化并递归更新，评论家与演员共享同一音频输入，可拒绝引用音频中不存在声学证据的推理，从而减少幻觉。

实验结论

在四个与AVQA训练分布无关的分布外基准上，AudioRSI优于强基线，包括在MMAU test-mini上优于Audio Flamingo 3和Gemini 2.5 Pro，在MMSU上排名第一，在MMAU-Pro的Multi-Audio分割上明显领先，并在无外部奖励模型的方法中于7B开源层级的MMAR上领先；还发展出逻辑推理、感知觉察分析和减少幻觉等结构化推理行为。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

音频大语言模型、强化学习、推理能力、多模态AI研究者。

和已有工作的关系

音频LLM、基于外部奖励的RLHF、自我改进、过程奖励模型。

对研究的启发

递归自我改进机制表明模型自身判断可作为不断进化的训练信号，为减少对外部奖励依赖提供新方向。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因其展示自我改进闭环在多个分布外基准上持续提升，并产生可解释行为变化。

S3-S2ST: Semantically Supervised Streaming Speech-to-Speech Translation

作者：Yuxiang Zhao, Zhiyun Fan, Yu Pu, Yanjie An, Yushen Chen, Wenrui Liang, Baiji Liu, Yunchong Xiao, Zhaoci Liu, Keqi Deng, Kai Yu, Zhifu Gao, Xiangang Li, Xie Chen

语音翻译评分 15speechaudiospeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出S3-S2ST模型，利用语义对齐的流式监督学习何时提交部分输入的翻译，实现流式语音到语音翻译。

解决了什么问题

流式语音到语音翻译需要及时决定何时部分输入足以进行准确翻译，现有方法缺乏语义对齐的流式监督。

核心方法

将话语级语音对转换为增量训练示例，训练学习策略决定何时提交翻译，其可调阈值在640ms输入网格上控制质量-延迟权衡。

主要创新点

通过语义对齐的流式监督和可调阈值，在固定输入网格上实现质量和延迟的显式权衡，并适应缓冲语音改善质量。

实验结论

在FLEURS上提供有竞争力的英汉翻译，在内部9对测试集上流式系统中翻译质量领先；在RealSI上，将阈值适应缓冲语音相比低固定阈值进一步提高质量，首次音频延迟几乎不变。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

语音翻译、流式处理、低延迟语音系统研究者。

和已有工作的关系

流式语音翻译、语音到语音翻译、增量学习、质量-延迟权衡。

对研究的启发

利用语义对齐的增量监督训练提交策略，为流式系统中的决策时机学习提供参考。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因针对流式翻译的关键难题提出具体训练策略，并计划开源模型和代码。

The Content Leash: Mitigating Degenerate Solutions in Invertible Speaker Anonymization2610.36768v1说话人
展开摘要

Voice anonymization aims to protect a speaker's identity while preserving the linguistic content of speech, enabling voice data to be shared and processed with reduced privacy risk. This work focuses on reversible voice anonymization, where the original voice can be recovered by an authorized user rather than being permanently discarded. However, existing approaches can achieve apparent speaker anonymization by degrading the speech itself, while their effectiveness may also depend on the speaker-verification representation used during training. This creates a need for methods that protect speaker identity without sacrificing speech intelligibility and that remain effective against unseen speaker verifiers. We introduce content preserving training constraints to improve the privacy-utility trade-off by preventing anonymization from being achieved primarily through speech degradation. We further introduce a verifier-generalization strategy that incorporates a stronger speaker-verification representation during training, improving anonymization robustness beyond the verifier used in the original training setup. The resulting approach maintains intelligible speech, strengthens anonymization, and preserves reliable voice restoration, with evaluation on unseen speakers and held-out speaker verification models. On 38 unseen speakers, the approach raises the attacker's equal error rate from a measured floor of 2.8% to 26.3%, and to 27.9% against a held-out verifier, with 11.8% word error rate and 94.9% speaker similarity on key-based restoration. These improvements advance reversible voice anonymization toward practical privacy-preserving speech applications, where anonymized speech can be safely processed or shared while the original voice remains recoverable under authorized conditions.

TAC2: Timestamped Audio Captioning as composable functions2610.36568v1音频生成
展开摘要

Real-world audio applications require precise, queryable representations grounded in physical time. Yet modern Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) increasingly favor conversational generation, producing free-form descriptions that conflate acoustic perception with higher-level reasoning and lack precise temporal grounding. We introduce TAC2, a foundation model that casts timestamped audio captioning as composable functions to construct granular, speaker-attributed acoustic timelines directly from in-the-wild audio. To avoid prohibitively expensive manual annotation, we programmatically fuse complementary predictions from specialized weak teachers through time-registered IoU matching. We model the resulting multi-granularity schema of timestamped events, speaker turns, and fine-grained speech attributes using an Instruction-Input-Output (IIO) completion format, in which each task is a function of the audio, an instruction, and an optional input. A single call decodes the full description autonomously, while composing calls lets earlier outputs or external priors, such as a reference transcript, condition later ones. We further extend TAC2 with expressive speech attributes to obtain TAC2-S, using parameter-efficient specialists to elicit latent paralinguistic capabilities from the pre-trained backbone and distill them into a unified model. We validate the resulting representation in two complementary settings. Paired with frozen text-only LLMs, it yields strong spoken audio understanding across diverse reasoning backends, demonstrating the utility of decoupling fine-grained acoustic perception from downstream reasoning. Used as paired supervision for text-to-audio generation, it supports semantically and temporally grounded reconstruction, together with explicit zero-shot control over event timing, speaker attributes, and word-level emphasis.

From Voiceprints to User Entities: Online Speaker-Memory Construction for Multi-User Spoken Agents2610.36713v1其他
展开摘要

Personalized spoken agents must identify speakers before accessing user-specific memory, yet memory benchmarks typically assume correctly attributed histories. We argue that voiceprints are observations of identity, not identities themselves, and formulate online speaker-memory construction to maintain persistent user entities as acoustic evidence evolves. We introduce EvoSpeakerMem, a training-free layer for causal speaker routing, selective acoustic-state updates, and identity-partitioned memory writes and retrieval through a replaceable text-memory backend. We also construct LoCoMo-ID, a spoken-memory benchmark with hidden speaker identities, speaker-sensitive queries, simulated recording-condition shifts, and four-speaker identity-confusion tests. On longitudinal SMIIP-TV, genuine-probe rejection under a frozen near-enrollment threshold rises from 29.8% to 55.6% over approximately one year. On LoCoMo-ID speaker-sensitive queries, gated updating improves identity-gated QA over fixed representations by 3.86 and 11.62 percentage points under clean and shifted conditions, respectively, with no wrong-user authorizations. The advantage persists in the four-speaker test. Yet reducing fragmentation and improving QA can coexist with incorrect cross-user links, revealing a fragmentation--contamination trade-off that QA alone cannot capture.

AudioRSI: Recursively Self-Improving Audio Reasoners2610.36578v1其他
展开摘要

Reinforcement learning has made Audio Large Language Models (Audio LLMs) better reasoners, but the reward that drives it still comes from outside the model: a final-answer check, a hand-crafted process score, or an external LLM judge that stays fixed while the policy improves. We show that an Audio LLM can supply this reward itself, and can do so recursively: each improved model becomes the critic that trains its successor, so the training signal improves along with the model. We introduce AudioRSI (Recursively Self-Improving Audio Reasoners), a reinforcement learning framework in which a single model plays two roles: as an actor it generates reasoning paths, and as a critic it compares them pairwise, turning its own judgments into a process-level reward. The critic is periodically replaced by the current policy, which closes the recursive loop. Because the critic hears the same recording as the actor, it can reject reasoning that cites acoustic evidence the audio does not contain, rather than reward reasoning that merely sounds plausible. The recursion is what sustains progress: under a frozen external judge, out-of-distribution accuracy plateaus during training, while a critic refreshed from the improving policy keeps it rising. Across four out-of-distribution benchmarks, all disjoint from the AVQA training distribution, AudioRSI outperforms strong baselines including Audio Flamingo 3 and Gemini 2.5 Pro on MMAU test-mini, ranks first on MMSU, achieves a clear lead on the challenging Multi-Audio split of MMAU-Pro, and leads the 7B open-source tier on MMAR among methods without external reward models. With no reward specifying how to reason, AudioRSI also develops structured reasoning behaviors (logical reasoning, perception-aware analysis, and reduced hallucination), corroborated by an external LLM-as-Judge evaluation and a keyword-based behavior analysis.

S3-S2ST: Semantically Supervised Streaming Speech-to-Speech Translation2610.36777v1语音翻译
展开摘要

Streaming speech-to-speech translation requires timely decisions about when partial input is sufficient for accurate translation. We present S3-S2ST, a model that learns to translate and speak from semantically aligned streaming supervision. By converting utterance-level speech pairs into incremental training examples, we teach a learned policy when to commit translations. Its adjustable threshold controls the quality--latency tradeoff on a fixed 640\,ms input grid. S3-S2ST delivers competitive English--Chinese translation on FLEURS and leads the evaluated streaming systems in translation quality on the internal 9-pair test set. On RealSI, adapting the threshold to buffered speech further improves quality over a low fixed threshold with little change in first-audio latency. We plan to release the model and code.

Tap2Listen: Instant Target Switching for Streaming Audio-Visual Speaker Extraction2610.36643v1其他
展开摘要

Streaming audio-visual target speaker extraction (AV-TSE) uses a target speaker's face to extract the speaker's speech from an input audio mixture in real time. However, existing systems fix the target speaker for an entire clip, while conversation demands frequent and immediate target switches. To address this problem, we propose Tap2Listen, a streaming AV-TSE model designed for instant response to target switches. The model separates computation by modality: the target-agnostic audio encoder processes only the mixture, while the target-specific visual cues of the selected face condition only the decoder. A switch requires only a 0.12ms re-projection and takes effect immediately. We also design component-wise losses: a prediction loss for the encoder and a supervised extraction loss for the decoder. For evaluation, we measure how quickly output quality recovers after a switch. On the standard LRS3-2mix test set, Tap2Listen improves SI-SNRi from 8.85 to 9.68 dB at a comparable parameter count. After a switch to a known face, the model produces full-quality output from the first frame, whereas the baseline needs about 1 second to recover. Finally, we show that the proposed streaming AV-TSE model runs in real time on a single CPU core.

Perceptual Differences and Preferences in Speech Enhancement2610.36892v1语音增强
展开摘要

Speech enhancement (SE) systems are commonly evaluated with objective metrics, with and without references, and with categorical subjective listening tests, all measuring speech quality and intelligibility. So far, it is unclear from such evaluations whether the processed speech is perceptually different or is preferred by everyday listeners. We investigate discrimination and preference as complementary dimensions of SE evaluation through crowdsourced triangle and pairwise preference tests across four SE systems, with comparisons to clean references. Results show that both listeners' ability to distinguish and whether they exhibit systematic preference vary across systems and substantially across acoustic conditions. Pairwise evaluations of objective metrics showed variable correlation with both discriminability and preference, motivating further studies into these novel aspects in speech enhancement evaluation.

StreamSE: Neural Audio Codec-based Streaming Speech Enhancement for On-device Inference2610.37024v1语音增强
