Evaluation of Music Version Retrieval Methods for Setlist Identification in Live Concerts

作者：Andres Ferraro, Albin Correya

其他评分 10audiomusic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该文在ASID基准上评估音乐版本检索方法，发现NAVE和CLEWS显著优于Re-MOVE，且NAVE以更低维度实现更好检索性能。

解决了什么问题

自动识别现场音乐会录音中的歌曲面临编曲差异、背景噪声和大规模连续录音高效处理的挑战。

核心方法

在ASID基准上比较Re-MOVE、CLEWS和NAVE（内部神经音频版本嵌入模型），并评估音乐/非音乐门控预处理。

主要创新点

使用段级音频嵌入进行可扩展setlist识别；展示256维NAVE嵌入比1024维CLEWS存储减少四倍且性能更优；引入音乐/非音乐门控改善检测和识别边界。

实验结论

NAVE和CLEWS大幅优于Re-MOVE；NAVE以256维嵌入相比1024维CLEWS减少四倍存储且检索性能更好；音乐/非音乐门控带来适度检测和时间覆盖改进，定性分析显示可改善识别边界。

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音乐信息检索、音频处理、现场音乐会分析领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

对比了既有系统Re-MOVE和CLEWS，以及内部模型NAVE。

对研究的启发

紧凑音频嵌入可在保持精度的同时降低存储和服务成本，为大规模音频检索任务提供参考。

TOP 2

Convolutional VocalTrax: Replacing Backpropagation Through Time with Convolution in Differentiable Articulatory Synthesis

作者：Yongyi Zang, Debasish Ray Mohapatra, Randal Leistikow, Perry R. Cook

其他评分 3speechaudio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Convolutional VocalTrax，用因果卷积替代可微发音合成中的时间反向传播，在A100 GPU上实现47-63倍加速并提高重建语音识别率。

解决了什么问题

VocalTrax通过可微Pink Trombone合成器梯度下降恢复发音控制，但声带波导每样本更新两次导致长递归反向传播在GPU上缓慢。

核心方法

假设声道在5.8ms块内平稳且损耗脉冲响应可在93ms后截断，计算相同波导的精确频率响应并应用为因果卷积，梯度通过FFT和沿声道的短递归传播。

主要创新点

将波导的递归运算替换为因果卷积，利用FFT和短递归，大幅提高GPU计算效率。

实验结论

在600条AudioMNIST录音上，声道前向和反向传播在A100 GPU快47-63倍；仅用真实语音训练的分类器识别重建语音比例从VocalTrax的10.0%提升到14.5%；log-mel距离比目标高11%；用本模型拟合的参数在原始VocalTrax渲染下保持增益（13.8%）。

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音合成、可微发音建模、深度学习效率优化领域的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

基于VocalTrax和Pink Trombone可微合成器。

对研究的启发

将长时递归替换为频域卷积可显著加速计算，为可微物理模型训练提供新思路。

是否值得精读

是，展示了明确的加速比和识别率提升，但log-mel距离略高需进一步分析。

Evaluation of Music Version Retrieval Methods for Setlist Identification in Live Concerts2611.17070v1其他
展开摘要

Automatic setlist identification aims to identify the songs performed in live concert recordings by matching noisy and variable-quality audio against a database of reference tracks. This task presents challenges beyond conventional music version identification, including substantial arrangement differences, background noise, and the need to process continuous recordings efficiently at large scale. In this work, we evaluate modern music version retrieval methods for setlist identification using the ASID benchmark, comparing the established Re-MOVE system with CLEWS and NAVE, an internal neural audio version embedding model. Our results show that NAVE and CLEWS substantially outperform Re-MOVE. Notably, NAVE uses 256-dimensional embeddings, compared with 1024 dimensions for CLEWS, providing a four-fold reduction in embedding storage while achieving better retrieval performance. We further evaluate music/non-music gating and find modest improvements in detection and temporal coverage, while qualitative analysis suggests that such preprocessing can improve recognition boundaries beyond what the standard ASID metrics capture. Overall, the results demonstrate that segment-level audio embeddings provide a strong basis for scalable setlist identification and that compact representations can achieve strong accuracy under realistic live-concert conditions at much lower storage and serving cost.

Convolutional VocalTrax: Replacing Backpropagation Through Time with Convolution in Differentiable Articulatory Synthesis2611.17181v1其他
展开摘要

VocalTrax recovers interpretable articulatory controls from a recording by gradient descent through a differentiable Pink Trombone synthesizer. Its vocal tract is a Kelly-Lochbaum waveguide updated twice per audio sample, so every gradient step backpropagates through a long recurrence, which is slow on GPUs. We propose Convolutional VocalTrax, which computes the exact frequency response of the same waveguide and applies it as a causal convolution. We assume that the tract is stationary within 5.8 ms blocks and that its lossy impulse response can be truncated after 93 ms. Gradients then pass through FFTs and a short recursion along the tract instead of through time. On 600 AudioMNIST recordings, the tract forward and backward pass runs 47 to 63 times faster on an A100 GPU, and a classifier trained only on real speech recognizes our reconstructions more often than those of VocalTrax (14.5% vs. 10.0%), although the log-mel distance to the target is 11% higher. Parameters fitted with our model keep this gain when rendered by the original VocalTrax (13.8%).

Augmented Hybrid TV Services. Habits, Preferences, Opinions and Expectations of Spanish Consumers2611.17284v1其他
展开摘要

The convergence of hybrid broadcast and broadband delivery, companion devices, and immersive technologies enables new forms of interactive, personalized, and accessible TV services. This paper introduces the concept of Augmented Hybrid TV services and presents an exploratory user requirements and preliminary acceptance assessment for 5 representative use cases. They were selected through an exploratory stakeholder consultation and 3 co-design workshops with audiovisual professionals and participants from organizations supporting people with visual or hearing impairments. They combine conventional TV with complementary content delivered to connected devices, including AR/VR HMD. They were subsequently evaluated through a cross-sectional CAWI survey of 626 Internet and connected TV users in Spain. Each participant viewed standardized video demonstrations of 2 randomly assigned cases and assessed their perceived usefulness, anticipated benefits/limitations, and interest in them. The results show substantial interest across all five use cases, ranging from 64.7% to 76.9%. Adaptive and personalized augmented subtitles received the highest level of interest and were particularly valued for their accessibility, language-learning, and personalization benefits. Immersive services involving complementary AR content, virtual screens, 3D objects, and 360 video were also evaluated positively, although participants identified device cost, ergonomics, connectivity, usability, and synchronization as anticipated limitations. The findings provide user-centered evidence that can inform the prioritization, design, and subsequent prototype-based evaluation of standards-compliant Augmented Hybrid TV services. Because the study assessed video-based scenarios rather than functional systems, the results represent perceived usefulness and stated interest rather than observed adoption or technically validated QoE.
