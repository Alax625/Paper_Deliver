Piano2Pop: Expanding Piano Demos into Pop Productions Without Aligned Supervision

作者：Yun-Chen Cheng, Chih-Pin Tan, Tzu-Hung Huang, Yi-Hsuan Yang

音乐生成评分 5audiomusicmusic generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

论文提出 Piano2Pop 任务，通过伪监督方法将钢琴 MIDI 扩展为流行器乐制作，并在真实钢琴输入上取得最佳指标。

解决了什么问题

缺少对齐的钢琴-流行音乐监督数据，难以训练钢琴 MIDI 到流行编曲的扩展模型。

核心方法

基于预训练流匹配模型，以钢琴卷帘为条件，利用从流行录音生成的合成钢琴-器乐配对进行伪监督训练；对比无配对转录增强（FST、RDA）与配对监督钢琴翻奏生成器作为伪演示来源。

主要创新点

提出音乐扩展任务 Piano2Pop；引入伪监督方法无需对齐监督；比较包括 FST 与 RDA 增强在内的无配对转录方法，其中 FST 可缓解钢琴过重输出。

实验结论

在 476 对真实钢琴翻奏/流行音乐测试对上，配对监督方法在 Frechet Audio Distance、伴奏提示依从性和频谱形状上取得最佳结果；FST 在无配对训练中缓解钢琴过重输出；所有微调系统显著优于零样本基线。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明。

适合谁读

音乐生成、音频合成与多模态学习研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及基于音乐生成与复音音乐转录的进展，并对比无配对转录增强（FST、RDA）与配对监督钢琴翻奏生成器。

对研究的启发

利用合成配对数据进行伪监督，为无对齐监督的音乐扩展任务提供思路；任务特定微调显著优于零样本基线，提示专用模型价值。

是否值得精读

是：新任务定义与伪监督方案对无对齐音乐生成有参考意义，且包含真实数据评估与基线对比。

TOP 2

Can an Agent Debug a Web Corpus? Agentic Auditing of Slovak FineWeb2

作者：Marek Šuppa, Ivan Vykopal, Jaroslav Kopčan

其他评分 1speaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

论文使用工具型智能体作为语料调试器审计斯洛伐克语 FineWeb2，发现并验证多个缺陷，包括大规模机器生成内容农场。

解决了什么问题

多语言管道如 FineWeb2 对上千种语言使用同一配方，个别语言的失败模式未被预期和发现。

核心方法

使用 LLM 一次作为语料调试器而非分类器，通过只读、有预算的 API 探索语料，将每个发现编译成可执行测试，再由母语者验证。

主要创新点

提出智能体审计框架，产出可执行测试套件，以低成本发现具体缺陷并解释管道遗漏原因。

实验结论

三次运行各花费 $2.21-$2.84，发现七项缺陷；最大为占 5.32% 的机器生成内容农场（1,408,437 篇文档）；盲标签支持 7 项中的 6 项；编译测试套件无 GPU 在 0.7 秒扫描全语料；智能体还贡献精确流行率、经抽查的精度估计、遗漏原因说明和可重用测试；最佳静态采样基线验证更多选择器且成本更低。

语料库审计、多语言 NLP 与数据质量研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与多语言管道（如 FineWeb2）及静态采样基线进行对比。

对研究的启发

利用工具型智能体进行一次性审计并生成可复用测试，为大规模语料缺陷发现提供低成本、可解释方案。

Signed Wasserstein distance for room impulse response comparison

作者：Linda Fabiani, Sebastian J. Schlecht, Filip Elvander

其他评分 1audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

论文提出基于符号 Wasserstein 距离的房间脉冲响应相似度度量，以应对点式度量对时间偏移敏感的问题，并在房间参数扰动与优化中验证优势。

解决了什么问题

房间脉冲响应信号稀疏、快速变化且带符号，传统点式度量对小时移敏感且优化损失面不佳。

核心方法

提出基于最优传输的符号 Wasserstein 距离，通过比较信号底层分布的位移而非一一对应来度量 RIR 相似性。

主要创新点

将符号 Wasserstein 距离用于 RIR 比较，显式考虑脉冲响应结构；可用于可微 Image-Source 模型中的房间参数优化。

实验结论

通过房间参数扰动研究，与常用音频基线指标相比，所提距离在几何评估任务中表现出优势，并在可微 Image-Source 模型中对房间参数优化展现出有前景的性能。

对研究的启发

将最优传输与信号结构结合，用于非对齐信号相似度度量，可为其他稀疏或带符号信号比较提供思路。

Piano2Pop: Expanding Piano Demos into Pop Productions Without Aligned Supervision2610.31510v1音乐生成
展开摘要

We introduce Piano2Pop, a music-expansion task that transforms piano MIDI into instrumental pop productions. Enabled by recent advances in music generation and polyphonic music transcription, our pseudo-supervised approach conditions a pretrained flow-matching model on piano rolls without aligned piano-pop supervision. We train on synthetic piano-instrumental pairs derived from pop recordings, comparing pair-free transcription, including function-selective (FST) and register-density (RDA) augmentations, with a pair-supervised piano-cover generator as pseudo-demo sources. On 476 testing real-world piano-cover/pop pairs, the pair-supervised approach achieves the best Frechet Audio Distance, Accompaniment Prompt Adherence, and spectral shape on real-piano inputs, while FST mitigates piano-heavy outputs in pair-free training and remains a promising alternative. All fine-tuned systems significantly outperform the zero-shot baselines, which suggests a role for task-specific modeling.

Can an Agent Debug a Web Corpus? Agentic Auditing of Slovak FineWeb22610.31636v1其他
展开摘要

Multilingual pipelines such as FineWeb2 apply one recipe across a thousand languages, leaving the failure modes of individual languages unanticipated. We use an LLM once, as a corpus debugger rather than a classifier: a tool-using agent explores the corpus through a read-only, budgeted API and compiles each finding into an executable test, which a native speaker then validates. On the 26.5M-document Slovak portion of FineWeb2, three runs costing \$2.21-\$2.84 each found seven findings over four phenomena. The largest is a machine-generated content farm of 1,408,437 documents (5.32%) that passed the pipeline's filters, 86.6% of it passing even stricter thresholds. Blinded labels support 6 of the 7 findings (defect rates .53-1.00 against .08 in a random control) and do not support one. The compiled suite scans the full corpus in 0.7 s of wall-clock time without a GPU, against \$12k-\$90k per LLM classification pass. The best static sampling baseline validates more selectors than any agent run, at under half the cost. The agent contributes exact prevalence, a precision estimate checked against sampled documents, an account of why the pipeline missed each slice, and a reusable test. We release the framework, Slovak test suite, validation data and agent trajectories.

Signed Wasserstein distance for room impulse response comparison2610.31596v1其他
展开摘要

The nature of room impulse response signals is often adverse to a clear evaluation of similarity: they are sparse, rapidly varying, signed signals resulting from the complex interaction of room reflections and phase relationships. Therefore, conventional point-wise metrics may be sensitive to small temporal shifts and provide poor loss surfaces for optimization. In this paper, we propose an optimal transport-based distance for RIR similarity that explicitly accounts for the structure of impulse responses, inspired by the signed Wasserstein distance formulation. Optimal transport provides a natural framework for this problem, by comparing signals based on how their underlying distributions are displaced rather than requiring a one-to-one correspondence. We evaluate the proposed distance against commonly used audio baseline metrics through a series of room-parameter perturbation studies. The results demonstrate the advantage of using the signed Wasserstein metric for geometric evaluation tasks and show a promising performance for room parameter optimization within a differentiable Image-Source model.
