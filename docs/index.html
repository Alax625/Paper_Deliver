SKAD: Sketch-Guided Audio-Contrastive Decoding for Reasoning in Large Audio-Language Models

作者：Junyu Wang, Peiyuan Jiang, Siyuan Zhang, Jian Zong, Tianrui Wang, Zikang Huang, Meng Ge, Longbiao Wang, Jianwu Dang

其他评分 20audioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出SKAD，一种训练免费的推理时方法，通过构建声学证据草图并执行草图引导的音频对比解码，提升大音频语言模型推理的可靠性。

解决了什么问题

大型音频语言模型在生成推理步骤时可能从声学证据漂移到语言延续模式，直接使用音频掩码分支的对比解码因掩码分支缺乏足够音频上下文而不稳定。

核心方法

首先构建总结问题相关听觉信息的声学证据草图，然后在音频条件推理分支和仅草图分支之间进行对比解码，利用草图作为共享语义支架，使掩码分支信息更丰富，鼓励解码更依赖原始音频。

主要创新点

引入声学证据草图作为对比参考，解决直接对比解码中掩码分支不稳定的问题；训练免费，适用于推理时。

实验结论

在Qwen2.5-Omni-7B和Step-Audio-R1上，在MMAR、MMAU、MMSU三个基准上，SKAD一致提高平均准确率；消融实验显示无草图的直接对比解码收益有限或为负，证实构建可靠对比参考的重要性。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音频语言模型、多模态推理、对比解码研究者。

和已有工作的关系

对比解码、音频掩码分支、链式思维推理（摘要中提及）。

对研究的启发

通过构建共享语义支架可以改善对比解码，该思路可能推广到其他模态或任务。

是否值得精读

值得深读，方法训练免费且有效，消融实验支持核心设计。

TOP 2

Slots Are Not Speakers: Training-Free Speaker Linking for Frozen Streaming Diarizers

作者：Aditya Srinivas Menon, Kumarmanas Nethil, Kavya Manohar

其他评分 18speechaudiospeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一种因果、训练免费的说话人链接器，使冻结的流式说话人分离器能够跟踪超过其输出槽位数量的说话人，仅通过重标记减少说话人混淆。

解决了什么问题

流式端到端分离器输出固定数量的并发说话人槽位，超过槽位数时复用槽位，导致说话人混淆。

核心方法

使用宿主模型的槽位后验和音频，保留单槽活跃帧，在说话人变化处分割槽位，用现成说话人编码器嵌入片段，与已见说话人库匹配，仅重标记。

主要创新点

训练免费、因果、即插即用，不改变漏检和误报，所有增益来自减少说话人混淆；可迁移到不同槽位数和不同模型家族。

实验结论

在八个语料库1418个会话上，将4槽主机的DER从超容量时的36.8%降至22.6%，容量内几乎不变；在最多23说话人的法庭听证上cpWER从82.7%降至24.4%；相同常数可迁移至8槽后继和两个流式EEND主机。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

说话人分离、流式处理、语音处理研究者。

和已有工作的关系

流式端到端分离器（如Streaming Sortformer），说话人嵌入匹配。

对研究的启发

通过事后链接固定槽位可以扩展模型容量而无需重新训练，类似思想可应用于其他固定输出结构。

是否值得精读

值得深读，训练免费且效果显著，跨模型和语料验证充分。

识别大型音频语言模型中的跨模态概念神经元，验证音频和文本表征在共享子空间对齐，并通过因果干预证实这些神经元可操纵。

大型音频语言模型如何整合音频表征与文本表征尚不清楚，假设存在共享对齐子空间。

识别负责同时表示音频和文本的跨模态神经元，通过因果干预（引导和失活）验证其必要性和可操纵性。

发现跨模态神经元对齐程度因模型和属性类型而异；因果干预确认这些神经元不仅必要且可操纵。

实验结论

在三个LALMs和四种声学属性类型上发现不同程度对齐；因果干预确认这些神经元对预测必要且可操纵。

SLM-SS: Token-Based Speech Separation with Speech Language Models

作者：Chenda Li, Tianhua Li, Yiming Chen, Wei Wang, Jiahe Wang, Xin Zhou, Yanmin Qian

音频生成评分 14speechspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出SLM-SS，基于语音语言模型的token级生成式语音分离框架，使用序列化输出训练和逐说话人非自回归细化，提升分离语音的可懂度和主观质量。

解决了什么问题

现有语音分离系统优化信号级目标，输出在波形指标上可接受但对可懂度敏感的下游任务可能次优。

核心方法

将干净语音离散化为多码本token序列，使用序列化输出训练（SOT）编码多说话人目标，自回归模型预测零阶序列建立说话人顺序和粗语言内容，非自回归模块细化高阶残差码本；引入逐说话人非自回归策略简化高阶建模。

主要创新点

使用语音语言模型进行生成式语音分离，token级建模；逐说话人非自回归策略提高稳定性。

实验结论

在Libri2Mix上，SLM-SS with per-talker NAR 达到6.92% WER，接近纯编解码重建；WavTokenizer变体获得最高主观质量评分，UTMOS和DNSMOS优势在未见噪声和中文混合上持续，无需微调。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音分离、语音生成、语音语言模型研究者。

和已有工作的关系

神经编解码、序列化输出训练、语音语言模型。

对研究的启发

利用生成式模型和序列化输出可以处理多说话人分离，可能推广到其他音频任务。

值得深读，方法新颖，结果包括客观和主观指标。

SKAD: Sketch-Guided Audio-Contrastive Decoding for Reasoning in Large Audio-Language Models2610.28146v1其他
展开摘要

Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) have shown increasing potential in audio understanding and reasoning by generating intermediate reasoning steps before answering. However, directly eliciting long-form reasoning from audio does not always lead to more reliable predictions. Even reasoning-oriented LALMs may gradually shift from acoustic evidence to language-continuation patterns during generation. A natural solution is to use contrastive decoding with an audio-masked branch, but directly applying this strategy to plain chain-of-thought reasoning is often ineffective, since the masked branch lacks sufficient audio-related context and provides an unstable reference. To address this issue, we propose \textbf{SKAD} (\textbf{Sk}etch-Guided \textbf{A}udio-Contrastive \textbf{D}ecoding), a training-free inference-time method for audio reasoning. SKAD first constructs an acoustic evidence sketch that summarizes question-relevant auditory information. It then performs contrastive decoding between an audio-conditioned reasoning branch and a sketch-only branch. By using the sketch as a shared semantic scaffold, SKAD makes the masked branch more informative and encourages decoding decisions that are more dependent on the original audio. We evaluate SKAD on Qwen2.5-Omni-7B and Step-Audio-R1 across three audio understanding and reasoning benchmarks: MMAR, MMAU, and MMSU. Experimental results show that SKAD consistently improves the average accuracy of both models across all benchmarks. Further ablations show that direct contrastive decoding without the acoustic evidence sketch provides limited or negative gains, confirming the importance of constructing a reliable contrastive reference before audio-contrastive decoding.

Slots Are Not Speakers: Training-Free Speaker Linking for Frozen Streaming Diarizers2610.28053v1其他
展开摘要

This paper presents a causal, training-free linker that allows a frozen streaming diarizer to track more speakers than it has output slots. Streaming end-to-end diarizers such as Streaming Sortformer emit a fixed number N of concurrent speaker slots. Above N speakers, they keep detecting speech but reuse slots across people, because N bounds how many speakers the model can express at once, not how many it will meet. The linker uses only the host's slot posteriors and the audio. It keeps the frames where one slot is active, splits each slot where the speaker changes, embeds each piece with an off-the-shelf speaker encoder, and matches it against a bank of speakers seen so far. It only relabels, so missed speech and false alarm are identical to the host's and every gain is a reduction in speaker confusion. On 1418 sessions from eight corpora, it reduces the 4-slot host's DER above its cap from 36.8% to 22.6%, and barely moves it below the cap. The same constants transfer to the host's 8-slot successor and to two streaming EEND hosts from a different model family. On courtroom hearings with up to 23 speakers it reduces cpWER from 82.7% to 24.4%.

Uncovering Audio-Text Alignment in Large Audio-Language Models through Cross-Modal Concept Neurons2610.28189v1其他
展开摘要

While the language model backbone is pretrained on text alone, large audio-language models (LALMs) can, to a great extent, recognize acoustic attributes, such as emotion, speaker gender, and spoken language. However, it is unclear how the audio representation is integrated, if at all, with the other text representation before the model produces text. We hypothesize that there is a shared subspace where the audio and text representations are aligned. To test this hypothesis, we identify cross-modal neurons that are responsible for representing both audio and text. We find alignment between audio and text neurons to various degrees across three LALMs and four acoustic attribute types. Causal interventions, steering and deactivating the neurons, confirm that the cross-modal neurons are not just necessary for prediction but can also be manipulated.

SLM-SS: Token-Based Speech Separation with Speech Language Models2610.27785v1音频生成
展开摘要

Speech separation (SS) has witnessed substantial progress in recent years with the advent of neural network-based methods, achieving notable improvements on signal-level evaluation metrics. Existing speech separation systems are mostly optimized with signal-level objectives, and often yield outputs that are acceptable in waveform metrics but suboptimal for intelligibility-sensitive downstream tasks. To address this gap, we propose SLM-SS, a token-based generative speech separation framework that models multi-talker mixtures with speech language models. We first discretize clean speech into multi-codebook token sequences with neural codecs and serialize multi-speaker targets via Serialized Output Training (SOT). An encoder-decoder autoregressive speech language model predicts the zero-order sequence to establish speaker ordering and coarse linguistic content, and a non-autoregressive module then refines higher-order residual codebooks for efficient reconstruction. We further introduce a per-talker non-autoregressive strategy that simplifies high-order modeling and improves the stability. On Libri2Mix, SLM-SS with per-talker NAR achieves $6.92\%$ WER, approaching codec-only reconstruction. The WavTokenizer variant eliminates NAR refinement and achieves the highest mean subjective quality score among evaluated separation systems. Its UTMOS and DNSMOS advantages over discriminative baselines persist on unseen noisy and Chinese mixtures without fine-tuning.

AD-Mamba: Architecture-Complementary Knowledge Distillation for Efficient and Robust Audio Classification2610.27713v1其他
展开摘要

Recent advancements in large-scale audio classification have been primarily driven by Transformer-based architectures due to their strong capacity in modeling long-range dependencies. However, their inherent quadratic complexity limits scalability and efficiency, especially when processing long or sparsely informative audio streams. Mamba, a recently proposed state space model (SSM), offers a promising alternative by enabling efficient long-range modeling with linear sequence scaling. Nevertheless, existing Mamba-based audio classification methods still lag behind mainstream Transformer-based models, suggesting that effective training strategies for fully exploiting the audio modeling potential of SSMs remain underexplored. To address this issue, we propose AD-Mamba, an architecture-complementary multi-stage knowledge distillation framework that transfers the local modeling strength of CNNs and the global contextual modeling capability of Transformers into a Mamba-based student model. Specifically, the CNN-guided stages encourage the student to adapt to local spectro-temporal structures, while the Transformer-guided stage further refines global semantic discrimination and contextual modeling. Experimental results on AudioSet show that AD-Mamba achieves 50.3% mean average precision (mAP) with only 30M parameters, outperforming representative Transformer-based and Mamba-based audio classification baselines with lower computational cost. Further evaluations under long-form audio, sparse-information, and noise-interference conditions show that AD-Mamba consistently outperforms representative CNN and Transformer baselines. When transferred to FSD50K, ESC-50, and DCASE16, the proposed model also achieves superior performance, demonstrating strong generalization across audio classification tasks.

Timing and Reliability Limits of Position-Assisted UWB Multicast Audio2610.28245v1其他
展开摘要

One-to-many ultra-wideband (UWB) transport can disseminate shared audio blocks without replicating a downlink stream for every receiver. Yet common reception does not ensure aligned electrical playback, coincident acoustic arrival, or delivery to an entire receiver group. This paper develops a unified feasibility analysis of position-assisted UWB multicast audio that separates clock and actuation error, age-dependent acoustic propagation compensation, correlated block erasures, and reserved communication resources. We derive a conditional bound on one-listener arrival spread, a stationary expression for all-receiver block delivery under common and receiver-local losses, and complementary bounds for reliability-constrained admission. Reproducible Monte Carlo experiments examine 4,590 policy--run records across clock, localization, channel, and slot configurations. For eight receivers, affine clock correction produces a mean run-level 95th-percentile electronic span of $21.19\us$, while position assistance reduces the corresponding one-listener acoustic arrival span from $21.95\ms$ to $419.43\us$. Under the stated $30\us$ timing criterion, 98.86\% of compensated events still exceed the threshold. For 64 receivers, three block copies attain an all-receiver delivery probability of approximately 0.97478 under the modeled correlated channel. The analysis exposes the distinct failure mechanisms of position age, shared fades, retransmission limits, and schedule saturation, and establishes joint criteria for interpreting synchronous multicast audio performance.

LoCA: Local Credit Assignment for Reinforcement Learning of Autoregressive Flow-Matching TTS2610.28035v1语音合成
展开摘要

Reinforcement learning can improve text-to-speech synthesis through feedback on generated speech. However, sentence-level rewards do not identify local errors, while signed regression updates can lead to excessive fitting or repulsion of sampled outputs. We propose LoCA (Local Credit Assignment) for autoregressive flow-matching text-to-speech. LoCA uses ASR-based text alignment to locate content errors in generated speech and redistribute sentence-level advantages across acoustic frames. To improve training stability, we introduce reference-relative positive and negative thresholds that limit continued fitting and repulsion. Experiments on VoxCPM2 show that LoCA reduces sample-weighted content error on a multilingual CV3 evaluation subset by 20.5% and 14.3% relative to the pretrained backbone and GROW, respectively, while improving speaker similarity. LoCA also improves content accuracy on the Seed-TTS-Eval hard subset. Ablation results support the contributions of local credit and threshold-based regression control.

Improved automatic speech-based cognitive assessment using neuropsychologically calibrated diagnostic labels2610.28288v1其他
展开摘要

Although automatic detection of cognitive impairment from conversational speech is a promising way, it becomes less accurate, as large real-world datasets often contain unreliable diagnostic labels due to self-referral and limited clinical verification, and here we investigated whether neuropsychologically calibrated diagnostic relabeling improves it. We analysed 138 hours of conversational speech from 720 participants, recalibrating unreliable diagnostic labels using attention, executive function, and memory performance via external clinical thresholds, Gaussian, and normative z-score calibration. Transformer-based classifiers were trained using both original and recalibrated labels and evaluated internally on 150 clinically verified participants and externally on Pitt, ADReSS and PROCESS-2 datasets. Memory- and MoCA-based z-score calibration produced the greatest improvements. Using our internal dataset, $F_1$ increased from $0.70$ to $0.83$ ($+13\%$) and from $0.54$ to $0.66$ ($+12\%$) for binary and multiclass classification, respectively. For external validation, the increases are $+4\%$, $+5\%$ and $+9\%$ on Pitt, ADReSS and PROCESS-2 corpus. Neuropsychologically calibrated relabeling substantially improves speech-based cognitive assessment, highlighting label quality, rather than model architecture, as a critical bottleneck in clinical AI for dementia screening.

VISH-ASR: Viterbi-Guided Selection of Hotwords for Speech-LLM Contextual ASR2610.27867v1语音识别
展开摘要

Prompt-based contextual ASR requires selecting a compact hotword subset from large dynamic inventories, yet existing pre-selection methods rely either on discrete ASR hypotheses that may discard acoustic evidence or on separately trained retrieval models.
