No-Shot Text-to-Speech: Limitations of Zero-Shot TTS and its Evaluation Methods in Representing Queer and Transgender Voices

作者：Juliana Francis, Robin Netzorg, Joakim Gustafson, Éva Székely

语音合成评分 21speechvoicespeakertext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

评估六个零样本TTS模型在性别扩展声音上的表现，发现自动化评估与人类评分不一致，揭示数据与指标的代表性缺陷。

解决了什么问题

性别扩展声音在语音数据集中代表性不足，零样本TTS系统对这些声音的泛化能力可能受限，且现有评估方法可能无法准确反映人类感知。

核心方法

在包含非性别扩展（N-GE）和性别扩展（GE）声音的两个数据集上评估六个零样本TTS模型，采用人类评分（声音相似度）和自动化指标（说话人相似度、自动MOS预测器）进行对比。

主要创新点

首次系统评估零样本TTS在性别扩展声音上的性能，并揭示自动化评估与人类判断之间的冲突。

实验结论

人类评分显示6个模型中有2个对GE声音的相似度较低，3个有改进；自动化评估结果与人类评分不一致，表明评估流程存在盲点。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明样本量、模型具体名称、数据集规模及评估协议细节；结论仅基于两个数据集和六个模型。

适合谁读

语音合成、语音评估、包容性AI、性别研究及少数群体语音技术研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中未明确说明。

对研究的启发

需要构建更具代表性的语音数据集，并开发与人类感知更一致的自动化评估指标，同时纳入跨性别、酷儿等少数群体参与数据创建和模型训练。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为该研究揭示了零样本TTS在少数群体声音上的评估盲点，对公平性和包容性有实际意义；但需注意摘要未给出实验细节。

TOP 2

AudioTrace: File-Level Membership Detection for Audio Large Language Models

作者：Guangzhi Sun, Xiao Zhan, Shuai Wang, Jose Such, Phil Woodland

其他评分 19audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出音频文件级成员推理任务及AudioTrace方法，无需文本监督即可检测音频录音是否被用于训练音频LLM。

解决了什么问题

音频LLM训练使用公开录音引发隐私和版权问题，但现有成员推理攻击需要完整训练样本和文本监督，而实际中音频常配有私密生成的文本，录音所有者无法获得这些信息。

核心方法

构建AudioTraceBench基准（含1285条带明确微调成员标签的音频）；提出AudioTrace，利用目标模型生成音频条件下的代理问题，从模型响应中提取成员证据并在录音级别聚合。

主要创新点

首次系统研究音频文件级成员推理，无需假设训练任务和标注知识，提供专用基准和检测方法。

实验结论

在三个音频LLM上，现有文本和视频成员推理方法适应到音频后接近随机猜测；AudioTrace AUROC达0.60，且随着微调暴露或适应能力增加而提升。

可能局限

AUROC 0.60仍较低，接近随机水平；摘要未明确说明基准的具体来源、模型架构、微调设置及影响因素实验的细节。

适合谁读

机器学习隐私、版权审计、音频大语言模型安全及成员推理攻击研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提到现有文本和视频成员推理方法被适应到音频，但未列出具体文献。

对研究的启发

音频模态的无监督成员推理具有实际审计价值，未来可探索更有效的代理问题生成和证据聚合策略，以提高检测性能。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为提出了新任务和基准，对音频LLM审计具有开创性；但当前性能有限，需结合原文判断实际可用性。

Think2Listen: Evidence-Centric Learning For Agentic Audio Understanding With LALMS

作者：Jin Wang, Liang Lin, Kaiwen Luo, Weiliu Wang, Yuxuan Li, Zengxin Han, Gan Zhang, Jingtong Wu, Yufei Guo, Kun Wang

其他评分 17speechaudiomusicacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Think2Listen框架，通过证据中心学习使音频代理主动获取声学线索并迭代推理，在多个音频类别上取得显著提升。

解决了什么问题

大型音频语言模型能感知声学内容，但难以识别哪些线索能解决不确定决策，感知与推理之间存在差距。

核心方法

Think2Listen框架将迭代推理与针对性LALM交互相结合，主动寻找区分竞争假设的声学线索，根据观察结果修正候选判断；利用观察引起的候选状态变化分配训练信用，学习何时提问、如何使用证据、何时停止。

主要创新点

证据中心学习范式，将证据获取与推理过程耦合，通过反馈优化提问策略和停止时机。

实验结论

在两个基准七个音频类别上，多步音频推理准确率比竞争音频代理高5.08%-17.67%；音频理解与推理整体准确率比基线高2.29%-13.10%。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明基准具体名称、模型参数量、计算开销及训练数据规模。

适合谁读

音频理解、多模态代理、强化学习及推理系统研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提到近期LALM和工具代理的进展，但未列出具体文献。

对研究的启发

将证据获取作为学习目标，通过观察反馈优化信息寻求策略，可推广到其他多模态推理任务。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为提出了新框架并有开源代码，性能提升显著；但需查看原文了解实验细节和泛化性。

Post-fusion Event-aware Time-Frequency Refinement for Audio Moment Retrieval

作者：Feiyang Xiao, Li'ang Luo, Kejia Zhang, Qiaoxi Zhu, Guangjun He, Pengming Feng, Jian Guan

其他评分 17audioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出ETR-AMR框架，在音频-文本融合后使用事件感知的时间-频率细化来增强音频时刻检索中的事件判别特征。

解决了什么问题

音频时刻检索中查询相关事件稀疏且嵌入复杂声学环境，背景声和相似事件干扰大；现有方法主要在融合前增强语义交互，很少在时序定位前细化音频表示，难以区分目标事件。

核心方法

ETR-AMR在音频-文本交互后引入事件感知的时间-频率细化（ETR）策略，包括时间帧门控（估计帧级事件相关性）和频率细化（可学习的时间-频率重加权），联合优化融合特征。

主要创新点

后融合事件感知细化，同时从时间和频率两个域增强事件相关模式并抑制背景干扰。

实验结论

在四个AMR数据集上优于现有方法，尤其在高质量CASTELLA基准上表现突出；时长分析证实对中长音频时刻有效。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明具体提升幅度、模型架构细节、计算复杂度及与所有基线的完整对比。

适合谁读

音频时刻检索、多模态时序定位、声学事件检测研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提到现有方法主要加强音频-文本语义交互，但未列出具体工作。

对研究的启发

后融合细化思路可应用于其他多模态定位或检测任务，特别是特征融合后的域特定增强。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为针对AMR提出了有效的细化模块并有多个数据集验证；但需结合原文确认具体实现和性能边界。

Does the Voice Matter? Evaluating Frustration Detection on Deployed Clinical Voice-Agent Calls

作者：Annika Michael, Zachary Ellis, Yuxiang Zhou, Samuel Thio, Yajie Vera He, Spencer Hazel, Jared Joselowitz

其他评分 16speechaudiovoice查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

在真实临床语音代理通话中研究挫败感检测，发现患者话语极短时仅用转写文本不足以排除挫败，语音的作用因模型和指令而异。

解决了什么问题

临床语音代理自动分诊需检测患者挫败感，但现有系统通常只使用转写文本，忽略语音信息；真实临床语音中患者话语极短，文本信息有限，其有效性未经验证。

核心方法

在部署的临床语音代理真实通话上（150轮标注、714轮扩展）进行轮次级挫败检测，比较仅转写、仅音频、音频-文本结合及不同指令下五个音频语言模型的表现；使用长度匹配的表演语料作为对照；训练WavLM探针。

主要创新点

首次在真实临床部署数据上系统评估语音对挫败检测的贡献，揭示短话语场景下文本和语音的局限性。

实验结论

患者话语59%为一两个词，表演语料为13%；一词轮次上仅文本检测不排除随机；五个音频语言模型在30个通话中只标出39个挫败一词轮次中的3-4个；仅词数分类器达57.3%；音频是否有帮助取决于模型和指令：两个Gemini 2.5模型关注表达方式时比文本高3.0 pp，但其他模型或指令下音频可能更差；WavLM探针总体准确率71.6%但错过所有新增24个通话中的挫败一词轮次。

可能局限

样本量较小（150轮和714轮）；摘要未明确说明模型具体版本、训练细节、标注一致性度量及统计显著性。

适合谁读

语音情感识别、临床对话系统、人机交互及医疗AI研究者。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中未明确说明。

对研究的启发

短话语场景需要专门方法，仅增加音频不一定有效；可探索融合策略、上下文建模或主动学习，以处理信息极度稀疏的轮次。

是否值得精读

值得深读，因为基于真实部署数据，揭示了实际应用中的关键挑战，但样本量有限且结论受模型选择影响，需谨慎解读。

No-Shot Text-to-Speech: Limitations of Zero-Shot TTS and its Evaluation Methods in Representing Queer and Transgender Voices2611.20822v1语音合成
展开摘要

Gender expansive voices remain underrepresented in speech datasets, which could cause challenges with generalizability in zero-shot Text-To-Speech (TTS) systems. We evaluate six zero-shot TTS models on two datasets: one containing non gender expansive voices (N-GE) and one with gender expansive (GE) voices from self-identified speakers. Human ratings found that 2 of the 6 models synthesize less similar GE voices compared to N-GE voices, and 3 have improved performance for GE voices. Automated evaluations yield conflicting results, with speaker similarity and automated mean opinion score predictors rating performance of GE voices inconsistently between models and human ratings. This highlights a potential blind spot in evaluation pipelines, emphasizing a need for more representative data and metrics that better capture human perceptual judgments. Our findings stress the need to include transgender, queer, and minority communities in creation of better datasets and model training.

AudioTrace: File-Level Membership Detection for Audio Large Language Models2611.21088v1其他
展开摘要

Significant privacy and copyright concerns are raised by the use of publicly available audio recordings to train audio large language models (LLMs), yet effective auditing methods remain limited. Existing membership inference attacks typically assume knowledge of complete training examples, including their associated textual supervision. In practice, however, audio recordings may be paired with privately generated transcripts, captions, or question-answer pairs that are unavailable to owners of the recordings. This motivates audio-file-level membership inference, which determines whether a recording was used for training without assuming knowledge of its training tasks and annotations. This paper provides the first systematic investigation of this challenge and introduces AudioTraceBench, a controlled benchmark comprising 1,285 audio recordings with explicit fine-tuning membership labels. Evaluations across three audio LLMs show that existing text- and video-based membership inference methods adapted to audio perform close to random guessing. To address this challenge, AudioTrace is proposed, where audio-conditioned proxy questions are generated using the target model, and membership evidence is extracted from its responses and aggregated at the recording level. Consistent improvements over existing baselines are observed, with AUROC reaching 0.60 and increasing further under increased fine-tuning exposure or adaptation capacity. Further experiments were conducted to examine the factors influencing membership signals for audio-file-level detection. These findings establish audio-file-level membership inference as a distinct and practically important auditing challenge, with AudioTrace providing an effective initial approach to this challenge.

Think2Listen: Evidence-Centric Learning For Agentic Audio Understanding With LALMS2611.21083v1其他
展开摘要

Recent advances in large audio-language models (LALMs) have broadened audio understanding across speech, sound, and music, with tool-based agents further supporting these tasks by invoking predefined audio tools. However, a persistent gap remains between perceiving acoustic content and using it as evidence for reasoning: recognizing what is audible does not ensure that a model can identify which cues resolve an uncertain decision. To bridge this gap, we propose Think2Listen, an evidence-centric learning framework for agentic audio understanding. Guided by evidence before commitment, Think2Listen couples iterative reasoning with a targeted LALM interaction to actively seek acoustic cues that distinguish competing hypotheses and revise candidate judgments based on the observations returned. It learns what to ask, how to use evidence, and when to stop, using observation-induced changes in candidate-state to inform training credit. Extensive evaluations on two benchmarks spanning seven audio categories demonstrate that Think2Listen achieves (I) enhanced multi-step audio reasoning, with gains in overall accuracy of 5.08%--17.67% over competing audio agents; and (II) improved audio understanding and reasoning across speech, sound, and music, boosting overall accuracy by 2.29%--13.10% over the corresponding baselines. The source code is available at https://github.com/JinWang79/Think2Listen.

Post-fusion Event-aware Time-Frequency Refinement for Audio Moment Retrieval2611.20725v1其他
展开摘要

Audio Moment Retrieval (AMR) aims to locate the temporal segment within a long audio recording that semantically corresponds to a natural-language query. This task is particularly challenging because query-related audio events are often sparsely distributed and embedded in complex acoustic environments, where background sounds and acoustically similar events can easily obscure the target moments. Existing methods mainly strengthen audio-text semantic interaction to enhance the query-related information in audio frames, but seldom refine the resulting audio representations before temporal localization, limiting their ability to accurately distinguish target events from irrelevant acoustic interference. To address this issue, we propose ETR-AMR, a post-fusion event-aware time-frequency refinement framework for AMR. Specifically, an event-aware time-frequency refinement (ETR) strategy is proposed after audio-text interaction to refine fused audio frame features before temporal moment localization. ETR consists of two complementary mechanisms: time frame gating and frequency refinement. The time frame gating mechanism estimates frame-wise event relevance to emphasize query-related audio frames and suppress redundant temporal responses, while frequency refinement applies learnable time-frequency reweighting to enhance discriminative event-related frequency patterns and reduce background-dominant interference. By jointly refining fused audio frame features in the temporal and frequency domains, ETR produces more discriminative event-aware features for accurate temporal boundary prediction. Experiments on four AMR datasets demonstrate that ETR-AMR achieves superior performance compared with existing methods, especially on the high-quality CASTELLA benchmark. Duration-wise analysis further confirms the effectiveness of ETR for medium and long audio moments.

Does the Voice Matter? Evaluating Frustration Detection on Deployed Clinical Voice-Agent Calls2611.21001v1其他
展开摘要

Autonomous voice agents now conduct routine clinical follow-up calls, so some automated triage is unavoidable. A patient becoming frustrated is one thing that triage should catch. These systems typically read only the transcript the speech-to-text stage produces, not the voice, and whether that is enough is largely untested on real clinical speech. We study per-turn frustration detection on real patient calls from a deployed clinical voice agent: 150 turns from six calls labelled by three annotators, with a length-matched acted corpus as a control, and 714 turns from 30 calls. Patient turns are overwhelmingly short: across the deployed calls, roughly 59% are one or two words, against 13% in the full acted corpus, and a corpus accuracy gap remains even after the acted set is length-matched. On one-word turns, transcript-only detection does not exclude chance. Across five audio-capable language models, the transcript flags 3 or 4 of the 39 frustrated one-word turns in the 30 calls, and a classifier given only the word count reaches 57.3%. Whether audio helps depends on the model and on the instruction. Asked to attend to delivery, two Gemini 2.5 models gain 3.0 pp over the transcript across 30 calls (-1.0 to 7.5 pp). Of the turns they confidently clear, 6.9% were in fact frustrated for audio against 13.0% for the transcript, a difference of 6.1 pp (2.2 to 10.4 pp). Across the 30 calls, given one prompt identical for both inputs, no model's audio-text interval excludes zero.
