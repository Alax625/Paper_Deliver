MusicRWKV: A Multimodal RWKV Framework for Music Representation Learning

作者：Terry Tan

自监督学习评分 17musicself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出基于RWKV架构的多模态音乐表示学习框架MusicRWKV，通过可逆压缩和FiLM条件策略高效建模长音乐序列，并引入Soft ROSA进行可微分神经符号交互。

解决了什么问题

现有音乐基础模型多采用Transformer架构，其二次注意力复杂度在建模长时音乐时带来较高计算和内存开销。

核心方法

构建基于RWKV的多模态框架，联合建模波形、频谱和元数据；引入可逆压缩模块减少序列长度；使用FiLM条件策略实现模态感知表示；集成Soft ROSA作为可微分检索机制。

主要创新点

使用RWKV替代Transformer以降低复杂度；可逆压缩模块在缩短序列的同时保留重建能力；FiLM条件避免昂贵的交叉注意力；Soft ROSA实现神经符号交互。

实验结论

在多个MIR基准上表明该架构可有效适应异构音乐理解任务；对照预训练消融显示Soft ROSA提高验证性能；逐层分析显示深层网络从分布式检索过渡到选择性检索。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

音乐信息检索、自监督学习、多模态表示学习和高效序列建模领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

提及现有基于Transformer架构的音乐基础模型，作为效率对比的隐含基线。

对研究的启发

探索线性注意力架构（如RWKV）在长序列多模态学习中的潜力；可逆压缩和条件调制可迁移至其他高效多模态框架。

是否值得精读

是，提出了非Transformer的音乐表示学习框架，并在多个基准验证有效性，具有潜在效率优势。

TOP 2

The Unseen Threat: How Complementary Combinations of Acoustic Features Compromise Speech Privacy

作者：Jule Pohlhausen, Jörg Bitzer

语音识别评分 15speechvoiceacousticspeakerspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

研究发现时域声学特征（RMS和基频）与平滑Mel频谱融合会显著增加语音内容泄露，而单个特征隐私评估会低估融合风险。

解决了什么问题

声学特征组合在泄露私人信息方面的交互作用尚未被充分探索。

核心方法

评估时域特征（宽带RMS、基频）单独及与时间平滑和子采样Mel频谱组合的隐私泄露；使用知情ASR和说话人验证攻击评估隐私，使用VAD和说话人分离评估效用。

主要创新点

揭示特征融合的互补效应导致隐私泄露放大；强调隐私评估需考虑特征组合，而非仅评估单个特征。

实验结论

RMS和基频单独泄露有限信息，但与500ms平滑Mel频谱融合增强效用同时显著增加语音内容泄露；与RMS结合时细粒度时间动态放大粗糙频谱特征泄露；说话人身份保护对所有测试特征仍然有限。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

语音隐私、声学特征分析、语音识别和说话人验证安全领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

未明确提及具体相关工作，但隐含对单个特征隐私评估的常见做法。

对研究的启发

提醒隐私评估应考虑特征组合，特别是在发布声学特征时需注意融合带来的泄露风险。

是否值得精读

是，因为发现特征融合的隐私泄露被低估，具有重要安全意义。

Structured Speaker Conditioning for Diarization-Guided Target-Speaker ASR

作者：Ruoyu Wang, Shutong Niu, Jun Du, Weixin Zhu, Yannan Wang

语音识别评分 14audioacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出结构化说话人条件（SSC）用于diarization引导的目标说话人ASR，结合局部活动结构和会话级声学身份，通过条件低秩调制实现高效条件注入。

解决了什么问题

在diarization引导的目标说话人ASR中，如何有效结合局部活动结构和会话级身份以构建一致的目标条件。

核心方法

SSC结合TCRE（目标中心关系编码）保留每个说话人活动历史并分解二阶和三阶关系；RSC（基于参考的说话人条件）提供会话身份；CLRM（条件低秩调制）将条件转换为通道仿射调制和低秩跨通道残差。

主要创新点

提出TCRE捕获重叠和说话人转换；RSC消除局部活动歧义；CLRM实现条件依赖核心的低秩调制，参数量小（1.46M）。

实验结论

在Qwen3-ASR-0.6B上，oracle diarization下，SSC在四个会议测试集上平均相对降低6.23% cpER；跨音频基础模型迁移；使用预测diarization，FireRedAudio文本系统相对DiCoW和MOSS-TD平均降低17.9%和17.3%，并在三个未见领域表现更好；场景分析显示重叠条件下增益；时间戳变体性能相近。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

语音识别、说话人分离、会议转录和目标说话人提取领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

对比DiCoW、MOSS-TD以及四状态对角条件（作为基线）。

对研究的启发

结构化条件编码和低秩调制可用于其他说话人相关任务；线性复杂度关系编码适合多说话人场景。

是否值得精读

是，提供了详细方法和多个基准上的显著改进，并有消融分析。

Kezek: Splitting Mono Two-Person Conversations into Per-Speaker Tracks for Full-Duplex Dialogue Data

作者：Bagzhan Karl, Alizhan Karl

其他评分 12speechaudiovoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Kezek模型，将两人单声道对话分割为每说话人独立音轨，保持原始长度和时间，通过两阶段设计保留短回复并减少泄露。

解决了什么问题

全双工口语对话模型需要每说话人单独音轨，但大多数对话音频是单声道混合。

核心方法

两阶段：阶段1使用两语音活动模型和相位不敏感说话人指纹确定谁何时说话及身份；阶段2条件时域分离器执行分割，输出总和等于输入。

主要创新点

针对对话数据定制评估指标（短回复保留、泄露、通道交换、覆盖率）；模型返回整个录音避免丢弃；运行效率高。

实验结论

与pyannote->DialogueSidon相比，Kezek短回复保留更高（0.83 vs 0.69 Open Yap；0.80 vs 0.71 CallHome），泄露更少、通道交换更少、快4-5倍（约230小时/GPU小时）；在两者都返回的音频上回复保留相当。负面结果和理想掩码上限表明剩余差距在安静回复淹没在较大声音下。

可能局限

安静回复在较大声音下仍难以分离，存在剩余差距（负面结果和理想掩码上限表明）。

适合谁读

对话系统、语音分离、全双工口语建模和数据准备领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

与开源管道pyannote -> DialogueSidon对比。

对研究的启发

针对特定应用设计评估指标的重要性；两阶段决策与执行分离的思路可用于其他分离任务。

是否值得精读

是，提供了实用模型和详细评估，针对对话数据特性。

MusicRWKV: A Multimodal RWKV Framework for Music Representation Learning2611.16718v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Recent music foundation models have achieved remarkable progress in music representation learning, yet most existing approaches rely on Transformer architectures whose quadratic attention complexity introduces substantial computational and memory overhead when modeling long-duration music recordings. In this work, we propose MusicRWKV, a multimodal music representation learning framework built upon the RWKV architecture. MusicRWKV jointly models waveform, spectrogram, and metadata information within a unified self-supervised learning framework. To improve efficiency for long musical sequences, we introduce an invertible compression module that reduces sequence length while preserving reconstruction capability. A FiLM-based conditioning strategy is employed to facilitate modality-aware representation learning without introducing expensive cross-attention operations. We further investigate the integration of Soft ROSA as a differentiable retrieval mechanism for neural-symbolic interaction. Experimental evaluation across a diverse collection of Music Information Retrieval (MIR) benchmarks demonstrates that the proposed RWKV-based architecture can be effectively adapted to heterogeneous music understanding tasks. A controlled pretraining ablation shows that Soft ROSA improves validation performance, while layer-wise telemetry analysis reveals a progressive transition from distributed retrieval to selective retrieval in deeper network layers. These results suggest that RWKV provides a promising architectural alternative to Transformer-based models for multimodal music understanding.

The Unseen Threat: How Complementary Combinations of Acoustic Features Compromise Speech Privacy2611.16637v1语音识别
展开摘要

The interaction of acoustic features in leaking private information remains underexplored. This work focuses on time-domain features, i.e., broadband RMS level and pitch, alone and combined with temporally smoothed and subsampled Mel spectrograms. Privacy is assessed using informed automatic speech recognition and speaker-verification attacks, while utility for conversational analysis is evaluated using voice activity detection and speaker diarisation. RMS level and pitch alone exposed limited information, but fusion with 500 ms-smoothed Mel spectrograms enhanced utility while substantially increasing speech-content leakage. Specifically, the combination with RMS level showed that fine-grained temporal dynamics can amplify leakage from coarsely sampled spectral features. Speaker-identity protection remained limited for all tested features. Overall, these findings indicate that privacy assessments performed on individual acoustic features may underestimate leakage after feature fusion.

Structured Speaker Conditioning for Diarization-Guided Target-Speaker ASR2611.16371v1语音识别
展开摘要

We propose structured speaker conditioning (SSC) for diarization-guided target-speaker recognition. SSC combines local activity structure with session-level acoustic identity to construct identity-consistent target conditions that control the acoustic encoder. Target-centric relational encoding (TCRE) retains per-speaker activity histories and factorized second- and third-order relations, capturing overlap and turn changes with aggregation cost linear in the number of tracks. Reference-based speaker conditioning (RSC) supplies session-derived identities that disambiguate local activity patterns within TCRE. Conditional low-rank modulation (CLRM) converts the joint condition into channel-wise affine modulation and a low-rank cross-channel residual with a condition-dependent core. On Qwen3-ASR-0.6B with oracle diarization, SSC uses 1.46M conditioning parameters and achieves a 6.23% mean relative reduction in concatenated minimum-permutation error rate (cpER) over four-state diagonal conditioning across four meeting test sets. SSC also transfers across audio foundation models. With predicted diarization and target-error group sequence policy optimization, the FireRedAudio text system achieves mean relative error reductions of 17.9% and 17.3% over DiCoW and MOSS-TD on the same four sets, and also outperforms both on three unseen domains. Scenario analyses identify gains under challenging overlap conditions, while fixed-checkpoint interventions support the contributions of session identity and the condition-dependent core. A timestamp variant jointly produces sentence boundaries and text. With predicted diarization, macro-average cpER across the four sets is 15.60% for text and 15.96% with timestamps.

Kezek: Splitting Mono Two-Person Conversations into Per-Speaker Tracks for Full-Duplex Dialogue Data2611.16933v1其他
展开摘要

Full-duplex spoken dialogue models learn turn-taking, overlaps and backchannels from conversations recorded with one channel per speaker, but most available conversational audio is a single mono mix. We present Kezek, a model that turns a mono recording of exactly two people into one track per person with the original length and timing; overlaps and short replies ("yeah", "mm-hm") stay on the track of the person who said them. Kezek decides who speaks when and who is who once for the whole recording (stage 1: a two-voice activity model guided by a speaker fingerprint trained to be insensitive to phase), and a conditioned time-domain separator only carries out that decision (stage 2), with outputs that always sum to the input. Standard separation metrics miss the failures that matter for dialogue data, so we report short-reply retention, leakage onto the other person's solo, channel swaps and coverage. Against the open pipeline pyannote -> DialogueSidon on the same recordings, Kezek keeps more short replies (0.83 vs. 0.69 on Open Yap test; 0.80 vs. 0.71 over the other's speech on unseen telephone speech from CallHome English), mostly because it returns the whole recording while the external pipeline drops part of it (on the audio both return, reply retention is about the same: 0.859 vs. 0.865 on Open Yap); it also leaks less, swaps channels less often, and runs 4-5 times faster (about 230 hours of audio per GPU-hour). We also report negative results and an ideal-mask ceiling that locate the remaining gap: quiet replies under a louder voice. Code and weights are released under permissive licences.

Evaluation of Music Version Retrieval Methods for Setlist Identification in Live Concerts2611.17070v1其他
展开摘要

Automatic setlist identification aims to identify the songs performed in live concert recordings by matching noisy and variable-quality audio against a database of reference tracks. This task presents challenges beyond conventional music version identification, including substantial arrangement differences, background noise, and the need to process continuous recordings efficiently at large scale. In this work, we evaluate modern music version retrieval methods for setlist identification using the ASID benchmark, comparing the established Re-MOVE system with CLEWS and NAVE, an internal neural audio version embedding model. Our results show that NAVE and CLEWS substantially outperform Re-MOVE. Notably, NAVE uses 256-dimensional embeddings, compared with 1024 dimensions for CLEWS, providing a four-fold reduction in embedding storage while achieving better retrieval performance. We further evaluate music/non-music gating and find modest improvements in detection and temporal coverage, while qualitative analysis suggests that such preprocessing can improve recognition boundaries beyond what the standard ASID metrics capture. Overall, the results demonstrate that segment-level audio embeddings provide a strong basis for scalable setlist identification and that compact representations can achieve strong accuracy under realistic live-concert conditions at much lower storage and serving cost.

Beyond Word Error Rate: A Failure Taxonomy of Streaming Dictation ASR2611.16919v1语音识别
展开摘要

Speech recognition (ASR) systems are usually compared by word error rate (WER) on recorded files, after punctuation, capital letters and number format are removed. In dictation, the audio is streamed live and the text goes into the user's message as written, so what matters is which mistakes the user has to fix. The test set, streamed to eleven ASR systems, comprises 106 scripted English dictations from one speaker (read in a quiet room, with a fan on, and whispered), 20 clips in each of eleven other language and dialect sets, and 400 recordings that join clips in two languages. Every error was sorted by a fixed rule into four levels: recognition, output formatting, language handling and, for the self-hosted models, audio dropped before the model. Systems with similar WER failed in different ways. Zoom Scribe and OpenAI's GPT-Live-Transcribe have almost the same WER on quiet English (7.4% and 7.3%), but 34.7% and 8.8% on whispered English. Zoom and both AssemblyAI models left out 26-28% of whispered words, and still 21-26% with their end-of-turn detection relaxed or off; seven other systems left out at most 3%. Qwen3-ASR's 32% came from the speech detection in our own deployment (6% when relaxed). Across 107 EARS speakers, the same four systems lost 7-31% of whispered words, while six others lost under 2%. With only English selected, Gemini, Deepgram and our self-hosted Whisper lost the non-English part of a two-language recording in 86-99% of recordings, against 33-46% with both languages selected, while GPT-Live-Transcribe, OpenAI's realtime Whisper and Meta lost it in at most 20%. Running Whisper behind a Silero voice activity detector that drops audio judged to be silence raised whispered WER from 10.1% to 51.4%. Most English results come from one read speaker. The code, the recordings, both references and every transcript are released.

Human-Likeness and Conversational Preference Diverge for Synthesized Agent Speech in Customer-Service Calls2611.16051v1语音合成
展开摘要

Listeners can judge an agent's speech on how human it sounds (human-likeness) and on how much they would want to talk to it (conversational preference). Listening tests rarely ask about conversational preference, so the relation between the two judgments is unclear. We compared them in paired-choice tests on 120 turns that actors improvised as customer-service representatives. On each trial, two agents spoke the same turn. In Study 1, both agents came from six commercial text-to-speech systems, each with eight voices. In Study 2, one was the actor's original recording and the other a system.
