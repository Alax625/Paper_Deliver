GigaSpeech 3: Scaling Multi-Dialect Arabic Speech Recognition with Weakly Labeled Data

作者：Junzhe Liu, Yujie Tu, Yifan Yang, Peng Wang, Haoran Wang, Guodong Lin, Leyan Liao, Chengquan Zhang, Zhifu Gao, Xiangang Li, Qinyuan Cheng, Xipeng Qiu, Wei-Qiang Zhang, Guoguo Chen, Kai Yu, Xie Chen

语音识别评分 25speechaudiospeech recognitionself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出 GigaSpeech 3 语料库，利用语音识别偏置的自监督学习和 noisy-student 精炼构建 39,000 小时多方言弱转录数据，显著提升阿拉伯语语音识别性能。

解决了什么问题

方言阿拉伯语语音识别因缺乏方言分辨的转录语音而受限，尽管网络音频丰富。

核心方法

从每种方言 60 小时人工转录开始，训练语音识别偏置的自监督标注模型，应用于方言匹配的网络音频，经数据选择和 noisy-student 精炼构建语料库，并用于方言特定和多方言模型训练。

主要创新点

构建大规模多方言弱转录语料库；使用语音识别偏置自监督学习进行标注；采用 noisy-student 精炼提升语料质量；发布语料库和训练配方。

实验结论

在 GigaSpeechBench 上，方言特定模型宏平均词错误率 23.86%，相对最强第三方系统降低 16.6%；多方言模型宏平均词错误率 25.51%；外部评估中 GigaSpeech 3 训练的 Zipformer 在全部八个条件下词错误率最低，Qwen3 适应后性能改善。

适合谁读

语音识别、阿拉伯语处理、自监督学习、语料库构建领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中未明确说明。

对研究的启发

利用少量人工转录结合自监督学习扩展弱标注数据，通过 noisy-student 精炼提升质量，可应用于其他低资源方言或语言。

是否值得精读

该工作发布了大规模语料库并展示了显著性能提升，对多方言语音识别研究具有参考价值。

Zero-Shot Voice Cloning for Synthetic Data Augmentation in Children's ASR

作者：Mohammad Mohsen Goodarzi, Stefan Wabnik, Matthias Stennes, Stephanus Volke, Alexander Schwarz

语音识别评分 22speechvoiceacousticspeakertext-to-speechspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

研究零样本语音克隆合成儿童语音用于数据增强，微调 Whisper-small 可有效降低儿童 ASR 词错误率，合成数据与真实数据结合接近真实数据效果。

解决了什么问题

儿童自动语音识别落后于成人，因成人训练的声学模型与儿童语音的声学不匹配，以及标注儿童语音语料稀缺。

核心方法

使用 OmniVoice 多语言 TTS 系统进行零样本语音克隆合成儿童样话语，在 ENNI 和 MyST 语料上微调 Whisper-small，并系统分析说话人数量、文本池大小和话语数的影响。

主要创新点

验证零样本语音克隆作为儿童 ASR 数据增强的可行性；发现多说话人声学多样性而非文本覆盖率是跨域泛化的主要决定因素；OOV 率是 WER 的差代理。

实验结论

合成数据单独将 ENNI 上 WER 从 19.8% 降至 16.4%，恢复约 30% 真实数据微调差距；合成与真实数据结合 WER 9.2% 对比真实数据 8.8%；说话人数量约 40 时跨域迁移趋于平稳。

适合谁读

儿童语音识别、数据增强、语音合成、ASR 微调领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

对研究的启发

OmniOPD: Stable On-Policy Distillation for Joint Audio-Video Generation

作者：Zihao Fan, Xin Lu, Jie Huang, Hexin Zhang, Xueyang Fu, Zheng-Jun Zha

其他评分 15audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出 OmniOPD 框架，通过模态感知的 on-policy 蒸馏解决联合音频视频生成中跨模态干扰和音频监督稀疏问题，稳定蒸馏并提升视频生成。

解决了什么问题

联合音频视频生成中，优化异构模态奖励引入跨模态干扰，on-policy 蒸馏中音频 token 数量少导致监督稀疏，蒸馏不稳定，且单纯增加音频损失权重无效。

核心方法

提出 OmniOPD，包括非对称噪声调度构建特权视觉信息、分组多噪声采样扩展音频监督、基于教师-学生转移散度的时间步重要性加权。

主要创新点

识别音频学习依赖视觉上下文，通过特权视觉信息改进音频教师目标；分组多噪声采样和重要性加权稳定训练。

实验结论

在 JavisBench 和 VBench 上，OmniOPD 保持音频质量同时显著增强视频生成，实现有效联合蒸馏。

Voice-Native LLMs Hear Frustration but Do Not Listen to It

作者：Hanjun Luo, Yuhan Lu, Hanan Salam

语音识别评分 13speechaudiovoice查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

研究语音原生 LLM 对沮丧语音的感知和利用，发现模型能检测但不利用语音中的沮丧信息，对沮丧措辞的不变性才是真正鲁棒性。

解决了什么问题

语音原生模型对用户沮丧的感知和利用未被充分评估，现有评估将不变性能误认为鲁棒性。

核心方法

构建 VoxAffect 数据集，采用交叉沮丧措辞和语音的 2x2 设计，测量表达、感知和利用，并使用线性探针解码分析。

主要创新点

提出测量表达、感知和利用的评估框架；发布 VoxAffect 数据集；发现模型听到但不听沮丧语音。

实验结论

在 8 个模型上，对 7 个模型准确性无可检测语音效果；中性措辞下所有模型在最多 3.1% 片段中标记沮丧语音为沮丧，但 7 个模型检测到表演愤怒在 73-100% 片段；线性探针解码配对准确率 0.67 和 0.69；模型对沮丧措辞的不变性在 40-75% 片段中被感知。

GigaSpeech 3: Scaling Multi-Dialect Arabic Speech Recognition with Weakly Labeled Data2611.02476v1语音识别
展开摘要

Dialectal Arabic speech recognition remains constrained by the scarcity of dialect-resolved transcribed speech despite the abundance of web audio. We introduce GigaSpeech 3, a corpus of 39,000 hours and 10 million weakly transcribed speech segments covering Algerian, Egyptian, Iraqi, Saudi, and Syrian Arabic. Starting from 60 hours of manual transcription per dialect, we train labeling models using speech-recognition-biased self-supervised learning and apply them to dialect-matched web audio. Data selection and four rounds of noisy-student refinement for Saudi and Syrian Arabic yield the final corpus. We validate its utility through dialect-specific and multi-dialect speech recognition training and cross-domain evaluation. On GigaSpeechBench, the final dialect-specific models outperform their labeling models across all five dialects and achieve a macro-average word error rate of 23.86%, a 16.6% relative reduction from the strongest third-party system covering all five dialects, while outperforming the best third-party result on three dialects. A multi-dialect model using self-predicted dialect identities achieves a macro-average word error rate of 25.51% while also supporting Modern Standard Arabic. Across eight conditions from three external corpora, a GigaSpeech 3-trained Zipformer achieves the lowest word error rate among six evaluated models in every condition, while adapting the Qwen3 speech recognizer on GigaSpeech 3 improves performance across all eight conditions. We release the corpus and recipes as a foundation for dialect-aware Arabic speech processing.

Zero-Shot Voice Cloning for Synthetic Data Augmentation in Children's ASR2611.03050v1语音识别
展开摘要

Children's automatic speech recognition (ASR) lags behind adult ASR due to theacoustic mismatch between adult-trained acoustic models and children's speech, as well as the scarcity of labelled children's speech corpora. We investigate whether zero-shot text-to-speech (TTS) synthesis with voice cloning, requiring only a short reference clip per speaker, can serve as a practical substitute for real children's speech when fine-tuning large pre-trained ASR models. Using the OmniVoice multilingual TTS system we synthesise up to 16,000 child-like utterances from two English corpora (ENNI, clinical narrative samples, ages 7-12, and MyST, conversational science-tutor speech, grades 3-5) and fine-tune Whisper-small under different experimental conditions covering real data, synthetic data, cross-corpus data, and all-data combination. A systematic sensitivity analysis over speaker count, text-pool size, and utterances per speaker reveals that synthetic data alone reduces word error rate (WER) from a 19.8 % zero-shot baseline to 16.4 % on ENNI, recovering approximately 30 % of the gap to real-data fine-tuning (8.8 %). Combining synthetic with real data is essentially indistinguishable from real-data-only fine-tuning (9.2 % vs. 8.8 %). Speaker count plateaus at ~40 speakers for cross-domain transfer, while multi-speaker acoustic diversity per transcript, rather than raw text coverage, emerges as the primary determinant of cross-domain generalisation; out-of-vocabulary (OOV) rate proves to be a poor proxy for WER given Whisper's pre-trained language modelling capacity.

OmniOPD: Stable On-Policy Distillation for Joint Audio-Video Generation2611.02803v1其他
展开摘要

Reinforcement learning (RL) elevates joint audio-video generation, but optimizing heterogeneous, modality-specific audio and video rewards often introduces cross-modal interference. On-policy distillation (OPD) alleviates this issue by transferring the capabilities of specialized audio and video RL teachers into a single student model. However, a significant imbalance in cross-modal token counts leaves audio with sparse supervision, causing unstable distillation. We observe that naively increasing the audio loss weight fails to solve this problem since audio learning relies heavily on visual context, indicating richer visual cues are crucial to offset sparse audio signals. To address this, we propose OmniOPD, a modality-wise on-policy distillation framework designed for balanced and stable audio-video generation. First, we construct privileged visual information using asymmetric noise scheduling, allowing the audio teacher to generate more informative targets from cleaner video context. Second, we introduce grouped multi-noise sampling to expand audio supervision coverage and stabilize training. Third, we introduce timestep-wise importance weighting based on teacher-student transition divergence, preserving reliable supervision while down-weighting unreliable learning signals, to stabilize fluctuations in audio learning. Evaluated on JavisBench and VBench demonstrate OmniOPD preserves audio quality while significantly enhancing video generation, achieving effective joint distillation.

Ananas: A Networked Audio System for Distributed Spatial Audio Applications2611.03077v1其他
展开摘要

Spatial audio has an accessibility problem. Largescale spatial and immersive audio installations remain costly and exclusive, with high barriers to entry. We propose ''Ananas,'' a networked audio system consisting of network protocols, control software and microcontroller firmware, with the aim of rendering spatial audio applications accessible to a broad cohort of potential users. Based on a network of low-cost microcontroller development boards programmed in the Faust programming language, synchronised to nanosecond order via IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol, and controlled by approachable, digital audio workstation-based tooling, Ananas represents an opportunity to disrupt and democratise the spatial audio landscape. In this paper we demonstrate Ananas's protocols for audio and control data transmission and device discovery, its methods for media clock and presentation time recovery, and its applications to wave field synthesis and higher order ambisonics in a modular, scalable form.

Voice-Native LLMs Hear Frustration but Do Not Listen to It2611.02893v1语音识别
展开摘要

Users convey frustration in what they say and in how they say it, and voice-native models, which hear speech instead of an ASR transcript, can register both. Existing evaluations read unchanged performance as robustness without checking perception. Following Brunswik's lens model, we measure on identical audio whether frustration is expressed, perceived, and utilized. We cross frustrated wording with frustrated voice in a within-item 2x2 design over 353 tasks and release the 1,412 clips as VoxAffect. Across 8 models, outputs look robust, with no detectable vocal effect on accuracy for 7. With neutral wording, all 8 label the frustrated voice frustrated in at most 3.1% of clips, although 7 of them detect acted anger in 73-100% of clips and human listeners classify the voice correctly in 65.3% of judgments. Yet linear probes decode the frustrated voice from the audio encoders of the 2 open-weight models, with paired accuracy 0.67 and 0.69, and in proprietary models too the voice raises perceived frustration once the words agree. The models thus hear the frustrated voice but do not listen to it, whereas their invariance to frustrated wording, perceived in 40-75% of clips, is genuine robustness. Our work is openly accessible at https://github.com/Astarojth/hear-not-listen.

ReplayFactor: Replay-Aware Dual-Attribute Learning for Practical Speech Deepfake Detection2611.02628v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Advances in synthesis techniques have made deepfake speech increasingly realistic, amplifying voice security threats, including unauthorized voice cloning and impersonation. Although existing deepfake detectors perform well on digitally generated speech, their performance often degrades on physically replayed deepfakes, which constitute a low-cost and practical threat to real-world systems. Existing research focuses primarily on dataset construction and evaluation, while available dataset diversity and replay-aware detector design remain limited. We propose ReplayFactor, a dual-attribute framework that enhances replay evidence by fusing self-supervised learning (SSL) representations with an acoustic prior, while leveraging multi-task learning and supervised contrastive learning to promote robust, generalizable representations. We also construct ReplayFake-CN, a large-scale, multi-device Chinese dataset comprising approximately 185k replayed synthetic utterances to complement public benchmarks. Our framework improves macro-F1 from 60.99% to 76.44% and reduces EER from 11.86% to 3.05% on the EchoFake open set over the baseline, demonstrating strong potential for practical replayed deepfake detection.

ufakzeka-kulak: Building and Evaluating a 31.6M-Parameter Turkish Speech Recognizer from Scratch2611.03605v1其他
展开摘要

ufakzeka-kulak is an open Turkish speech-to-text model of 31.6M parameters, a FastConformer encoder with a token-and-duration transducer (TDT) and a CTC head, trained from scratch and released under Apache-2.0. It writes cased, punctuated Turkish with numbers as words. Its 4,977 training hours come from sources whose licences allow commercial use; 281 hours are human-transcribed, and for the other 4,696 the training text is an uploader subtitle or a machine transcript, kept only where a second, independent reading of the audio agrees with it. The recipe, checkpoint, labelling and export choices were made on development data, and the test sets were scored once, in a release gate fixed before training. With greedy TDT decoding the normalised word error rate (WER) is 0.0921 (95 percent interval 0.0792 to 0.1055) on FLEURS Turkish test and 0.0916 (0.0873 to 0.0958) on Common Voice 27.0 Turkish test, lower than Whisper base and Whisper small on both sets with paired intervals wholly below zero; Whisper large-v3 (greedy) scores 0.0604 and 0.0767. On conversational speech the WER is 0.3807. We also report punctuation and casing, degraded audio, and how closely each shipped export reproduces the trained model.

A Compact Statistical View of Musical-Instrument Directivity2611.02927v1其他
展开摘要

Directivity is a defining feature of musical-instrument radiation and is known to vary strongly with frequency. With the growing availability of measured directivity datasets and their use in sound-field simulation and spatial audio, there is a practical need for representations that are both compact and comparable across sources. This work addresses that need by proposing a statistically grounded description of frequency-dependent directivity that reduces complex radiation patterns to a small set of meaningful descriptors. The approach enables directivity patterns to be summarized in an interpretable way and compared consistently across frequency bands, even when the radiation is not well described by a single dominant direction. The resulting representation supports tasks such as analysis, categorization, and model building, providing a bridge between detailed measurements and higher-level descriptors useful for musical acoustics and audio rendering.
