Joint Language Modeling for In-the-Wild Speech-Scene Generation with Distinct Token Representations

作者：Semin Kim, Ju Yeon Kang, Sunghwan Ahn, Seonuk Kim, Nam Soo Kim

其他评分 21speechaudioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一种基于大语言模型的联合建模框架，通过分离的语音和环境token流生成野外语音场景。

解决了什么问题

语音和环境音频在语义和声学特性上差异显著，但现有方法通常使用单一混合声学表示建模整个场景；同时，真实场景中语音和环境声紧密相关，而传统方法依赖独立录制的源混合模拟场景。

核心方法

使用源感知神经音频编解码器从混合音频中提取时间对齐的语音和环境token，由预训练大语言模型以交错方式生成两个token流，条件为自然语言场景描述和语音内容。

主要创新点

提出分离的语音和环境token表示，并在共享生成上下文中联合建模；直接从混合录音中提取token流，使模型能适应野外语音场景，无需孤立源。

实验结论

实验结果表明该框架在语音可懂度、场景相关性和音频质量上优于现有技术，且真实世界适应带来一致增益。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及局限性。

适合谁读

语音生成、音频场景合成、多模态大语言模型领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

现有方法通常使用单一混合声学表示建模，或依赖模拟场景混音；本工作区分于使用独立源的方法。

对研究的启发

可借鉴分离源特定token表示和交错生成策略，用于其他多源音频生成任务；直接从混合数据学习避免对孤立源的依赖。

是否值得精读

摘要描述了新颖的建模框架和针对真实数据的适应能力，但缺乏具体实验细节，可进一步阅读原文评估效果。

Sonic Palette: Predicting Dynamic Color Themes from Music

作者：Wen-Li Wei, Kenneth Yang, Jen-Chun Lin

其他评分 15audiomusic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Sonic Palette，一个从音乐预测动态色彩主题的框架，将音乐-色彩对应建模为跨模态序列预测。

解决了什么问题

音乐到调色板生成通常被视为静态问题，未建模调色板级视觉属性随音乐的时间演变。

核心方法

使用以音乐为条件的自回归Transformer，从输入音频特征预测逐帧色彩主题，每个主题由五种主色表示；并采用调色板引导的重着色过程进行可视化。

主要创新点

将音乐-色彩对应作为跨模态序列预测任务，构建了包含265个完整官方音乐视频的音频-色彩序列数据集，并提出调色板引导的重着色可视化方法。

实验结论

定量结果表明预测的色彩主题更接近真实值，主观结果表明色彩过渡更自然、音乐-色彩氛围对齐更强。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及局限性。

适合谁读

音乐可视化、计算艺术、跨模态生成领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

现有工作通常将音乐到调色板生成视为静态问题，本工作强调时间动态。

对研究的启发

可借鉴序列预测方法处理其他跨模态时间同步任务，如音乐到动画参数生成；数据集构建思路值得参考。

是否值得精读

提出了新的任务形式和时间动态建模，但摘要缺少具体指标数值，需进一步查看原文。

Xing-Audio-NVVASR: Scaling and Refining Supervision for Joint Recognition of Speech and Non-Verbal Vocalizations

作者：Pengyu Xu, Gang Liu, Genliang Zhao, Hongjie Chen, Jian Kang, Jie Lian, Jie Li, Yongxiang Li

语音识别评分 12speechaudioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Xing-Audio-NVVASR，一个通过渐进数据挖掘和迭代监督细化训练的端到端模型，用于联合识别语音和非语言发声。

解决了什么问题

联合识别语音和非语言发声受限于事件丰富训练数据稀缺以及标签不准确或缺失。

核心方法

采用渐进数据挖掘和迭代监督细化：两个异构模型依据统一声学准则交叉审计标注，分歧交教师重新标注；互补呼吸检测器筛查整个语料库供人工审核；多轮细化，最终用约80万 utterances 训练一个1.7B参数模型，以行内标签转录语音及六类非语言发声。

主要创新点

提出渐进数据挖掘和迭代监督细化流程，结合模型交叉审计与互补呼吸检测器发现共享遗漏；多轮标注积累并保留；训练单一模型同时识别语音和非语言发声。

实验结论

在四个人工标注基准上，该模型在综合得分和六类Macro-F1上排名第一（九个模型中）；平均综合得分64.46，超过最强对比模型StepAudio 3 Chat Preview 6.03分；呼吸F1超过标注教师14.40到31.57个百分点。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及局限性。

适合谁读

语音识别、非语言发声识别、多模态模型领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

现有工作受限于数据稀缺和标签质量；本工作通过数据挖掘和标签细化克服。

对研究的启发

可借鉴渐进数据挖掘和迭代标签细化策略，用于其他标注稀缺的多标签识别任务；交叉审计与互补检测器结合的思想值得探索。

是否值得精读

From Attention to Semantics: Detecting and Mitigating Sound Event Hallucinations in Large Audio-Language Models

作者：Tianyi Sun, Minghao Lv, Chunyang Yu, Shenyue Wang, Mengyue Wu

其他评分 11audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一种可解释且无需训练的检测-缓解框架，用于检测和减轻大型音频语言模型中的声音事件幻觉。

解决了什么问题

大型音频语言模型容易幻觉输入中不存在的声音事件；之前工作将幻觉归因于音频注意力不足，但作者发现真实和幻觉事件可能获得相似的音频注意力质量，但关注不同位置，且幻觉事件与所关注表示的语义不匹配。

核心方法

采用 detect-then-mitigate 框架：聚合语义支持，并应用差分音频掩码，保护与预测为受支持事件相关的区域；基于 LatentLens 检索邻近上下文化文本表示来检查语义支持。

主要创新点

发现了真实和幻觉事件在注意力上的差异（位置不同、语义不匹配），并提出基于语义支持聚合和差分音频掩码的可解释、无需训练的检测与缓解方法。

实验结论

在三个大型音频语言模型和两个数据集上的实验表明，该方法显著改善了幻觉检测和缓解性能。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及局限性。

适合谁读

音频语言模型、幻觉检测、可解释性领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

之前工作将幻觉与音频注意力不足关联；本工作进一步揭示注意力位置和语义不匹配问题。

对研究的启发

可借鉴基于语义支持的可解释性分析用于其他幻觉问题；差分音频掩码策略可用于保护关键区域。

是否值得精读

发现了有趣的注意力机制现象，并提出了无需训练的方法，但摘要缺少量化指标，建议阅读原文评估。

UMelCodec: An Ultra-Low-Bitrate and Lightweight Mel-Domain Neural Speech Codec

作者：Hui-Peng Du, Yang Ai, Zhen-Hua Ling

语音合成评分 8speechacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出UMelCodec，一个在梅尔域工作的超低比特率轻量级神经语音编解码器，比特率325 bps。

解决了什么问题

超低比特率语音编码需要在仅几百bps下保持可懂度、自然度和精细声学细节，现有纯声学编解码器比特率较高，而语义编解码器降低比特率但复杂度增加。

核心方法

两阶段：第一阶段在梅尔域用AF-SC编码器-解码器建模时间和谐波结构，因子化单码本矢量量化实现超低比特率量化，多尺度梅尔频谱判别器改善重建；第二阶段用轻量级MDCT神经声码器从重建的梅尔频谱预测MDCT谱并通过逆MDCT恢复波形。

主要创新点

纯声学、超低比特率（325 bps）、轻量级架构；提出AF-SC骨干、因子化单码本VQ和MSMD判别器。

实验结论

在LibriTTS数据集上的实验表明，UMelCodec在大多数客观重建指标上优于强基线，同时保持有竞争力的主观自然度；模型参数19.67M，MACs 1.93G，支持GPU和CPU实时推理。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及局限性。

适合谁读

语音编码、神经声码器、低比特率通信领域的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

现有纯声学编解码器比特率较高，语义编解码器复杂度高；本工作实现纯声学超低比特率。

对研究的启发

可借鉴梅尔域编码和MDCT声码器结合的低复杂度设计；因子化VQ和判别器可用于其他低比特率任务。

是否值得精读

提供了具体性能和效率数据，表明方法的实用价值，值得阅读详细了解架构。

Joint Language Modeling for In-the-Wild Speech-Scene Generation with Distinct Token Representations2611.25503v1其他
展开摘要

Speech-scene generation aims to synthesize intelligible target speech together with diverse environmental sounds as a coherent acoustic scene. The task is considered challenging in two respects. Speech and environmental audio differ substantially in their semantic and acoustic characteristics, yet existing approaches typically model the complete scene using a single mixed acoustic representation. At the same time, the two sounds are closely related in real-world speech scenes, whereas conventional approaches usually rely on simulated scenes mixed from independently recorded sources. We propose a large language model (LLM)-based framework for joint modeling of distinct speech and environment token streams. A source-aware neural audio codec extracts temporally aligned speech and environment tokens from mixed audio, and a pretrained LLM generates both token streams in an interleaved fashion conditioned on the natural-language scene description and speech content. This formulation preserves source-specific representations while modeling the token streams within a shared generative context. Since both streams are extracted directly from mixed recordings, the model can also be adapted to in-the-wild speech scenes without isolated sources. Our experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework improves speech intelligibility, scene relevance, and audio quality over existing speech-scene generation techniques, with consistent gains from real-world adaptation. We further investigate the effects of distinct token representations and interleaved generation, and analyze whether the environmental sound remains stable while speech is active and how the two streams interact.

Sonic Palette: Predicting Dynamic Color Themes from Music2611.25781v1其他
展开摘要

We present Sonic Palette, a music-driven framework for predicting time-varying color themes from audio. Rather than treating music-to-palette generation as a static problem, our method formulates music--color correspondence as a cross-modal sequence prediction task that models how palette-level visual attributes evolve over time in response to music. Specifically, Sonic Palette uses a music-conditioned autoregressive Transformer to predict frame-wise color themes, each represented by five dominant colors, from input audio features. To support this task, we curate a dataset of paired audio--color sequences derived from 265 full-length official music videos. We further visualize the predicted color themes through a palette-guided recoloring process that preserves image structure while revealing temporally varying color appearance associated with the music. Quantitative results show that Sonic Palette predicts color themes that more closely match the ground truth, while subjective results indicate more natural temporal color transitions and stronger perceived music--color atmosphere alignment, highlighting its potential for music-driven visual design, computational art, and creative visual authoring.

Xing-Audio-NVVASR: Scaling and Refining Supervision for Joint Recognition of Speech and Non-Verbal Vocalizations2611.26000v1语音识别
展开摘要

Joint recognition of speech and non-verbal vocalizations (NVVs) is hindered by scarce event-rich training data and inaccurate or missing event labels. We present Xing-Audio-NVVASR, an end-to-end recognizer trained through progressive data mining and iterative supervision refinement. Guided by unified acoustic criteria, two heterogeneous models cross-audit annotations, flagging disagreements for teacher reannotation. To uncover shared omissions that model agreement may conceal, complementary breath detectors screen the entire corpus for human review. Human-confirmed labels accumulate across rounds and are preserved during subsequent refinement. Over five rounds, we refine annotations for 170,000 utterances from public corpora and incorporate 630,000 mined utterances. The resulting corpus of approximately 800,000 utterances is used to train a single 1.7B-parameter model that transcribes speech with inline tags for six NVV classes. Across four human-annotated benchmarks, Xing-Audio-NVVASR ranks first among nine models in both composite score and six-class Macro-F1. It achieves a mean composite score of 64.46, surpassing the strongest comparison model, StepAudio 3 Chat Preview, by 6.03 points, and exceeds the annotation teacher's breathing F1 by 14.40 to 31.57 percentage points. We plan to release the model weights, inference code, four evaluation benchmarks, annotation guidelines, and evaluation code.

From Attention to Semantics: Detecting and Mitigating Sound Event Hallucinations in Large Audio-Language Models2611.25789v1其他
展开摘要

Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) excel at diverse audio tasks, yet remain prone to hallucinating sound events absent from the input. Prior work links such hallucinations to insufficient audio attention. However, we find that real and hallucinated events can receive comparable audio attention mass while attending to different audio positions. To examine whether the attended representations support the generated events, we adapt LatentLens to retrieve neighboring contextualized text representations. The retrieved contexts reveal semantic support for real events, but a semantic mismatch between hallucinated events and their attended audio representations. Directly masking the corresponding audio regions can suppress hallucinated events at the cost of real content. We are motivated by these findings to introduce an explainable and training-free detect-then-mitigate framework for effectively hallucination detection and mitigation that aggregates semantic support and applies differential audio masking to protect regions associated with events predicted to be supported. Extensive experiments on three LALMs and two datasets demonstrate that our method significantly improves hallucination detection and hallucination mitigation performance.

UMelCodec: An Ultra-Low-Bitrate and Lightweight Mel-Domain Neural Speech Codec2611.25569v1语音合成
展开摘要

Ultra-low-bitrate speech coding is crucial for bandwidth-constrained communication and compact storage, but preserving intelligibility, naturalness, and fine-grained acoustic details at bitrates of only a few hundred bps remains challenging. Existing purely acoustic codecs generally operate at relatively high bitrates, whereas incorporating semantic representations can reduce the bitrate, but at the cost of increased complexity. Therefore, we propose UMelCodec, a purely acoustic neural speech codec operating in the mel domain at an ultra-low bitrate of 325 bps with a lightweight architecture. UMelCodec consists of two stages. In the first mel-domain coding stage, alias-free Snake-ConvNeXt (AF-SC) encoder-decoder backbones model temporal and harmonic structures, a factorized single-codebook vector quantizer (VQ) enables ultra-low-bitrate quantization, and a multi-scale mel-spectrogram discriminator (MSMD) improves mel-spectrogram reconstruction. In the second waveform synthesis stage, a lightweight modified discrete cosine transform (MDCT)-based neural vocoder predicts the MDCT spectrum from the reconstructed mel spectrogram and recovers the waveform through inverse MDCT. Experiments on LibriTTS dataset demonstrate that UMelCodec outperforms strong baselines across most objective reconstruction metrics while maintaining competitive subjective naturalness. In terms of model complexity and inference efficiency, UMelCodec has only 19.67 M parameters, requires 1.93 G MACs, and supports real-time inference on both GPU and CPU platforms.

DRVEXO-4B-Unified: Design and Evaluation of a Modular Speech-Vision System2611.25521v1语音合成
展开摘要

We present DRVEXO-4B-Unified, a modular multimodal assistant built around a 4B language backbone. A self-contained checkpoint combines text, image, and speech inputs with reference-conditioned speech output and structured interaction. A temporal residual connector adapts acoustic sequences to the language interface; explicit text boundaries support incremental speech synthesis; and three task-conditioned scoring heads rank supplied action candidates alongside generative tool calls. These interfaces connect conversation to application-defined choices while leaving action authorization to the application. The system obtains 74.22% on all 900 MMMU validation questions, exceeding the published validation scores of Gemma 3 4B IT and InternVL3.5-4B without test-time scaling. Strict prompt-level IFEval accuracy is 82.26% and MATH-500 accuracy is 89.40%, above the original Qwen3-4B non-thinking report's 81.2% and 84.8%, respectively. On NVIDIA GB10, the model achieves a speech-synthesis first-chunk latency of 390 ms. For concurrent text generation, a growth-aware admission policy completes a 128-answer batch at 594.41 output tokens/s, including filling and draining, without preemptions. We plan to release the model weights and accompanying code under Apache License 2.0, retaining applicable third-party terms.

Language-Oriented Semantic Modeling for Target Language Extraction2611.26317v1其他
展开摘要

Target Language Extraction (TLE) aims to isolate speech in a target language from multilingual mixtures. Existing approaches primarily rely on acoustic modeling with language identity labels, which provide only coarse categorical guidance. In this work, we propose a language-oriented semantic modeling framework that augments acoustic extraction with language-specific semantic representations. A pre-trained speech semantic model is adapted via language-aware prompt conditioning and parameter-efficient fine-tuning to generate target-language-focused semantic representations from the mixture. {These capture high-level semantic structure of the acoustics and they} are integrated into the extraction model to provide structured semantic guidance beyond identity labels. Experiments on the CommonVoiceMix dataset show that incorporating semantic representations consistently improves over acoustic-only baselines, achieving up to 1.16 dB SI-SDR gain.

When Should a Voice Agent Speak? End-of-Turn Detection in Turkish2611.25489v1其他
展开摘要

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used as judges and graders to replace human annotators, raising the question of when such replacement is statistically justified. To support such decisions, the Alternative Annotator Test (Alt-Test) was introduced for dense settings where all items are annotated by the same annotators. However, in many large-scale annotation settings, items are annotated by different subsets of annotators. AltRA-Test uses a bootstrap that resamples items, draws k human annotations for each item, and substitutes one of them with an LLM annotation. This produces paired all-human and mixed human-LLM panels. A replacement is supported when the inter-annotator agreement (IAA) of the mixed panel is statistically non-inferior to the IAA of the all-human panel, i.e., when the LLM preserves the signal that the human annotators share.
