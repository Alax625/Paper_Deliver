Teaching LLMs to Hear Who Spoke What: Metadata-Supervised Pretraining for Encoder-Free Speech-LLMs

作者：Mohan Shi, Ruchao Fan, Sunit Sivasankaran, Keqi Deng, Jinyu Li

语音识别评分 22speechacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出元数据监督预训练（MSP）方法，用于无编码器的语音大模型，通过说话人身份和情感等元数据训练，增强说话人区分和副语言能力。

解决了什么问题

基于编码器的语音大模型常使用预训练语音编码器，侧重语言内容但可能丢失对说话人区分和副语言理解至关重要的细粒度声学线索；无编码器模型直接将梅尔频谱特征映射到LLM输入空间，但缺乏系统性预训练策略。

核心方法

提出元数据监督预训练（MSP），利用说话人身份和情感等语音属性；引入说话人感知话语组合（SAUC）增强说话人区分；应用随机跨度掩码正则化预训练。

主要创新点

提出针对无编码器语音大模型的元数据监督预训练策略；设计SAUC和随机跨度掩码；在匹配训练数据条件下无编码器模型优于随机初始化的编码器模型，在有限元数据标注数据下与大规模预训练编码器模型竞争。

实验结论

在多说话人对话的联合ASR和说话人日志任务上，匹配数据条件下无编码器模型优于随机初始化的编码器模型；在有限元数据标注数据下与大规模预训练编码器模型竞争，并在若干设置中超越。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及。

适合谁读

语音识别、多模态大模型研究者。

和已有工作的关系

基于编码器的语音大模型（使用预训练语音编码器）、无编码器语音大模型（直接映射梅尔频谱）。

对研究的启发

无编码器架构通过元数据监督预训练可以学习声学细节，为构建原生多模态LLM提供思路。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因为展示了无编码器架构的潜力。

TOP 2

Beyond Decodability: Do Acoustic Factors Drive Predictions in Speech-Based Alzheimer's Assessment?

作者：Serli Kopar, Alkis Koudounas, Roshan P. Rane, Sam Gijsen, Paula A. Perez-Toro, Kerstin Ritter

自监督学习评分 17speechaudioacousticself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

研究声学因素是否会影响基于自监督学习的阿尔茨海默病语音评估模型的预测，结果显示受控声学干预会系统改变预测，强调干预测试的必要性。

解决了什么问题

基于语音的AD评估依赖预训练自监督学习模型，直接从原始音频学习声学表示，但尚不清楚记录因素（如噪声、混响）是仅被编码还是能系统改变预测。

核心方法

使用ADReSSo数据集和三种大型SSL骨干网络，对参与者语音、非语音和全录音音频施加受控噪声和混响干预；结合逐层线性解码、输入/表示空间干预和几何对齐分析区分声学可解码性与对AD预测的影响。

主要创新点

提出干预式鲁棒性测试方法，区分声学可解码性与预测影响；发现噪声虽在原始数据中无显著诊断组差异，但干预效果最强，且效果与分类器决策方向结构化相关。

实验结论

受控声学干预在三个SSL骨干上均改变AD预测；噪声产生最强干预效果；效果与决策方向结构化相关，在留出测试集上复现，且当表示空间干预方向反转时效果反转。高预测性能和测量声学因素无显著组间差异不足以保证鲁棒性。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及。

适合谁读

临床语音模型、自监督学习、鲁棒性研究者。

和已有工作的关系

基于预训练SSL模型的语音AD评估；使用线性解码探测声学信息的工作。

对研究的启发

应引入干预式鲁棒性测试作为临床语音模型的标准，而不仅依赖预测性能和组间差异。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因为该研究强调鲁棒性验证的重要性。

Q-SPT: Learnable Query-Based Compression for Low-Frame-Rate Speech Tokenization

作者：Jeeyoung Yun, Seohwan Yun, Sungwoong Kim

音频生成评分 14speechacoustictext-to-speechspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出Q-SPT，一种低帧率双流语音分词器，使用可学习的基于查询的压缩器分别处理语义和声学流，在相同帧率下实现最佳重建和下游性能。

解决了什么问题

神经语音编解码器作为语音语言模型的分词器，降低帧率可减少计算内存成本，但难以同时保留语言信息和声学细节；现有规则化压缩方法（平均池化或相似度合并）可能丢失语言信息或依赖固定阈值。

核心方法

Q-SPT采用双流结构，分离的、上下文感知的、可学习的基于查询的压缩器分别处理语义和声学表示；固定速率的查询分别注意语义和声学流作为键值源；自回归文本损失显式监督语义压缩器保留语言信息。

主要创新点

提出可学习的基于查询的压缩，替代规则化压缩；双流分离压缩实现流特定聚合；自回归文本损失保证语言信息。

实验结论

在相同帧率下，Q-SPT在评估的编解码器中实现最佳重建；在下游SLM中，实现最佳语音识别准确率和文本到语音感知质量，且可懂度具有竞争力。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及。

适合谁读

音频生成、语音语言模型、语音编解码研究者。

和已有工作的关系

神经语音编解码器；规则化压缩方法（平均池化、相似度合并）。

对研究的启发

可学习的基于查询的压缩可以替代规则方法，在低帧率下保留多流信息。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因为方法在效率和性能上取得平衡。

AudioJev: Direct Audio Decisions with Order-Calibrated Probabilities

作者：Sihan Lv, Zhen Li, Zhiqi Cao, Jinshan Zhang, Ying Li, Meng Xi, Jianwei Yin

其他评分 13speechaudio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

AudioJev通过共享全参数模型直接将波形、问题和选项映射到候选分布，引入顺序校准训练以保持概率在选项排列下不变，实现直接音频决策。

解决了什么问题

音频决策依赖转录无法保留的证据，且概率估计可能受答案选项顺序影响；需要直接音频接口并校准顺序偏差。

核心方法

AudioJev采用单一共享全参数模型，映射波形、问题和候选答案到分布；定义顺序校准为在保持含义的选项排列下概率不变；随机错排SKL训练：将每个问题与选项重排视图配对，监督两个答案，对齐分布后应用对称KL惩罚；推理时仅单次候选打分前向，无校准头或顺序集成。

主要创新点

顺序校准目标作用于候选含义而非呈现位置；随机错排SKL训练；推理无需额外部件。

实验结论

在三个训练种子上，AudioJev在完整MMAU/MMAR上平均准确率为68.88%/55.33%，相对单视图移除消融将随机顺序SKL降低43.8%/60.5%；同一模型处理意图、环境声音、音符属性、语音活动和对话转换。配对移除消融和多顺序评估同时度量预测准确率和概率稳定性。

可能局限

摘要中未明确提及。

适合谁读

音频理解、多模态决策、校准研究者。

和已有工作的关系

基于转录的音频决策；顺序敏感的选项处理。

对研究的启发

直接音频决策结合顺序校准可提升概率稳定性，为音频接口设计提供思路。

是否值得精读

值得精读，因为方法简单有效且提供代码和权重。

AVSD-Scenes: A Dataset for Audio-Visual Description of Urban Scenes

作者：Dhanunjaya Varma Devalraju, Arshdeep Singh, Mark D. Plumbley

其他评分 11audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

构建AVSD-Scenes数据集，包含12,291条城市音视频场景描述，由音频和视觉描述经LLM融合生成，实验表明多模态描述改善语义对齐和跨模态检索。

解决了什么问题

自然语言描述可提供丰富的音视频城市场景语义表示，但联合描述听觉和视觉信息的数据集有限。

核心方法

从TAU Urban Audio-Visual Scenes数据集出发，先使用Qwen2-Audio-7B和Qwen2.5-VL-7B分别生成音频和视觉描述，再用Qwen3-14B、Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct-2506和Gemma-3-27B-it融合产生多模态描述；使用语义对齐、跨模态检索、场景分类、LLM-as-a-judge和人工主观评估进行基准测试。

主要创新点

构建城市音视频场景描述数据集；利用多个LLM融合生成互补信息描述；验证描述的场景判别性。

实验结论

多模态描述相比模态特定描述改善语义对齐和跨模态检索性能，同时保持强场景判别信息；生成的描述在城市场景分类中达到最高94.5%准确率，结合音频、视觉和描述嵌入进一步提高到95.4%；即使移除场景标签提示，描述仍高度场景判别，表明捕获了音视频内容语义而非标签信息。

适合谁读

多模态学习、音视频场景理解、数据集构建研究者。

和已有工作的关系

模态特定描述生成（使用Qwen2-Audio、Qwen2.5-VL）；LLM融合多模态描述。

对研究的启发

利用LLM融合模态特定描述可生成高质量多模态语义描述，为场景理解提供新数据资源。

是否值得精读

Teaching LLMs to Hear Who Spoke What: Metadata-Supervised Pretraining for Encoder-Free Speech-LLMs2610.01695v1语音识别
展开摘要

Encoder-based speech large language models (Speech-LLMs) commonly employ pretrained speech encoders that prioritize linguistic content but may discard fine-grained acoustic cues essential for speaker discrimination and paralinguistic understanding. Encoder-free Speech-LLMs instead map Mel-spectrogram features directly into the LLM input space through lightweight embedding layers, enabling the LLM to learn from low-level acoustic features. However, systematic pretraining strategies for encoder-free Speech-LLMs remain underexplored, limiting their ability to compensate for the absence of large-scale pretrained speech encoders. We propose metadata-supervised pretraining (MSP), which leverages speech attributes such as speaker identity and emotion to develop speaker-discriminative and paralinguistic capabilities. We further introduce speaker-aware utterance composition (SAUC) to strengthen speaker discrimination and apply random span masking to regularize pretraining. We primarily evaluate our approach on joint ASR and speaker diarization in multi-speaker conversations, complemented by experiments on paralinguistic speech-understanding tasks. Under matched training-data conditions, our encoder-free model outperforms its randomly initialized encoder-based counterpart. With limited metadata-annotated data, it is competitive with models using speech encoders pretrained on substantially larger corpora, outperforming them in several settings. These results demonstrate the potential of encoder-free architectures for building native multimodal LLMs that acquire diverse speech capabilities.

Beyond Decodability: Do Acoustic Factors Drive Predictions in Speech-Based Alzheimer's Assessment?2610.01846v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Speech-based Alzheimer's disease (AD) assessments increasingly rely on pretrained self-supervised learning (SSL) models that learn acoustic representations directly from raw audio, exposing the model to recording factors. We ask whether such factors are merely encoded in SSL representations or can systematically alter predictions. Using ADReSSo and three large SSL backbones, we apply controlled noise and reverberation interventions to participant-speech-only, non-speech, and full-recording audio. We combine layer-wise linear decoding, input- and representation-space interventions, and geometric alignment analysis to distinguish acoustic decodability from influence on AD prediction. Our results show that controlled acoustic interventions alter AD predictions across all three SSL backbones. Noise, despite showing no significant diagnostic-group difference in the original data, produces the strongest intervention effects. Importantly, these effects are systematically structured relative to the classifier's decision direction, replicate on the held-out test set and reverse when the representation-space intervention direction is reversed. Together, these findings show that high predictive performance and the absence of a significant diagnostic-group difference in a measured acoustic factor are not sufficient for robustness. We argue that intervention-based robustness tests should become standard for trustworthy clinical speech models.

Q-SPT: Learnable Query-Based Compression for Low-Frame-Rate Speech Tokenization2610.01492v1音频生成
展开摘要

Neural speech codecs increasingly serve as tokenizers for speech language models (SLMs). Lowering the frame rate reduces the computational and memory costs of SLMs, but makes it difficult to preserve both linguistic information and acoustic detail. Existing approaches rely on rule-based compression: average pooling can discard linguistic information, whereas similarity-based merging uses a fixed threshold on adjacent-frame similarity and applies the resulting boundaries to the acoustic stream. We propose Q-SPT, a low-frame-rate dual-stream speech tokenizer with separate, context-aware, learnable query-based compressors specialized for semantic and acoustic representations. In particular, queries at a fixed rate independently attend to the semantic and acoustic streams as separate key-value sources, enabling stream-specific, context-aware aggregation through two separately learned compressors. In addition, an autoregressive text loss explicitly supervises the semantic compressor to preserve linguistic information. Experimental results show that Q-SPT achieves the best reconstruction among the evaluated codecs at the same frame rate. In downstream SLMs, it yields the best speech recognition accuracy and text-to-speech perceptual quality with competitive intelligibility.

AudioJev: Direct Audio Decisions with Order-Calibrated Probabilities2610.01293v1其他
展开摘要

Audio decisions often depend on evidence that a transcript does not preserve, while their probability estimates can depend on how answer options are ordered. AudioJev maps a waveform, question and supplied alternatives directly to a candidate distribution through one shared full-parameter model. We define order calibration as preserving an answer's probability under meaning-preserving option permutations. Random-derangement SKL training pairs each question with a reordered view in which every alternative changes position, supervises both answers, and aligns the two distributions before applying a symmetric KL penalty. Inference retains a single candidate-scoring forward, with no calibration head or order ensemble. Across three training seeds, AudioJev reaches 68.88%/55.33% mean accuracy on complete MMAU/MMAR and reduces random-order SKL by 43.8%/60.5% relative to the single-view removal ablation. The same model handles intent, environmental sound, note properties, speech activity and conversational transitions. Paired removal ablations and multi-order evaluation measure predictive accuracy and probability stability together, establishing a direct audio interface whose calibration objective acts on candidate meaning rather than presentation position. Inference code and model weights are available at https://github.com/SihanLv/AudioJev-Inference and https://huggingface.co/shlv/AudioJev.

AVSD-Scenes: A Dataset for Audio-Visual Description of Urban Scenes2610.01861v1其他
展开摘要

Natural language descriptions can provide rich semantic representations of audio-visual urban scenes, yet datasets that jointly describe both auditory and visual information remain limited. In this paper, we introduce AVSD-Scenes, a paired audio-visual scene description dataset for urban environments. The dataset contains 12,291 audio-visual scene descriptions generated from the TAU Urban Audio-Visual Scenes dataset. To construct the dataset, we first generate audio- and visual-based descriptions using Qwen2-Audio-7B and Qwen2.5-VL-7B, respectively. These modality-specific descriptions are then combined using large language models, namely Qwen3-14B, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct-2506, and Gemma-3-27B-it, to produce multimodal descriptions that capture complementary information from both modalities. We benchmark AVSD-Scenes using semantic alignment, cross-modal retrieval, scene classification, LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, and human subjective assessment. Results show that multimodal descriptions improve semantic alignment and cross-modal retrieval performance compared with modality-specific descriptions while preserving strong scene-discriminative information. The generated descriptions achieve up to 94.5% accuracy in urban scene classification, while combining audio, visual, and description embeddings further improves accuracy to 95.4%. Furthermore, the descriptions remain highly scene-discriminative even when scene labels are removed from the prompting instructions, indicating that they capture semantic information derived from the audio-visual content rather than merely reflecting label information.

SHAMS: An Audio-Grounded Pronunciation Benchmark for Levantine Arabic2610.01427v1语音识别
展开摘要

Levantine Arabic (LA) is spoken by tens of millions of people, creating a pressing need for shared benchmarks to evaluate LA speech-language technologies. Evaluating such technology is particularly challenging given LA's internal diversity and its opaque and non-standardized orthography. We present SHAMS (SHami Annotated Multi-dialect Speech), a benchmark comprising 1,300 utterances drawn from open audio corpora, balanced across five LA varieties (Urban and Rural Palestinian, and Urban Jordanian, Lebanese, and Syrian). Each utterance is represented across four aligned tiers: audio, unvocalized orthography, diacritized text, and phonetic transcription. This structure supports evaluation of various downstream tasks such as diacritization, grapheme-to-phoneme conversion, automatic speech recognition, and audio-to-phoneme, grounded in audio and stratified by variety. We benchmark open and proprietary models across these tasks to demonstrate the utility of this benchmark for measuring progress across LA. We release SHAMS at https://shams-nlp.github.io .

PADP: Perceptual Audio Data Perturbation for Probing Perception Awareness in Audio Quality Models2610.01405v1其他
