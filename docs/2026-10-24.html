Spark-Audio 1.0 Technical Report

作者：Chenyue Zhang, Cong Liu, Dingshu Tian, Dong Sun, Genshun Wan, Guanming Meng, Guolong Zhong, Hang Chen, Hankun Yang, Haoming Wang, Haoyang Wang, Hengquan Mei, Huaxin Wu, Jiahao Li, Jiahuan Fan, Jiamin Ma, Jianqing Gao, Jigang Tang, Jin Liu, Jingchen Li, Jinjie Fu, Kaixuan Guo, Kun Song, Li Xu, Li Zhang, Lijuan Liu, Liyan Luo, Ming Sheng, Mingen Zhao, Mingting Hu, Ningqian Wu, Pan Xu, Pei Xu, Qiangyu Tan, Rui Wang, Ruzi Li, Saier Jin, Shibo Xu, Shifu Xiong, Shihao Jiang, Tan Liu, Wei Wu, Wei Xu, Wenyu Wang, Xiangyu Jia, Xiao Li, Xiaowen Duan, Xiaoxue Guo, Xin Chen, Xinyi Liang, Xu Chen, Yanan Song, Yang Xu, Yexia Huang, Yibo Li, Yifan Xu, Yikang Liu, Yikun Li, Yiting Lin, Yi Fang, Yuxin Wang, Zan Feng, Zhenchuan Li, Zhuo Chen, Zichao Zhang, Ziwen Wang

语音识别评分 28speechaudiospeech recognitionspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

介绍Spark-Audio 1.0语音基础模型，基于国产算力训练，支持多语言多方言语音识别与翻译等任务，性能与Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash相当且部分超越。

解决了什么问题

在国产计算基础设施上构建高性能、多语言多方言支持的语音基础模型。

核心方法

采用0.65B密集音频编码器与30B-A3B MoE大语言模型耦合；音频编码器用表示增强BEST-RQ预训练，再经多任务监督训练；音频-LLM分阶段预训练，后训练结合监督微调与on-policy蒸馏。

主要创新点

首次在国产硬件（华为昇腾910B和海光BW1000）上成功训练语音基础模型；使用表示增强BEST-RQ；结合on-policy蒸馏；用13M小时音频数据取得竞争力性能。

实验结论

在主流音频和文本基准及内部测试集上，与Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash相比，多数任务性能相当，多语言语音识别、口语对话和真实应用场景表现更优，接近更大闭源模型；但指令跟随和复杂音频推理仍有不足。

可能局限

指令跟随和复杂音频推理方面仍有改进空间。

适合谁读

语音识别、语音翻译、音频理解、语音对话及国产AI算力方向的研究人员与工程师。

和已有工作的关系

与Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash及更大闭源模型进行了性能比较。

对研究的启发

证明了国产算力训练大语音模型的可行性；MoE LLM与音频编码器结合的架构及on-policy蒸馏后训练策略可借鉴。

是否值得精读

是，该工作展示了国产技术路线构建语音基础模型的有效性，并在多语言识别等任务上取得竞争力。

Lip-Focused Audio-Visual Consistency Detects Speech Content Manipulation beyond Coarse Synchronization

作者：Jian Zong, Chenghan Lin, Meng Liu, Zhao Xu, Junyu Wang, Siyuan Zhang, Peiyuan Jiang, Longbiao Wang, Jianwu Dang

语音识别评分 22speechaudioacousticspeakerspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出唇部聚焦的音频-视觉一致性检测器C-MIND，在SyncSpeech基准上有效检测语音内容操纵，但对未见生成器泛化有限。

解决了什么问题

检测通过替换语音轨道或重生成嘴部运动实现的语音内容操纵，传统基于纹理伪影或粗同步的方法难以应对现代重说话模型。

核心方法

构建SyncSpeech基准（含真实视频、音频拼接cheapfakes和Wav2Lip攻击）；C-MIND检测器在帧级别对齐音频与嘴部运动表示，建模一致与不一致，无需转录、ASR特征或未操纵参考。

主要创新点

提出唇部聚焦的检测思路，验证声学动态与嘴部运动局部关系受扰动的假设；引入SyncSpeech基准；C-MIND无需额外参考。

实验结论

在SyncSpeech上，预训练SyncLip-C达到95.813%场景平均准确率和0.9943 AUC，优于句子级融合、全脸输入和SyncNet基线；在FakeAVCeleb未见说话人子集上正确分类278/280样本，但对未见Video-Retalking攻击准确率降至82.775%。

可能局限

对未见生成器（如Video-Retalking）泛化能力有限，准确率明显下降。

适合谁读

音频取证、深度伪造检测、多媒体安全及语音识别相关研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

与SyncNet基线、句子级融合和全脸输入方法对比，并在FakeAVCeleb和Video-Retalking攻击上测试。

对研究的启发

利用唇部聚焦的帧级音视频一致性可检测操纵，无需转录或参考；提出的SyncSpeech基准可为后续研究提供测试平台。

是否值得精读

是，针对语音内容操纵检测提出新基准和有效检测器，在音频取证方向有参考价值。

Measuring the Verifier's Floor: How Much Apparent Rare-Word Text-to-Speech Error Is ASR Error?

作者：Kirill Borodin, Vasilii Kudryavtsev, Nikita Vasiliev, Grach Mkrtchian

语音合成评分 14speechtext-to-speechspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

测量ASR在TTS评估中对罕见词的识别错误，发现ASR漏词导致TTS错误率虚高，多识别器交叉检查可部分缓解但罕见词仍需人工听力测试。

解决了什么问题

TTS评估中ASR错误被计入TTS错误，导致评估偏差，尤其对罕见词影响严重。

核心方法

在LibriSpeech和FLEURS人类录音上测量多个ASR对罕见词的错误率；使用两个额外识别器家族交叉检查每个词；对剩余错误进行裁决分析。

主要创新点

量化ASR错误对TTS评估的影响；提出多识别器交叉检查方法降低表观TTS错误；揭示忽略人类语音中识别器漏词的偏差。

实验结论

识别器在人类语音中漏掉33-67%非常见词，三个识别器全漏22-42%；多识别器交叉检查将表观TTS错误从10-13%降至3-5%；忽略漏词存在偏差，其他识别器发现TTS错误频率更高（4-6% vs 0.2-0.3%）；剩余约一半裁决错误可能是拼写变体，需听力测试。

可能局限

非常见词仍是最难处理的类别；剩余裁决错误可能包含拼写变体，需人工听力测试确认。

适合谁读

TTS评估、ASR、语音合成与识别评估方向的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

基于LibriSpeech和FLEURS数据集，使用多个ASR识别器家族进行交叉验证。

对研究的启发

评估TTS时应考虑ASR错误，采用多识别器交叉验证，并对罕见词进行人工听力测试以确保可靠性。

是否值得精读

是，揭示了TTS评估中系统性偏差，对改进评估流程有实际指导意义。

CAVC: Convergence-Adaptive Audio-Visual Cache for Modality-Asymmetric Diffusion Generation

作者：Lifan Jiang, Yibo Zhao, Senlin cheng, Bolei Xu, Xiaobo Li, Can Ye, Dehai Zhao, Deng Cai, Boxi Wu

其他评分 13audio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出无训练模态不对称缓存框架CAVC，在扩散音视频生成中缓存音频计算，实现1.10-1.35倍加速并保持质量。

解决了什么问题

扩散音视频生成中均匀联合去噪导致不必要的计算开销，推理效率低。

核心方法

利用模态不对称可恢复性，采用三明治结构计算分配：中期缓存音频去噪输出，绕过音频去噪和跨模态注意力，视频分支继续独立时空细化，最后恢复少数联合步骤。

主要创新点

发现模态不对称可恢复性；提出无训练缓存框架；三明治结构选择性缓存音频计算。

实验结论

在多个公共基准上，CAVC实现1.10-1.35倍时钟加速，改善视觉动态和时序统计，保持竞争性音频保真度和音视频对齐。

可能局限

加速比相对有限（1.10-1.35倍）。

适合谁读

音视频扩散模型、生成模型加速及多媒体生成方向的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

与现有联合去噪的扩散音视频生成方法进行比较。

对研究的启发

利用模态不对称性进行选择性缓存，为无训练推理加速提供了新思路。

是否值得精读

是，无训练且效果明显的加速方法，对扩散生成模型部署有实用价值。

MQAScore: Fine-Grained Text-Music Alignment Evaluation via Large Audio Language Model

作者：Wei-Jaw Lee, Chun-Ping Wang, Xuanjun Chen, Yi-Hsuan Yang

解决了什么问题

实验结论

概念级评分改进了问答和基于相似性的评估器；MQAScore可作为全局相似性的更可靠和可解释的细粒度补充。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音乐生成、文本-音乐对齐评估及多模态评估方向的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

与全局相似性评估方法以及问答和基于相似性的评估器进行比较。

对研究的启发

利用LALM进行细粒度概念验证，并设计敏感性-不变性比率评估可靠性，为多模态评估提供新范式。

Spark-Audio 1.0 Technical Report2610.31292v1语音识别
展开摘要

This paper introduces Spark-Audio 1.0, a speech foundation model trained on domestically produced computing infrastructure. The model accepts text and speech inputs and generates text outputs. It supports multilingual (99 languages) and multi-dialect (202 dialects) speech recognition, speech translation, audio perception and understanding, and both text- and speech-based dialogue, marking a shift from merely ``hearing clearly'' to genuinely ``understanding'' speech. Architecturally, Spark-Audio 1.0 couples a 0.65B-parameter dense audio encoder with a 30B-A3B Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) large language model (LLM). The audio encoder is pretrained with representation-enhanced BEST-RQ and then refined through multi-task supervised training. The audio--LLM is trained through staged pretraining, followed by post-training that combines supervised fine-tuning with on-policy distillation. In total, the model is trained on 13 million hours of audio data together with large-scale text data. We evaluate Spark-Audio 1.0 on mainstream audio and text benchmarks, as well as on in-house test sets drawn from real-world application scenarios. Despite using substantially less audio training data than Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash, Spark-Audio 1.0 achieves comparable performance on most audio and text tasks, outperforms it in multilingual speech recognition, spoken dialogue, and real-world application scenarios, and approaches the performance of larger closed-source models. Nevertheless, there remains room for improvement in instruction following and complex audio reasoning. Finally, the successful training of Spark-Audio 1.0 on domestic hardware, including Huawei Ascend 910B and Hygon BW1000 chips, demonstrates the feasibility of a domestic technical pathway for building speech foundation models.

Lip-Focused Audio-Visual Consistency Detects Speech Content Manipulation beyond Coarse Synchronization2610.30872v1语音识别
展开摘要

Speech-content manipulation changes what a visible person appears to say by replacing the speech track and, in stronger attacks, regenerating mouth motion to match the forged utterance. Such attacks are difficult to expose with texture artifacts or coarse lip-sync scores because modern retalking models explicitly optimize audio-visual synchrony. We test the narrower hypothesis that speech replacement still perturbs the local relationship between acoustic dynamics and mouth motion after speaker identity or coarse synchrony is controlled. To isolate this signal, we introduce SyncSpeech, an LRS3-derived benchmark containing real videos, audio-splicing cheapfakes and Wav2Lip retalking attacks under same-speaker and different-speaker replacement. We then introduce C-MIND, a lip-focused detector that aligns audio and mouth-motion representations at frame level and models their agreement and disagreement without transcripts, automatic speech recognition features or an unmanipulated reference. On SyncSpeech, pretrained SyncLip-C achieves 95.813% scenario-averaged accuracy and 0.9943 AUC, outperforming sentence-level fusion, full-face inputs and a reproduced SyncNet baseline under the reported protocol. When trained on the corresponding FakeAVCeleb training split, C-MIND correctly classifies 278 of 280 samples in the selected unseen-speaker test subset, whereas accuracy falls to 82.775% on unseen Video-Retalking attacks. These results identify lip-focused speech-articulation consistency as a useful but generator-sensitive forensic signal within the evaluated settings.

Measuring the Verifier's Floor: How Much Apparent Rare-Word Text-to-Speech Error Is ASR Error?2610.31470v1语音合成
展开摘要

Text-to-speech (TTS) is usually evaluated by transcribing its output with an automatic speech recognition (ASR) system, so the word error rate also counts recognizer errors. We measure these errors on human recordings of the test sentences. On LibriSpeech and FLEURS, the evaluation recognizer misses 33-67% of very rare words even in human speech, and all three recognizers miss 22-42%. Checking every word with two more recognizer families lowers the apparent TTS error on words that at least one recognizer hears two- to three-fold (very rare LibriSpeech words: from 10-13% to 3-5%), yet very rare words remain the hardest. Ignoring words the recognizer misses in human speech is biased: the other recognizers find TTS errors far more often on these words (4-6% vs. 0.2-0.3%) and overturn about half of the errors this mask keeps. As half of the remaining adjudicated errors may be spelling variants, a listening test is needed.

CAVC: Convergence-Adaptive Audio-Visual Cache for Modality-Asymmetric Diffusion Generation2610.30854v1其他
展开摘要

Diffusion-based audio-visual generation methods typically perform joint denoising of audio and video latents throughout the entire sampling trajectory, repeatedly executing modality-specific denoising branches and cross-modal attention at every timestep. However, we observe that such uniform joint computation is not always necessary. The key is a modality-asymmetric recoverability: audio-side drift introduced by mid-stage caching can be substantially reduced by only a few terminal joint denoising steps, whereas video-side degradation caused by caching leads to persistent loss of motion and temporal details. This asymmetry suggests that inference acceleration should selectively cache recoverable audio-side computation while preserving full video refinement. Motivated by this observation, we propose \textbf{Convergence-Adaptive Audio-Visual Cache (CAVC)}, a training-free modality-asymmetric caching framework. CAVC adopts a sandwich-structured computation allocation: after detecting that audio denoiser outputs enter a cache-tolerant regime, it caches and reuses the audio denoiser output to bypass audio denoising and cross-modal attention in the middle stage, while the video branch continues independent spatio-temporal refinement; a few joint steps are then restored at the end to reduce accumulated cross-modal drift and improve synchronization. Experiments across multiple public benchmarks demonstrate that CAVC achieves 1.10--1.35x wall-clock acceleration, improves several visual dynamics and temporal statistics, and maintains competitive audio fidelity and audio-visual alignment.

MQAScore: Fine-Grained Text-Music Alignment Evaluation via Large Audio Language Model2610.31083v1其他
展开摘要

Text-to-music alignment is commonly evaluated by the global similarity between text and audio embeddings, which collapses all described attributes into a single score and obscures which ones are missing. We instead introduce MQAScore, which verifies each atomic musical concept along five clip-level dimensions (genre, instrument, mood/theme, vocal, and usage) by the probability that a large audio language model (LALM) answers affirmatively to a binary question. To assess evaluator reliability, we construct verification labels from presence/absence annotations and LLM-guided concept substitutions, and propose the Sensitivity-Invariance Ratio, which measures how selectively an evaluator responds to meaning-changing substitutions. Experiments show that concept-level scoring improves both question-answering and similarity-based evaluators, and MQAScore can be a more reliable and interpretable fine-grained complement to global similarity for evaluating text-to-music alignment.

When Do Self-Supervised Representations Help Speech Restoration?2610.30869v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Self-supervised learning (SSL) representations are widely used in speech restoration (SR), and the prevailing trend is SSL feature cleansing: fine-tuning the SSL to recover clean features from degraded speech, then conditioning the restoration model on them. However, the representational capability of SSL when encoding degraded speech has been underexplored, and we ask whether cleansing always benefits SR training. Across SSL encoders and perceptual quality proxies, we discover perceptual correlation collapse---under severe degradation, cleansing degraded SSL features toward clean ones misaligns with the SR objective.
