SimPOTrans: Self-Adaptation for Offline-to-Simultaneous Speech Translation via Reinforcement Learning

作者：Haotian Tan, Hiroki Ouchi, Sakriani Sakti

语音翻译评分 13speechspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

SimPOTrans 通过两阶段强化学习后训练使离线语音翻译模型自适应到对话式同时翻译，无需合成轨迹或显式延迟优化。

解决了什么问题

现有对话式同时语音翻译方法依赖合成读写轨迹或联合优化翻译质量与延迟，可能提供次优训练信号或导致优化不稳定。

核心方法

提出强化学习框架 SimPOTrans，采用两阶段 RL 后训练，在给定部分语音输入时奖励离线模型产生有利于同时场景的翻译行为。

主要创新点

无需合成读写轨迹或显式延迟优化；推理时通过调整语音输入块大小实现灵活的质量-延迟控制，且无需外部决策策略。

实验结论

在 ACL 60/60 和 MCIF 基准、三个语言方向（英→德、英→中、英→日）上，SimPOTrans 优于多个强基线，实现了更好的质量-延迟权衡。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

从事语音翻译、强化学习和大语言模型应用的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

摘要提及使用大语言模型进行对话式同时语音翻译的方法，以及采用合成读写轨迹或联合优化质量与延迟的现有方案。

对研究的启发

利用强化学习后训练将离线模型转换为低延迟在线模型，避免设计显式训练信号，可启发其他序列生成任务的低延迟适应。

ImageTTS: Repurposing Pretrained Image Diffusion Transformers for Speech Synthesis

作者：Hanchen Pei, Shujie Liu, Yayue Deng, Fangyun Wei, Yanqing Liu, Jianwei Yu, Gongping Huang

语音合成评分 13speechspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

ImageTTS 通过重用图像扩散模型 FLUX.2 的冻结 VAE 和预训练 DiT 初始化，将视觉表征和生成先验迁移到语音合成。

解决了什么问题

预训练图像生成模型已成功重用于视觉任务，但其表征和生成先验能否跨模态迁移到语音合成尚未解决。

核心方法

将语音表示为伪 RGB log-mel 谱图，使用 FLUX.2 的冻结图像 VAE 编码，并仅适应预训练 DiT，采用条件隐式流匹配进行训练。

主要创新点

分离了两种视觉到语音转移：通过图像 VAE 的表征转移和通过 DiT 初始化的生成先验转移；冻结 VAE 保留可懂度和说话人身份，DiT 初始化加速收敛并改善语言准确性。

实验结论

冻结图像 VAE 在 0.99 STOI 和 0.95 说话人相似度下保留可懂度和身份；DiT 初始化将 WER 从 4.2% 降至 2.7%（100k 匹配更新）；扩展至 100K 小时数据后，在 Seed-TTS test-en 上 WER 1.12%，CapSpeech 上 Style-ACC 0.66，优于语音原生基线。

SimPOTrans: Self-Adaptation for Offline-to-Simultaneous Speech Translation via Reinforcement Learning2610.35042v1语音翻译
展开摘要

Simultaneous speech translation (SimulST) aims to translate ongoing speech with low latency while preserving translation quality. Recent studies have adopted large language models (LLMs) for SimulST and formulated it as a multi-turn dialogue task for efficient generation, achieving state-of-the-art performance. However, existing dialogue-style SimulST approaches typically rely on specialized training schemes to acquire simultaneous translation behavior, either using synthesized read/write trajectories, which may provide suboptimal training signals, or jointly optimizing translation quality and latency, whose competing objectives may cause optimization instability. In this paper, we introduce SimPOTrans, a reinforcement learning (RL) framework that enables offline speech translation models to self-adapt to dialogue-style SimulST. Through a simple two-stage RL post-training pipeline, SimPOTrans rewards an offline model for exhibiting translation behaviors beneficial to simultaneous scenarios when given partial speech inputs, thereby enabling offline-to-simultaneous self-adaptation without synthesized read/write trajectories or explicit latency optimization. At inference time, SimPOTrans requires no external decision policy and enables flexible quality--latency control by simply adjusting the chunk size of speech input. Experiments on the ACL 60/60 and MCIF benchmarks across three language directions (En$\rightarrow$De, En$\rightarrow$Zh, and En$\rightarrow$Ja) demonstrate that SimPOTrans outperforms several strong baselines, achieving a superior quality--latency trade-off.

ImageTTS: Repurposing Pretrained Image Diffusion Transformers for Speech Synthesis2610.34807v1语音合成
展开摘要

Pretrained image-generation models have been successfully repurposed for various downstream visual tasks. Whether their learned representations and generative priors can transfer beyond vision to other modalities remains largely unresolved, particularly for speech synthesis, which requires linguistic fidelity and preservation of speaker identity from a short reference. We present ImageTTS, which repurposes FLUX.2 for speech synthesis by encoding pseudo-RGB log-mel spectrograms with its frozen image VAE and adapting only its pretrained Diffusion Transformer (DiT) using conditional latent flow matching. This formulation disentangles two forms of vision-to-speech transfer: representation transfer through the image VAE and generative-prior transfer through DiT initialization. The frozen image VAE largely preserves intelligibility and speaker identity with 0.99 STOI and 0.95 speaker similarity, while image-pretrained DiT initialization accelerates convergence and improves linguistic accuracy, reducing WER from 4.2\% to 2.7\% at 100k matched updates relative to random initialization. The same framework supports both zero-shot and instruction-guided TTS and, when scaled to 100K hours of speech data, achieves 1.12\% WER on Seed-TTS test-en and a Style-ACC of 0.66 on CapSpeech, outperforming speech-native baselines trained on comparable data across both tasks. These results suggest that pretrained image models can transfer beyond architectural reuse, supplying useful latent representations and generative priors for intelligible and controllable speech synthesis.

S2SND-v2: Unified Online and Offline Speaker Diarization with Speaker-Adapted w2v-BERT 2.02610.34707v1说话人
展开摘要

This paper presents S2SND-v2, a unified online and offline speaker diarization framework built on S2SND that improves acoustic feature extraction and the use of the resulting representations. For acoustic feature extraction, we introduce a speaker-adapted w2v-BERT 2.0 front end and compare full and pruned construction routes. To better use these features, overlap-aware activity-query masking improves speaker embedding extraction under overlap, while late-speaker training aligns unknown-speaker training with online inference conditions. Combining these improvements, the pruned system achieves competitive average diarization error rates of 14.52% online and 12.03% offline across eight test sets.

TIMUSE: Text-Initialized Masked Semantic Token Modeling for Universal Speech Enhancement2610.34814v1语音增强
展开摘要

Discrete-token-based universal speech enhancement (USE) enables strong sequence modeling but often suffers from a quality--fidelity trade-off, where perceptually plausible outputs may alter linguistic content or speaker characteristics. We present TIMUSE, a two-stage USE framework combining text-initialized masked semantic restoration with acoustic reconstruction. A pretrained diffusion language model (DLM) is adapted with LoRA to recover clean semantic tokens using masked generative modeling, while a conditional flow matching model reconstructs clean 24\,kHz speech from the recovered semantic tokens and original degraded speech. Both system-level and controlled comparisons show that TIMUSE achieves a better balance between perceptual quality and fidelity than existing discrete USE models. Ablations further show that text DLM initialization brings a targeted gain under severe degradations.

A Robust, Explainable, and Efficient Deep Metric for Speech and General Audio Quality2610.35810v1其他
展开摘要

We present a robust Speech and Audio Quality Metric (SAQM) that merges features of traditional quality metrics with a CNN trained on a large amount of data using contrastive learning methods. To overcome the problem of limited amounts of human-labeled training data, we propose two pretraining strategies relying on pseudo-labels. One uses an established objective metric PEAQ-2f (based on ITU-R BS.1387) as surrogate loss, the other is based on contrasting a wide range of coding modes by a rule-of-thumb ordering based on expected perceived quality. As a key novelty, our approach lets us generate saliency maps by propagating gradients to identify which feature has the greatest impact on the final metric outcome and enables our system to provide insights regarding the type and extent of distortions present in the tested audio signal. The resulting (SAQM) operates at low computational complexity, performs comparably to (SOTA) metrics on speech while still performing well on general audio content.

DecisionTraceBench: Tracking Decision-Critical Information in Multi-Party Spoken Conversations2610.34746v1其他
展开摘要

In high-stakes domains such as finance, critical information evolves through multi-party interaction. Recovering these changes and their sources is fundamental to reliable speech understanding. Existing speech benchmarks focus on transcription, local understanding, and turn-level state prediction, leaving joint decision evolution across speakers insufficiently evaluated. We introduce DecisionTraceBench, a speech state-tracking benchmark for Decision-Critical Information State Tracking (DCIST). It jointly evaluates decision event sequences, speaker attribution, scope, and temporal evidence, using trajectory-level F1 to measure complete event-chain recovery. Our dataset comprises Chinese financial dialogues involving two to four speakers, with 100.39 hours of synthetic audio and 8.61 hours of human-performed recordings. On a fixed cohort of human-performed recordings, five cascaded systems and two direct-audio models achieve DCIST scores of 5.73% to 21.35%. Ablations with speaker-labeled reference scripts and no temporal localization requirements yield a maximum score of only 38.72%. Audio perturbation experiments further show that overall transcription error rates do not adequately reflect complete trajectory recovery. These findings reveal limitations in jointly understanding information provenance and state evolution, providing a benchmark for evaluating decision-process understanding and decision traceability in high-stakes speech applications.

The Voice on the Line2610.35272v1其他
展开摘要

A transcriber listening through one microphone also hears everyone near it. We train causal voice isolators: masking transformers of 140M, 243M and 374M parameters, 20 ms of latency, enrolling the caller from the call itself. Short evaluation windows hide two failures that real calls expose: forgetting the caller when another voice talks alone for a while, and damaging a caller who is alone. Long calls with turns, memory beyond the attention window, real headset crosstalk and drifting caller levels address both, but each trades one against the other. WavLM-Large's own weights, made causal and trained with the model, move the trade: M with them (555M parameters) lowers the error on our development sets from 11.44% to 10.55%; a student trained from scratch to imitate WavLM does not, and w2v-BERT 2.0 and Whisper's encoder, pre-trained on far more audio, help less or not at all. A lone caller loses most at the start of a recording: no training call opened with someone already talking. Training on windows cut from longer calls lowers the error on clean development calls from 3.92% to 2.96%; this is our release. Live, the release makes 16.13% word errors on 60 s AMI windows, against 23.66% for VoxLock, the strongest open isolator, and 10.19% on its own AMI windows, against 11.71%; it is level on Krisp's benchmark (5.74% against 5.63%), and a caller who is alone still loses more words than with VoxLock (5.58% against 2.92% on clean test calls). Distilled into 22.1M parameters without WavLM, it runs live on one CPU thread inside a LiveKit agent, at 33% of a core per call in 20 ms frames, with 10.66% error on our development sets (the release 10.49%). We open-source the release and this CPU model, with a LiveKit plugin, at https://huggingface.co/collections/Scicom-intl/voice-isolator.

Correspondence Is Not Transfer: A Pairing-Controlled Audit of Clinical Audio-Metadata Alignment2610.35747v1其他
展开摘要

Clinical audio models increasingly align recordings with participant metadata or metadata-derived text. This supervision is useful but ambiguous: the paired target can contain symptoms, demographics, missingness, and cohort structure, not just disease information. We study this ambiguity by making the audio-metadata pairing itself the intervention. Our Pairing-Controlled Transfer Audit trains matched contrastive trajectories under correct pairing, global shuffling, disease-label-preserving shuffling, and label-and-recorded-sex-preserving shuffling. Each endpoint is then frozen and tested with three readouts: profile correspondence, attribute decodability, and matched disease prediction. On UKCOVID, correct improves retrieval and sex decodability over label-only shuffling, showing that the pairing intervention worked. The sex-probe contrast is largely absorbed by the sex-preserving control, while smaller retrieval differences remain. Disease-transfer contrasts, however, remain uncertain. After UKCOVID discovery fixed the pipeline, CODA TB, Cambridge COVID-19 Sounds, and Coswara were rerun as a locked external panel with the same arms, backbones, matching, readout, calibration, primary contrast, and primary metric. The external panel preserves the descriptive separation without proving a universal null. Successful profile correspondence and improved disease transfer are therefore different empirical claims.

Geometric Consistency Based Label-Free Validation for Checkpoint Selection in Audio Active Learning2610.35329v1其他
展开摘要

Audio active learning (AL) trains a classifier at low annotation cost by labeling only an informative fraction of an unlabeled collection over successive rounds. Each round picks one of the checkpoints saved during training. Mainstream AL protocols do so with an assumed validation set, but in practice its labels also count toward the annotation cost, shrinking the advantage of an already small annotation budget.
