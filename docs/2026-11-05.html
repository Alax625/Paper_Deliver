VIPBench: A Human-Aligned Benchmark for Voice Identity Perception in the Age of Voice Cloning

作者：Xudong Tang, Matthew Groh

语音合成评分 29speechvoicespeakertext-to-speechself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

引入VIPBench基准，包含人类对真实、克隆和混合语音的同异判断，用于评估说话人表示模型与人类声音身份感知的一致性。

解决了什么问题

现有说话人识别基准只评估模型恢复说话人标签的准确性，未考虑人类如何感知声音身份，尤其在语音克隆时代，人类感知与标签可能不一致。

核心方法

构建VIPBench数据集，包含来自1290名听众对9800对录音的124876个判断，涵盖真实、克隆和混合语音；定义四个任务评估模型相似度得分与听众判断的一致性；评估11个公开语音模型。

主要创新点

首次提出面向人类声音身份感知的基准，专门针对语音克隆场景；包含混合语音；将模型与人类感知对齐，而非仅标签。

实验结论

在41%的克隆语音对中，听众判断克隆与同一说话人的真实录音为不同人；最佳模型解释61%的可解释方差；用说话人标签训练的模型比自监督模型更一致，但说话人标签错误率12%的模型与低错误率模型对齐程度相当。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

研究语音合成、说话人识别、声音身份感知、人机交互以及语音克隆伦理的学者和开发者。

和已有工作的关系

说话人识别基准、语音克隆、自监督语音模型、声音相似度度量等。

对研究的启发

提示可在听众判断上直接训练说话人模型以更好地对齐人类感知，并考虑在克隆和混合语音场景下评估模型。

是否值得精读

摘要表明这是首个针对人类声音身份感知的基准，包含大规模人类判断，对语音克隆时代的身份验证和安全有重要意义。

Holistic Parallel Supervision for Expressive Speech to Speech Translation

作者：Hao Zhang, Ismail Rasim Ulgen, Bismarck Bamfo Odoom, Rui Liu, Zihan Zhang, Berrak Sisman, Philipp Koehn

语音翻译评分 19speechvoicespeakerspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一种结合专业配音和多语言语音转换的整体并行监督构建策略，并开发端到端DirectS2ST系统，在表达性语音到语音翻译上取得全面改进。

解决了什么问题

现有S2ST数据集很少在内容、韵律、时序和说话人身份上联合对齐源和目标语音，导致系统依赖间接条件或转移机制，表达性不足。

核心方法

构建训练数据：利用专业配音获得内容、韵律和时序的对应，再用多语言语音转换减少说话人不匹配；采用整体并行监督训练；开发DirectS2ST，直接从源语音预测完整目标语音表示。

主要创新点

提出整体并行监督的概念，提供联合对齐的监督信号；DirectS2ST端到端预测目标语音表示，避免间接条件；首次系统研究表达性S2ST的整体并行监督。

实验结论

在英语-德语和英语-西班牙语上，整体并行监督改善所有评估系统的表达实现；DirectS2ST在内容、韵律、时序、说话人相似度和人工评分表达性上增益最大。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

语音翻译、语音合成、表达性语音处理的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

表达性S2ST、语音转换、端到端语音翻译、韵律建模等。

对研究的启发

可以通过构建更全面的对齐数据来提升表达性翻译，并鼓励端到端方法直接预测完整语音表示。

是否值得精读

首次系统研究整体并行监督，提出有效的数据构建和模型方法，对提升S2ST自然度有实际价值。

LibriSpeech-Real-QA: A Benchmark for Streaming Question Answering

作者：Zewen Zhan, Hui Lu, Xueyuan Chen, Huimeng Wang, Yikai Huang, Xixin Wu, Shiyin Kang, Bin Xia, Zhiyong Wu

其他评分 16speechaudio查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出LibriSpeech-Real-QA基准，用于评估语音语言模型在流式音频上的实时问答能力。

解决了什么问题

大多数语音大语言模型离线处理，无法满足实时流式交互需求，缺少相应基准和评估协议。

核心方法

从LibriSpeech构建不同类型的实时问答对，要求模型基于接收到的音频块回答问题（如计数特定词出现次数）；评估商业实时模型，并探索使预训练SLM执行流式问答的建模设计，建立基线。

主要创新点

提出首个面向实时语音交互的问答基准；设计基于流式音频的问答任务；演示了预训练SLM可适配此任务。

实验结论

流行的实时商业模型仍倾向于在语音轮次结束后才响应；通过探索建模设计，可以启用预训练SLM执行此任务，并建立基线。

Speaker-Consistent Grounding for Confusion-Resilient LLM-Based Target Speech Extraction

作者：Hanyu Meng, Hao Shi, Zhanhong He, Qiquan Zhang, Yang Xiao, Sungwoo Cho, Haowei Lou, Haizhou Li

其他评分 14speechaudioacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出修复后接地的框架，通过条件多样性候选选择和码空间接地，提高LLM-based目标说话人提取的可靠性。

解决了什么问题

确定性TSE可能提取干扰说话人，生成式TSE易出现内容幻觉和说话人漂移；用确定性证据接地生成式模型时，若证据错误会强化错误。

核心方法

提出repair-before-grounding框架：训练免证据修复阶段，使用CDCS从互补的冻结TSE输出中选择目标说话人一致证据，然后用CSG约束LLM-based TSE解码。

主要创新点

在接地前修复证据身份，避免强化错误；CDCS训练免修复，利用互补输出；CSG在码空间接地，保持LLM解码。

实验结论

在Libri2Mix上，CDCS将干净混合上的错误说话人提取错误从6.87%降到0.75%，噪声下从18.95%降到10.46%；CSG进一步降低噪声内容和声学切换率；生成式TSE仍保留感知质量优势。

LiveFoley: Dynamic Real-Time Video-to-Audio Generation for Immersive Interactive Worlds

作者：Yusheng Dai, Vladimir Iashin, Kangdi Wang, Yuxuan Jiang, Ilpo Viertola, Weiqiang Wang, Qiuhong Ke, Min Zhao, Jianfei Cai

音频生成评分 12audioaudio generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出LiveFoley，一种流式因果自回归扩散架构，通过Trailing Sliding Window和LocDiT变体实现具有不可察觉延迟的高质量实时视频到音频生成。

解决了什么问题

现有流式V2A方法受限于实时约束，视觉前端感受野缩小导致视听对齐差，因果自回归扩散后端孤立去噪损害音频质量。

核心方法

视觉前端引入Trailing Sliding Window将非因果段级编码器转为流式模块，保持对齐并降低前瞻；音频后端探索扩大LocDiT去噪窗口，History LocDiT添加过去上下文，Future-Warmup LocDiT近似未来条件；提出Unified Varhop Framework适应不同延迟预算。

主要创新点

Trailing Sliding Window无需重训练即可实现流式；Unified Varhop Framework灵活配置；Future-Warmup打破自回归死锁；整体架构在不可察觉延迟下匹配离线模型。

实验结论

大量实验表明LiveFoley在时间对齐上匹配离线模型，并在生成质量上领先流式系统。

VIPBench: A Human-Aligned Benchmark for Voice Identity Perception in the Age of Voice Cloning2611.05638v1语音合成
展开摘要

Speaker-recognition benchmarks test how accurately a model recovers the speaker label of a recording, treating voice identity as a property of the person who produced it. However, these benchmarks do not evaluate how humans perceive voice identity. Whether a listener hears two voices as the same person has consequences for intellectual property rights, cybersecurity, and everyday life. We introduce the Voice Identity Perception benchmark (VIPBench), which contains 124,876 same-or-different judgments from 1,290 listeners on 9,800 recording pairs from 100 public figures. The pairs include real recordings, voice clones generated by a text-to-speech system, and morphs between two speakers' voices. We also define four tasks that evaluate the alignment between speaker-representation models and human identity perception. The first two tasks measure how well a model's similarity score predicts the proportion of listeners who respond "same" and the majority response. The other two measure whether a model and listeners find the same speaker pairs similar, and whether a model's relationship to listener judgments holds equally for real and synthetic recordings. We evaluate eleven publicly available speech models. Listener judgments diverge from speaker labels most often for voice clones: in 41% of responses, listeners judged a clone and a real recording of the same speaker to be different people. No model reaches the noise ceiling; the best model explains 61% of the explainable variance. Models trained with speaker labels align with listeners better than self-supervised models do, yet a model with a 12% error rate on speaker labels aligns with listeners as well as models with error rates below 2%. VIPBench enables the evaluation of speaker models against human perception on real, cloned, and morphed speech, and our results motivate training speaker models directly on listener judgments.

Holistic Parallel Supervision for Expressive Speech to Speech Translation2611.06010v1语音翻译
展开摘要

Expressiveness is central to speech-to-speech translation (S2ST), yet existing datasets rarely align source and target speech jointly in content, prosody, timing, and speaker identity. Consequently, current systems often rely on indirect conditioning or transfer mechanisms. To obtain this supervision, we introduce a training-data construction strategy that combines professional dubbing, which provides human-performed correspondence in content, prosody, and timing, with multilingual voice conversion to reduce speaker mismatch. We evaluate this supervision using existing expressive S2ST systems and DirectS2ST, an end-to-end method developed in this work that predicts the complete target-speech representation directly from source speech. Experiments on English-German and English-Spanish show that holistic parallel supervision improves expressive realization across all evaluated systems. DirectS2ST exhibits the largest gains in content, prosody, timing, speaker similarity, and human-rated expressiveness. To our knowledge, this work provides the first systematic investigation of holistic parallel supervision for expressive S2ST.

LibriSpeech-Real-QA: A Benchmark for Streaming Question Answering2611.06496v1其他
展开摘要

Most speech large language models (SLMs) still process speech in an offline manner. They only start responding after they receive the complete speech segment. However, many real-world applications require streaming speech interaction, for which the audio continuously flows in and the machine is required to answer questions based on the so far received audio chunks. For example, a user may ask the system to continuously count how many times a person's name has been mentioned while they are speaking. We observe a lack of benchmarks and evaluation protocols for real-time speech interaction. Motivated by this, we propose LibriSpeech-Real-QA, a dataset and a benchmark for real-time speech interaction. We construct different types of real-time question-answer pairs from LibriSpeech to evaluate whether speech LLMs can identify target words in streaming audio. We evaluate several popular real-time commercial models and find that they still tend to respond only after the speech turn has finished. We demonstrate that we can enable a pretrained SLM to perform this task, where we explore several modeling designs and establish a solid baseline for future research.

Speaker-Consistent Grounding for Confusion-Resilient LLM-Based Target Speech Extraction2611.06297v1其他
展开摘要

Deterministic target-speaker extraction (TSE) tends to preserve observed linguistic content but may extract the interfering speaker. In contrast, autoregressive generative TSE improves perceptual quality, but is prone to content hallucination and speaker identity drift, which can be mitigated by model grounding with the evidence from the deterministic TSE. However, grounding such a generative TSE model with speaker-confused evidence may therefore reinforce the error. To address this gap, we proposed a repair-before-grounding framework with a training-free evidence-repair stage. Conditioning-diverse candidate selection (CDCS) selects target speaker-consistent evidence from complementary frozen TSE outputs. The selected waveform can then be used by code-space grounding (CSG) to constrain LLM-based TSE decoding. On Libri2Mix, CDCS reduces wrong-speaker extraction errors from 6.87% to 0.75% on clean mixtures and from 18.95% to 10.46% under noise. With the LLM-based TSE checkpoint unchanged, CSG further reduces noisy content- and acoustic-switch rates from 12.79% and 1.68% to 9.10% and 0.51%, respectively. Despite this stronger grounding, generative TSE retains its predicted perceptual-quality advantage over direct deterministic extraction. These results show that repairing evidence identity before grounding improves the reliability of generative TSE. Audio demos and code are available at https://hanyu-meng.github.io/LLM-TSE-Grounding-Demo/.

LiveFoley: Dynamic Real-Time Video-to-Audio Generation for Immersive Interactive Worlds2611.05988v1音频生成
展开摘要

Streaming video-to-audio (V2A) generation is essential for immersive interactive applications. However, real-time constraints leave existing streaming V2A methods far behind offline models: (1) visual frontends shrink their receptive fields to cut look-ahead latency, severely degrading audio-visual alignment; (2) causal autoregressive diffusion backends denoise each audio patch in isolation, impairing audio quality. To this end, we present LiveFoley, a streaming causal autoregressive diffusion architecture that addresses both bottlenecks with imperceptible latency. For the visual frontend, we introduce a Trailing Sliding Window that converts non-causal segment-level encoders into streaming modules without retraining. By simply realigning the window, it preserves precise audio-visual alignment, while a configurable hop size reduces the frontend look-ahead well below the human perceptual threshold. Building on this tunable hop size, we propose the Unified Varhop Framework to accommodate scenarios with diverse latency and computation budgets. For the audio backend, we explore widening the denoising window of the local diffusion transformer (LocDiT) head in two temporal directions. History LocDiT adds past context to improve quality at no latency cost and serves as our default. Alternatively, Future-Warmup LocDiT breaks the autoregressive deadlock with an approximate future condition, further improving generation quality at the cost of an acceptable backend look-ahead. Extensive experiments demonstrate that LiveFoley matches offline models in temporal alignment and leads streaming systems in generation quality. Project page: https://dalision.github.io/livefoley-page/

CLRM: Cross-Layer Relation Modeling for Audio Deepfake Detection2611.05678v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Audio deepfake detection (ADD) aims to distinguish bonafide speech from synthetic or manipulated speech. Representations from different layers of self-supervised learning (SSL) speech encoders vary in their ability to distinguish bonafide from spoofed speech. Comparing representations of the same utterance across encoder depths may reveal useful spoofing cues, yet common layer-selection and aggregation methods do not provide these comparisons as detection inputs. We propose Cross-Layer Relation Modeling (\ours{}), which explicitly encodes cross-layer relations alongside layer-specific features, making both directly available for ADD. CLRM reuses the existing detector's SSL encoder and identifies informative layer pairs by combining speaker-relative representation geometry with source-domain detection performance. The resulting relation score is fused with the detector's original score, requiring only the training of compact relation heads and fusion parameters while keeping the existing detector frozen. Across five external datasets, CLRM reduces the mean equal error rate (EER) for all three evaluated detectors, achieving relative reductions of 7.8--16.7%. Controlled comparisons with identical layer summaries and approximately matched auxiliary-head parameter counts also show a lower mean EER for explicit relations than direct concatenation, both before and after score fusion. These findings support explicit cross-layer comparisons as useful detection inputs for SSL-based audio deepfake detectors.

Drowning in Noise: On Signal Saliency in Self-Supervised Learning for Ocean Acoustics2611.06725v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Self-supervised learning (SSL) for audio typically benefits from larger pre-training corpora, but this relationship assumes that training data carries meaningful learning signal. In ocean acoustics, this assumption is often violated: passive recordings are dominated by waves, wind, and silence, with biological vocalizations occupying a small fraction of the total duration. We show that this overrepresentation of noise causes SSL models to allocate representational capacity to acoustically trivial structure, degrading downstream performance. Using BEATs as a testbed, we demonstrate that pre-training on 30 hours of salient recordings (segments containing animal vocalizations) outperforms 1,240 hours of unfiltered data on a 31-class marine mammal classification benchmark. Under linear probing, the manually curated salient set achieves markedly better accuracy than the full corpus, despite using 41 times less data. Automatic saliency-based filtering improves over unfiltered pre-training, but still falls well short of the manually curated salient set. Our findings suggest that, in domains where informative content is temporally sparse, data curation matters more than data scale for SSL pre-training.
