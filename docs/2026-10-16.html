Qwen-Audio-Jev: A Unified Interface for Audio Decisions

作者：Ying Fang, Zhifu Gao, Qing Gu, Jiajun He, Kangqi Jing, Xiangang Li, Yu Pu, Wupeng Wang

语音识别评分 18audiospeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出 Qwen-Audio-Jev，通过在 Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next 上增加 Jev 决策头，以直接输出概率分布或分数的方式统一处理选择、二元判断和评分任务，实现约 20 倍推理加速。

解决了什么问题

大型音频语言模型在需要频繁选择、判断和评分的高吞吐应用中，自回归文本生成带来额外计算开销和响应延迟。

核心方法

在 Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next 基础上增加专用 Jev 决策头，通过 CHOICE、NOUL、SCORE 三种决策原语，根据音频、自然语言问题和答案规范直接产生任务条件概率分布或数值分数。

主要创新点

提出统一决策接口，将选择、二元判断和评分集成到一个模型中，避免自回归生成，实现高吞吐决策。

实验结论

在 18 个标注配置上评估：CHOICE 在 MMAU-mini 和 MMSU 上准确率分别为 78.70% 和 84.06%；NOUL 在 VoxCeleb1-O 上等错误率 6.50%；SCORE 在 NISQA 上归一化平均绝对误差 0.1062，Pearson 和 Spearman 相关系数分别为 0.8210 和 0.8074；相比原模型推理加速约 20 倍。

和已有工作的关系

大型音频语言模型在音频理解上取得进展，但自回归生成带来延迟；本文针对该问题提出专用决策头方案。

Rethinking Batch Composition: Promoting Feature Diversity for Speech Enhancement

作者：Mingchi Hou, Ina Kodrasi

语音增强评分 17speechacousticspeech enhancement查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出特征分层的小批量采样策略，通过按干净语音声学特征分位数划分并强制每个小批量等比例包含不同分位，提升语音增强训练性能。

解决了什么问题

语音增强系统通常使用随机采样的小批量，可能缺乏干净语音内在声学特征的多样性。

核心方法

特征分层小批量采样：根据一种干净语音特征将训练样本划分到分位数，并强制每个小批量内不同分位等量表示。

主要创新点

无需修改增强模型或训练目标，仅通过改进数据采样提高训练效果，简单且互补。

实验结论

在两个大规模英语和西班牙语语音数据集及一个包含神经典型和病理语音的较小数据集上，相比标准随机小批量采样，在多个客观增强指标上有所提升；消融研究表明增益在不同训练运行中具有鲁棒性，但幅度取决于用于分层的声学特征。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

从事语音增强训练、数据采样策略和深度学习优化的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

先前研究发现干净语音特征与增强性能相关，本文受此启发提出分层采样。

对研究的启发

考虑数据特征分布对训练的影响，通过简单采样策略提升模型性能，可迁移到其他语音任务。

是否值得精读

方法简单有效，值得阅读了解具体实施细节和特征选择，但需注意其适用范围。

CF-AVSS: A Coarse-to-Fine Audio-Visual Speech Separation Framework with Target-Speaker Refinement and Post-Fusion for Real-World AVSE Challenge

作者：Longjie Luo, Zhongshu Hou, Yuepeng Li, Gang Yang, Shidong Shang, Fan Zhou

语音增强评分 17speechaudiospeakerspeech enhancement查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出粗到细两阶段音视频语音分离框架 CF-AVSS，通过渐进 SPAV-TFGridNet 产生视觉有序粗估计，再用 TFGridNet 目标说话人细化网络和开发集校准融合提升分离性能。

解决了什么问题

音视频语音增强需要从混响、噪声和重叠说话人的混合中恢复目标语音，挑战在于如何有效利用视觉线索并处理干扰。

核心方法

两阶段框架：第一阶段扩展渐进 SPAV-TFGridNet 到两个同步视觉流，产生视觉有序粗估计，逐步抑制干扰语音同时保留目标混响和共享背景噪声；第二阶段基于 TFGridNet 的目标说话人细化网络以原始混合和两个粗估计为输入进一步恢复目标语音；最后用开发集校准融合组合粗估计和细化估计。

主要创新点

采用粗到细策略，第一阶段保留混响和背景噪声以利于第二阶段细化；结合开发集校准的融合。

实验结论

在 ISCSLP 2026 Real-World AVSE Challenge 上，Track 1 并列第一、Track 2 第二（OVRL），两个赛道 CER 最低分别为 12.31% 和 14.79%。

现有 AVSE 方法利用视觉线索，但可能在处理混响和噪声方面不足；本文提出渐进抑制和细化。

对研究的启发

主要创新点

同时学习多个属性，揭示不同属性在 SSL 编码器不同深度自然涌现的层聚合策略。

实验结论

在多语言语义检索、说话人验证、语言识别和语音情感识别上，性能接近专用单任务学生，同时仅需一个共享编码器；层聚合策略显示不同属性在不同深度出现。

实验表明现有 AV-LLMs 在空间接地和关系推理上受限，而 HLR-LLM 大幅提升空间音视频场景理解且不损害语义感知。

Qwen-Audio-Jev: A Unified Interface for Audio Decisions2610.21614v1语音识别
展开摘要

Large audio language models (LALMs) have made substantial progress in audio understanding. However, in high-throughput applications requiring frequent selection, judgment, and scoring, autoregressive text generation introduces additional computational overhead and response latency. To address this limitation, we introduce Qwen-Audio-Jev by augmenting Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next with a dedicated Jev decision head. The model unifies selection, binary judgment, and scoring through three decision primitives: CHOICE, NOUL, and SCORE. Given audio, a natural-language question, and an explicit answer specification, it directly produces a task-conditioned probability distribution or a numerical score through typed decision readouts. Evaluation across 18 labeled configurations covers all three primitives: for CHOICE, Qwen-Audio-Jev achieves accuracies of 78.70% on MMAU-mini and 84.06% on MMSU; for NOUL, it achieves an equal error rate of 6.50% on the VoxCeleb1-O speaker verification benchmark; and for SCORE, the model achieves a normalized mean absolute error of 0.1062 on NISQA overall quality assessment, with Pearson and Spearman correlations of 0.8210 and 0.8074, respectively. Compared with the original Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash-Next, Qwen-Audio-Jev achieves an approximately 20x inference speedup. It demonstrates its potential for efficient, high-throughput audio decision-making in applications such as large-scale audio screening, speaker verification, and automated quality assessment.

Rethinking Batch Composition: Promoting Feature Diversity for Speech Enhancement2610.21803v1语音增强
展开摘要

Speech enhancement systems are commonly trained using randomly sampled mini-batches, which may contain limited diversity in intrinsic acoustic characteristics of the clean speech signals. Motivated by previous findings linking clean speech characteristics to enhancement performance, we propose a feature-stratified mini-batch sampling strategy that divides training examples into quantiles according to a clean-speech characteristic and enforces equal representation within each mini-batch. Experiments across two large-scale English and Spanish speech datasets and a smaller dataset containing neurotypical and pathological speech demonstrate improvements over standard random mini-batch sampling across multiple objective enhancement metrics. Ablation studies demonstrate that the gains are robust across training runs, while their magnitude depends on the acoustic characteristic used for stratification. The proposed strategy requires no modification to the enhancement model or training objective, providing a simple and complementary approach to speech enhancement training.

CF-AVSS: A Coarse-to-Fine Audio-Visual Speech Separation Framework with Target-Speaker Refinement and Post-Fusion for Real-World AVSE Challenge2610.21773v1语音增强
展开摘要

Audio-visual speech enhancement (AVSE) leverages visual cues to recover target speech from mixtures corrupted by reverberation, noise, and overlapping speakers. We propose a coarse-to-fine two-stage AVSE framework. In the first stage, a progressive SPAV-TFGridNet is extended to two synchronized visual streams to produce visually ordered coarse estimates by progressively suppressing interfering speech while preserving target reverberation and shared background noise. In the second stage, a TFGridNet-based target speaker refinement network takes the original mixture and both coarse estimates as input to further recover target speech and improve signal quality. The coarse and refined estimates are finally combined using development-set-calibrated fusion. On the ISCSLP 2026 Real-World AVSE Challenge, our system tied for first place on Track 1 and ranked second on Track 2 in OVRL, while achieving the lowest CER on both tracks at 12.31% and 14.79%, respectively.

Learning Speech Attribute Representations via Multi-Teacher Distillation2610.21941v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Recent post-training approaches have shown that self-supervised speech encoders can be adapted to learn a single utterance-level speech attribute through knowledge distillation. In this work, we investigate whether a single speech encoder can simultaneously preserve multiple complementary utterance-level speech representations. We propose a multi-attribute multi-teacher distillation framework that learns semantic, speaker, language, and emotion representations from four frozen teacher models using a shared SSL backbone and attribute-specific branches. Experiments on multilingual semantic retrieval, speaker verification, language identification, and speech emotion recognition show that the proposed model achieves performance close to dedicated single-task students while requiring only a single shared encoder. Furthermore, the learned layer aggregation strategies reveal that different speech attributes naturally emerge at different depths of the SSL encoder, providing new insights into the organization of speech representations.

Hear, Localize, and Reason: Spatially Aware Scene Understanding for Audio-Visual LLMs2610.21588v1其他
展开摘要

Audio-visual large language models (AV-LLMs) have made strong progress in multimodal understanding, yet most treat audio as monaural semantic content, limiting their ability to reason about where sounds originate and how they relate to the visual scene. Recent spatial audio-visual studies address parts of this problem, but often focus on specific abilities, such as spatial correspondence or direction and distance reasoning. In this work, we introduce HLR-AVSceneQA, a comprehensive benchmark with a hear-localize-reason hierarchy that evaluates whether models can recognize what is heard, localize sound sources in egocentric and allocentric views, and use these localized sources to reason about how they relate to the visual scene. We further introduce HLR-LLM, which augments a strong audio-visual foundation model with a binaural spatial audio branch alongside its monaural semantic audio pathway. We train HLR-LLM with a curriculum that incorporates final-stage chain-of-thought supervision for multi-step spatial reasoning. Experiments show that existing AV-LLMs remain limited in spatial grounding and relational reasoning, while HLR-LLM substantially improves spatial audio-visual scene understanding without compromising semantic audio-visual perception.

From Signals to Evidence: Interpretable Underwater Acoustic Target Sensing with Large Language Models - Part I: Framework and Recognition2610.22064v1其他
展开摘要

Underwater acoustic target detection and recognition are essential to marine surveillance and intelligent ocean sensing. Despite substantial advances in deep learning, existing systems typically produce binary detection decisions, categorical labels, or scalar confidence scores, offering limited insight into the physical evidence underlying their outputs. Large language models (LLMs) offer an opportunity to move underwater acoustic sensing toward interpretable, evidence-grounded understanding; however, generic audio encoders struggle to capture the distinctive physical characteristics of underwater acoustics, and physically annotated data are scarce. In this paper, the first of a two-part series, we propose a from-signals-to-evidence framework that integrates physics-aware acoustic encoding with a pretrained LLM, organized around a unified feature-evidence-decision chain with a shared reasoning core. An open input layer accommodates heterogeneous information-raw waveforms, time-frequency representations, environmental and geographic data, and textual physical priors-unifying detection and recognition within a common formulation. In Part I, we instantiate this framework for target recognition through an Underwater Acoustic Physics-aware Transformer (UPT), which tokenizes complementary acoustic representations and learns task-disentangled physical representations through four physics-oriented learning tasks. We develop a physics-parameterized data engine that provides traceable supervision at two levels of granularity and introduce an attribute-level evaluation protocol to assess the physical fidelity of generated descriptions. Experiments on simulated recordings of four vessels demonstrate accurate vessel recognition and reliable estimation of shaft frequency, blade-passing frequency, and machinery-related attributes.

How Much Physics Can Vision Learn from Audio? Probing and Distilling Damping Structure2610.22221v1其他
展开摘要

Physical understanding is crucial for decision making, yet vision models struggle to infer properties such as damping that govern how materials behave. Audio encodes such properties directly, because an object's acoustic response to impact reveals its damping through measurable decay. We probe frozen encoders for the quality factor $Q$, a dimensionless measure of how many oscillations an object completes before its ring dies away, and report how well each embedding orders impacts by $Q$ using the Spearman rank correlation $ρ$, for which $ρ=1$ is a perfect ordering and $ρ=0$ an unrelated one. Audio embeddings reach $ρ=0.73$ and retain most of that advantage on materials withheld from training, while a single video frame taken at the moment of impact, encoded by CLIP, supports only $ρ=0.28$. Having established where the signal lives, we ask how much of that gap vision can recover. We distill a frozen audio teacher into a frozen CLIP backbone through a trained projection head and find that this recovers a useful fraction of the audio advantage. Because pooled correlations on this task are dominated in practice by category identity, we also evaluate under a leave-one-material-out protocol in which the held-out material is withheld from the projection head as well as the probe. Distillation with a four-frame input improves the correlation there from 0.234 to 0.338 (+0.053 over the matched four-frame baseline, 14 of 17 materials), a gain that no category-level strategy can produce. Widening the visual input to four frames spanning the impact supplies half of that improvement at no acoustic cost, which indicates that part of what a single frame misses is visible dynamics.

Full-Duplex Interaction with Speech, Expressions and Gestures2610.21452v1其他
展开摘要
