Exploiting Acoustic and Content-Oriented Speaker Verification Attacks Against Multilingual Voice Anonymization

作者：Ridwan Arefeen, Ze Li, Rong Tong, Ming Li, Xiaoxiao Miao

说话人评分 21speechvoiceacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该论文研究多语言语音匿名化场景下声学与内容导向的说话人验证攻击，发现攻击效果取决于匿名语音的语言效用（信息保留程度），并构建多语言语音转换数据集以改善跨语言泛化。

解决了什么问题

现有语音匿名化的攻击者ASV研究主要限于英语，多语言场景下攻击行为不明；传统ASV显示声学和上下文信息对多语言说话人验证重要，但匿名化语音上的攻击者ASV是否同样如此尚未探索。

核心方法

在多语言匿名化语音上评估声学导向与内容导向的攻击者ASV，并构建多语言语音转换数据集以提升跨语言泛化。

主要创新点

系统研究多语言匿名化语音上的声学与内容导向攻击；构建多语言语音转换数据集；揭示攻击效果与语言效用（信息保留）的关系。

实验结论

攻击有效性取决于匿名语音的语言实用程度；整体上声学导向攻击性能更优，但当语言信息保留较好时，内容导向与声学导向攻击的性能差距相比强失真条件缩小；多语言语音转换数据集可进一步提升性能并部分缩小跨语言差距。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明局限。

适合谁读

语音隐私、说话人验证、语音匿名化安全评估研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

已有攻击者ASV研究主要针对英语；传统ASV表明声学与上下文信息对多语言说话人验证重要。

对研究的启发

提示语音匿名化评估应同时考虑隐私与语言实用价值，纳入声学和内容攻击者以及多语言泛化测试。

是否值得精读

是，因为引入多语言攻击者评估和数据集，并揭示攻击策略与语言信息保留的关系。

TOP 2

Audiovisual joint learning for end-to-end hearing aids

作者：You-Jin Li, Yu Tsao, Borching Su, Kuan-Chung Ting, Fan-Gang Zeng

语音增强评分 20speechaudioacousticspeakerspeech enhancement查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该论文提出AV-NeuroAMP端到端视听框架，联合噪声语音、目标说话人视频和听力图进行语音增强、个性化放大和动态范围压缩，在客观评测和听测中优于传统与两阶段基线。

解决了什么问题

助听器用户在噪声特别是竞争说话人条件下言语理解困难；传统两阶段处理可能使增强误差和失真传递到放大阶段，纯音频语音增强因目标与干扰语音声学相似而在竞争说话人条件下受限。

核心方法

提出端到端视听模型AV-NeuroAMP，集成噪声语音、目标说话人视频和听者听力图，采用听力图条件的特征线性调制（AC-FiLM）联合执行语音增强、个性化放大和动态范围压缩。

主要创新点

端到端联合优化取代分阶段处理；利用目标说话人视频和听者听力图；提出AC-FiLM模块融入个性化听力曲线。

实验结论

客观评测显示在域内英语测试集上优于传统放大、纯音频NeuroAMP模型和两阶段系统，且在未见过的普通话测试集上保持改进；听测显示语音质量和可懂度改善，竞争说话人条件下获益最大。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明局限。

适合谁读

助听器、语音增强、多模态语音处理、个性化听力补偿研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

传统助听器将语音增强与听力损失补偿分阶段处理；纯音频语音增强在竞争说话人下效果有限；已有音频NeuroAMP模型和两阶段系统作为比较。

对研究的启发

多模态信息与听者个性化特征联合端到端建模可提升噪声及竞争说话人条件下的语音增强效果。

是否值得精读

是，因为展示了视听个性化端到端框架及跨语言泛化能力。

TOP 3

DirectSpeech2LLM: A Simple End-to-End Framework to Mitigate Prompt Overfitting in Speech-LLMs

作者：Hemant Yadav, Sunayana Sitaram, Roger Zimmermann, Rajiv Ratn Shah

语音识别评分 18speechspeech recognitionspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该论文提出DirectSpeech2LLM，通过CTC对齐的语音嵌入直接输入冻结LLM，仅用ASR数据训练即缓解提示过拟合，零样本泛化到语音翻译和情感识别。

解决了什么问题

Speech-LLM在仅用ASR指令训练时出现提示过拟合，无法泛化到语音翻译等新指令，仍主要表现ASR行为。

核心方法

在冻结LLM嵌入矩阵上计算基于距离的CTC损失，使用贪心CTC标签分别导出几何与时间对齐的语音嵌入作为LLM输入；仅使用960小时LibriSpeech ASR数据训练。

主要创新点

简单端到端框架缓解提示过拟合；通过修改CTC损失提供隐式几何对齐，无需显式回归损失；保留LLM在未见任务上的指令跟随能力。

实验结论

在ASR（可见任务）上超过级联系统；在语音翻译和情感识别（不可见任务）上零样本泛化，接近级联系统上限；几何对齐强度作用较先前假设小；结果跨两个LLM家族一致，并随训练数据和模型容量扩展。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明局限。

适合谁读

Speech-LLM、语音识别、语音翻译、多任务语音指令学习研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

已有Speech-LLM存在提示过拟合问题；级联系统作为对比基线；先前工作可能使用显式回归损失进行几何对齐。

对研究的启发

表明通过嵌入空间CTC对齐可保留LLM指令跟随能力，减少对显式对齐损失的依赖，为语音LLM训练提供简单有效路径。

是否值得精读

是，因为简单框架与零样本泛化结果，并重新审视几何对齐的作用。

Feature Encoding in VAE-based Audio Decoders: Effects of Input, Depth and Distribution

作者：Louis McCallum, Mick Grierson

音频生成评分 14audiomusic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该论文通过分层与跨层聚类分析RAVE和EnCodec解码器激活，考察音高和BPM等音乐特征的编码，发现合成刺激编码更强、自然音频需非线性探针，中间层联合编码提升，跨层聚类改善BPM编码。

解决了什么问题

神经音频合成模型如RAVE生成质量高，但其内部表征如何编码音乐特征尚不清楚。

核心方法

对三种音乐域训练的RAVE解码器激活进行逐层和跨层聚类分析，使用四种刺激类型，并以通用EnCodec模型评估架构泛化；采用线性与非线性探针测量特征编码。

主要创新点

系统分析音频解码器内部表征的音乐特征编码；比较合成与自然刺激、线性与非线性探针、层次深度与跨层聚类。

实验结论

RAVE对合成刺激编码较强（音高相关0.45，BPM相关0.76），自然音频编码减弱但仍存在；自然音频使用非线性探针时编码更强（平均R2=0.56）；中间层联合编码能力增强；通用EnCodec解码器呈现类似趋势；跨层聚类改善BPM编码强度和普遍性，但对联合编码无影响。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明局限。

适合谁读

神经音频合成、音乐信息检索、模型可解释性、音频生成研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

已有RAVE等神经音频合成模型生成质量高但可解释性差；使用通用EnCodec模型进行架构泛化比较。

对研究的启发

为神经音频模型内部表征提供可解释性证据，提示不同层和集群可能适合不同音乐特征控制。

是否值得精读

是，因为提供了系统的可解释性分析和跨模型发现。

Voice Anonymization Made Simple: Training-Free Anonymization with Projected Classifier-Free Guidance

作者：Xiang Shi, Han Zhu, Ming Li, Xiaoxiao Miao

其他评分 13speechvoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

该论文提出一种无需训练的两阶段语音匿名化方法，通过修改预训练流匹配语音转换系统的条件构建和生成引导，在提升说话人隐私的同时保持词错误率和情感识别性能。

解决了什么问题

语音匿名化需要隐藏说话人身份并保留语言和副语言信息，现有方法多需专门训练。

核心方法

在预训练流匹配语音转换系统上进行两阶段修改：条件构建阶段用随机说话人上下文重新生成源内容表征以减少源说话人残留；语音生成阶段抑制与源说话人相关的生成方向并保留内容引导；仅修改推理时条件和引导，不更新任何预训练参数。

主要创新点

训练免费，不修改预训练模型参数；通过投影分类器自由引导抑制源说话人分量。

实验结论

在VoicePrivacy 2026 Track 1上，两阶段方法改善说话人隐私，同时保持有竞争力的词错误率和情感识别性能。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明局限。

适合谁读

语音隐私、语音匿名化、语音转换、说话人信息保护研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

已有语音匿名化方法多需专门训练；基于预训练流匹配语音转换系统。

对研究的启发

表明推理时条件与引导调整即可实现训练免费的匿名化，为轻量隐私保护提供思路。

是否值得精读

是，因为无需训练且结果有竞争力，适合关注部署效率的研究者。

Exploiting Acoustic and Content-Oriented Speaker Verification Attacks Against Multilingual Voice Anonymization2610.08107v1说话人
展开摘要

Attacker ASV systems for voice anonymization have been studied primarily in English, leaving their behavior in multilingual settings largely unexplored. Conventional ASV has shown that both acoustic and contextual information are important for multilingual speaker verification. Inspired by this, we investigate whether the same holds for attacker ASV on anonymized speech. We evaluate both acoustic- and content-oriented attackers on multilingual anonymized speech and construct a multilingual voice-converted dataset to improve cross-lingual generalization. Our results show that attacker effectiveness depends on the linguistic utility of the anonymized speech. Overall, acoustic-oriented attackers achieve better performance. However, when linguistic information is well preserved, the performance gap between content- and acoustic-oriented attackers narrows compared with conditions involving stronger speech distortion. The multilingual voice-converted dataset further improves performance and partially reduces the cross-lingual gap. Audiovisual joint learning for end-to-end hearing aids2610.08579v1语音增强
展开摘要

Speech understanding in noise remains challenging for hearing-aid users, particularly in the presence of competing speakers. Conventional hearing aids typically perform speech enhancement (SE) and hearing-loss compensation in separate stages, which may cause enhancement errors and signal distortions to carry over to the amplification stage. Moreover, audio-only SE often provides limited benefits under competing-speech conditions because the target and interfering speech share similar acoustic characteristics, making them difficult to separate. To address these limitations, we propose AV-NeuroAMP, an end-to-end audiovisual framework that integrates noisy speech, target-talker video, and the listener's audiogram to jointly perform SE, personalized amplification, and dynamic-range compression. We further introduce audiogram-conditioned feature-wise linear modulation (AC-FiLM) to effectively incorporate listener-specific hearing profiles. Objective evaluations showed that AV-NeuroAMP outperformed conventional amplification, the audio-only NeuroAMP model, and two-stage systems on an in-domain English test set, and that these improvements were retained on an unseen Mandarin test set. Listening tests involving normal-hearing participants under simulated hearing loss and listeners with hearing loss further demonstrated improvements in speech quality and intelligibility, with the greatest benefits observed under competing-speech conditions. These findings support end-to-end audiovisual personalized amplification as a promising approach for improving hearing-aid performance in challenging acoustic environments.

DirectSpeech2LLM: A Simple End-to-End Framework to Mitigate Prompt Overfitting in Speech-LLMs2610.08085v1语音识别
展开摘要

Speech-LLMs often exhibit prompt overfitting, where models solely trained on automatic speech recognition (ASR) instruction fail to generalize to new instructions such as speech translation and continue to behave primarily as ASR system. We propose DirectSpeech2LLM, a simple end-to-end framework that preserves the instruction-following ability of the LLM on unseen tasks when conditioned on speech. It computes distance-based CTC loss over the frozen LLM embedding matrix and uses greedy CTC labels to derive geometrically and temporally aligned speech embeddings respectively as an input to the LLM. Trained solely on 960 hours of LibriSpeech ASR data, DirectSpeech2LLM outperforms the cascaded system on ASR (seen task) and generalizes zero-shot to speech translation and emotion recognition (two unseen tasks), closely matching the cascaded system upper bound on these two new instructions despite seeing neither during training. We also find that geometric alignment strength plays a smaller role than previously assumed, as our modified CTC loss is shown to provide sufficient implicit geometric grounding without requiring an explicit regression loss. Results are consistent across two LLM families and scale with both more training data and model capacity.

Feature Encoding in VAE-based Audio Decoders: Effects of Input, Depth and Distribution2610.07966v1音频生成
展开摘要

Neural audio synthesis models like the Realtime Audio Variational autoEncoder (RAVE) achieve impressive genera tion quality, yet how their internal representations encode musical features remains poorly understood. We present a systematic layer-wise and cross-layer cluster analysis of RAVE decoder activations across three models trained on different musical domains, tested with four stimulus types. We then evaluate architectural generalization with a general purpose EnCodec model. For RAVE, we find that synthetic stimuli are encoded well across models and audio features (pitch |\r{ho}|=0.45, 5.1x the null, BPM |\r{ho}| = 0.76, 8.6x the null). These results are reduced but still substantively apparent when using natural audio (mean across features |\r{ho}|=0.25, 2.8x the null). Natural audio sees a stronger encoding when nonlinear probes are used (mean across features R2=0.56, 18x the null, +0.152 nonlinear gain over the linear probe R2). Encoding strength varies throughout the layers of the decoder and an increased ability to joint-encode in the middle layers is seen across all audio features (\b{eta}2 all negative, p < 0.05). The general purpose EnCodec decoder also sees similar strong synthetic responses across audio features, similar nonlinear gains for natural audio joint encoding and similar depth profiles. We find the best cross-layer cluster improves the strength (r = 0.65, p = 0.006) and prevalence (r = 0.75, p = 0.001) of BPM encoding when compared against the best whole layers within the same section, with no effect for joint encoding. These findings advance the interpretability of neural audio models and inform targeted control strategies for neural synthesis.

Voice Anonymization Made Simple: Training-Free Anonymization with Projected Classifier-Free Guidance2610.08276v1其他
展开摘要

Voice anonymization aims to conceal speaker identity while preserving linguistic and paralinguistic information, yet many existing methods require dedicated training. We propose a training-free, two-stage anonymization method for pretrained flow-matching voice conversion systems. In the condition-construction stage, the source content representation is regenerated under a randomly selected speaker context to reduce residual source-speaker information while preserving linguistic content. In the speech-generation stage, the source speech is used to identify the generation direction associated with the original speaker, and the source-aligned component is suppressed while useful content guidance is retained. The proposed method modifies only inference-time conditioning and guidance, without updating any pretrained parameters. Experiments on VoicePrivacy 2026 Track 1 show that the two-stage method improves speaker privacy while maintaining competitive word error rate and emotion recognition performance.

ARIA: Audio-Driven Melody-Tone Relation Modeling for Cantonese Lyric Authoring2610.07902v1其他
展开摘要

Cantonese lyric writing requires close alignment between lexical tones and melodic pitch. Existing melody-guided lyric generation methods typically rely on symbolic melody to generate lyrics. However, in real songwriting scenarios, melodies are often expressed as raw singing audio or hummed recordings, where pitch is implicit, noisy, and unstructured, making these methods difficult to apply directly. To address this limitation, we propose ARIA, a two-stage audio-driven melody-tone relation modeling framework for Cantonese lyric authoring that generates Cantonese lyrics from singing recordings with provided character-level timestamps. Specifically, we first design a Tri-Stream Relation-Aware Tone Estimator (TRATE) to predict 0243 sequences from timestamped singing audio by modeling multi-stream acoustic cues and relational tonal structure. We then propose a Decoupled Retrieval-Augmented Tone-Conditioned Lyric Generator (DRA-TCLG) to generate fluent lyrics conditioned on predicted tonal plans with retrieval-enhanced lexical guidance. Moreover, we construct a large-scale aligned audio-Jyutping-0243 dataset from real Cantonese singing recordings to support this new task.
