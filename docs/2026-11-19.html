21Codec: Decoder-side Speaker Control for Text-to-Speech

作者：David Lindevelt, Suzan Verberne, Joost Broekens

语音合成评分 20speechspeakertext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出21Codec流式编解码器，通过对抗训练和共振峰移位数据增强减少令牌流中的说话人信息，实现解码器端说话人控制。

解决了什么问题

现有编解码器从令牌流推断说话人身份，流式传输时每个块从局部上下文重新推断身份，对说话人漂移不够鲁棒。

核心方法

使用对抗性说话人分类器和NANSY风格共振峰移位数据增强，推动模型依赖提供的说话人嵌入，并减少令牌流中可恢复的说话人信息；通过条件向量控制说话人身份。

主要创新点

提出减少令牌流中说话人信息的编解码器设计，实现解码器端说话人控制，并支持流式传输中跨块保持说话人身份及中途切换。

实验结论

摘要中说明21Codec可以通过条件向量控制说话人身份，且流式传输中跨块保持说话人身份；未提供具体实验指标或对比结果。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

语音合成、流式TTS、说话人控制相关的研究人员和工程师。

和已有工作的关系

与现有编解码器模型（从令牌流推断说话人身份）和NANSY风格数据增强相关。

对研究的启发

为流式TTS系统提供更好的说话人身份可控性，提示可以通过减少令牌流中的敏感信息并结合外部条件来增强控制。

是否值得精读

可能值得深入阅读，因为提出了针对流式说话人漂移的解决方案。

TOP 2

Exploring Speaker-Aware Engagement Modeling for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder

作者：Kaleen Shrestha, Austin Joel Dympep, Young Suh Hong, Maja Matarić

说话人评分 16audioacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

探究说话人分离是否改善ASD儿童声学参与度建模，发现分离音频AUROC更高但差异不显著，且声学特征贡献存在个体差异。

解决了什么问题

在自然互动中，多说话人和非典型互动模式使基于声学特征的ASD儿童参与度建模复杂，需要确定说话人分离是否有益。

核心方法

使用说话人分离技术从儿童和父母/照顾者音频中提取声学特征，训练经典机器学习模型，并与未分离音频训练的模型比较AUROC性能。

主要创新点

将参与者水平差异证据扩展到声学领域，并证明在复杂自然互动中隔离说话人特定发声的可行性。

实验结论

分离音频始终产生更高的AUROC值，但差异无统计学显著性；儿童与照顾者声学特征贡献因参与者而异。

可能局限

样本量小（仅7名儿童），统计差异不显著，且声学信息与参与度的关联不一致。

适合谁读

自闭症谱系障碍研究、语音处理、情感计算、人机交互领域的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与先前视觉模态的ASD参与度建模工作相关，本研究扩展到声学特征和说话人分离。

对研究的启发

提示在自然多说话人场景中，说话人分离可能有助于提升建模性能，但需注意个体差异，可能需要个性化建模。

是否值得精读

可能值得深入阅读，因其提供了说话人分离在真实场景中的实用证据，尽管统计不显著。

核心方法

主要创新点

发现声音与风格条件化的不对称关系，为TTS架构设计提供层次化条件策略的实用指导。

实验结论

摘要中未明确说明具体实验细节，但指出声音受益于早期条件化以保持身份，风格受益于早期条件化以提升表现力。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

语音合成、语音风格控制、神经网络架构设计的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

与TTS中声音和情感风格独立控制的相关工作有关。

对研究的启发

Great Listeners, Poor Decision-Makers: Rethinking Agentic Audio AI

作者：Siddique Latif, Moid Sandu, Farrukh Pervez, Raja Jurdak

其他评分 13speechaudiomusicvoicespeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出在音频AI中增加显式决策层以解决语音代理误行动问题，通过基于证据的行动门控显著减少误执行并保持有效服务。

解决了什么问题

语音代理在不应行动时行动（如执行对他人命令、误听、用户未说完就承诺），识别能力已高但决策不可靠。

核心方法

设计参考架构，在感知和推理之外引入显式决策层，利用受话者、说话人身份、内容置信度等证据，通过模糊推理、进化优化和顺序决策决定行动、等待、询问或沉默。

主要创新点

将问题的焦点从识别转向决策，提出显式行动门控层，并证明模糊门对过度自信估计更鲁棒。

实验结论

在录音评估中，显式门控将执行的旁白从41%降至7-10%，同时服务83-85%的有效请求（在成功澄清下），优于基于提示的基线；受话者和说话人证据最重要。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明局限性。

适合谁读

语音代理、音频AI、人机交互、决策系统研究者与开发者。

和已有工作的关系

与现有语音识别和基于提示的代理基线相关，对比了显式门控与提示方法。

对研究的启发

强调在音频AI中决策层的重要性，为可靠语音代理架构提供设计方向。

是否值得精读

值得深入阅读，因提供了具体性能和架构改进。

在较大数据集中女性比男性分类更准确；模型在一种性别上训练不能泛化到另一种性别。

21Codec: Decoder-side Speaker Control for Text-to-Speech2611.24094v1语音合成
展开摘要

We propose 21Codec, a streaming codec model with reduced recoverable speaker information from the token stream and decoder-side speaker control. Existing codec models infer speaker identity from token stream. Making them streamable is not sufficiently robust to speaker drift, because each chunk re-infers the identity from the local chunk context alone. Our adversarial speaker classifier and NANSY-style formant-shift data augmentation pushes the model to rely on the provided speaker embedding while reducing the recoverable speaker information from the token stream. We show that 21Codec can control speaker identity through the conditioning vector, and that the speaker identity is maintained across chunks during streaming. With the ability to control speaker identity even in mid-stream, 21Codec is a promising candidate for streaming TTS systems for better controllability of speaker identity.

Exploring Speaker-Aware Engagement Modeling for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder2611.24794v1说话人
展开摘要

Prior work on engagement modeling in children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) has demonstrated substantial individual variability in behavioral features, particularly in visual modalities. In this work, we extend the literature to acoustic features in naturalistic interactions, where multiple speakers and atypical interaction patterns complicate speaker-specific analysis. We investigated whether speaker diarization improved acoustic engagement modeling during month-long, in-home socially assistive robot (SAR) interventions with seven children with ASD. We trained classical machine learning models using acoustic features extracted from diarized child and parent/caregiver audio and compared their performance with models trained on undiarized audio. Diarized audio consistently yielded higher area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) values, although these differences were not statistically significant. More importantly, the relative contributions of child and caregiver acoustic features varied across participants, indicating that the acoustic information associated with engagement was not consistent across children. These findings extend evidence of participant-level variability in ASD engagement modeling to the acoustic domain and demonstrate the feasibility of isolating speaker-specific vocalizations for studying engagement in complex, naturalistic interactions.

Location Matters: Layer-wise Prompt Conditioning for Voice--Style Disentanglement in Text-to-Speech2611.24125v1语音合成
展开摘要

Independent control of voice and emotional style characteristics of speech is a long-standing problem in text-to-speech (TTS) systems. We investigate how the conditioning location of voice and style prompts across TTS decoder layers affects the trade-off between preservation and expressiveness for voice identity and emotional style. We introduce a layer-dependent gated fusion mechanism and systematically study which layers should be influenced by which prompt type. Our findings show an asymmetric relationship. Voice benefits from early conditioning for identity preservation, while style benefits from early conditioning for expressiveness. Our findings provide practical guidance that helps neural network architects make an appropriate trade-off for the architecture of TTS systems according to their use cases.

Great Listeners, Poor Decision-Makers: Rethinking Agentic Audio AI2611.23847v1其他
展开摘要

Audio artificial intelligence (AI) now recognizes speech, sounds, and music remarkably well, yet voice agents still act when they should not: they execute commands addressed to others, act on misheard content, and commit before the user finishes speaking. This position article argues that the bottleneck in agentic audio AI has shifted from recognition to decision-making. We propose a reference architecture with an explicit decision layer, separate from perception and reasoning, that chooses whether to act, wait, ask, or remain silent from evidence on addressedness, speaker identity, content confidence, tool reliability, utterance completeness, and action risk. We motivate the design through six recurring decision failures and a decision-theoretic argument that transcript confidence alone is insufficient to justify an action. We implement the layer with fuzzy inference, evolutionary optimization, and sequential decision-making, and evaluate it through simulations, ablations, and recorded audio, including egocentric recordings. Addressedness and speaker evidence matter most, risk-aware gating suppresses costly errors, and well-tuned policies perform similarly, with fuzzy gates more robust to overconfident addressedness estimates. On recorded audio, explicit gating cuts executed side talk from 41\% to 7--10\% while serving 83--85\% of valid requests under successful clarification, outperforming prompting-based baselines. These results support explicit action gating as an architectural requirement for reliable voice agents.

Sex Differences in the Detection of Parkinson's Disease from Speech2611.24741v1其他
展开摘要

Parkinson's disease (PD) is the second most common neurodegenerative disease in the world, and 70\% to 75\% of PD patients suffer from speech disorders at some stage. Since PD affects men more frequently than women, previous publications tend to rely on unbalanced datasets that potentially introduce sex biases into trained models. This study investigates sex differences in PD detection from speech using deep learning and traditional machine learning approaches. We evaluated speech-based PD detection using embeddings from three pre-trained speech models, combined with handcrafted acoustic features and different classifiers. Sex-specific experiments were conducted on balanced subsets of four sustained phonation datasets, controlling for sex, disease status, and, where possible, age. All recordings were obtained during a sustained phonation task. Results show that female speakers were more accurately classified than male speakers in the larger dataset. Sex-specific results show that when the models are trained on one sex, they fail to generalize to the other one. Our results highlight the importance of including sex as a variable in the development of PD detection systems.

Induction Head Origins of SLAM-ASR Speech Recognition Models2611.24721v1语音识别
展开摘要

What makes a small trained map from encoder states to the embedding space of a frozen language model sufficient for competitive speech recognition? The premise of the SLAM-ASR architecture is that a frozen language model possesses linguistic knowledge rich enough to perform the recognition task. This assumption, while reasonable, provides little insight into the mechanisms underlying these models. We therefore seek the concrete internal structures on which they rely. This work shows that SLAM-ASR models rely on induction heads of the frozen language model: the same heads that copy repeated token sequences in text are also important for mapping speech embeddings to transcript tokens. Moreover, we find pairs of heads that behave as a constrained form of differential attention, cancelling attention noise and sharpening attention onto the previously generated word. Finally, we show how induction heads could be used to improve transcription quality under noisy audio.

Harmonic Circuit: Chord Progression Lies in a Sparse Principal Information Flow in Symbolic Music Models2611.24453v1音乐生成
展开摘要

Mechanistic Interpretability (MI) seeks to decode neural network computations into human-understandable logic. A core methodology in MI is circuit discovery: identifying the information flow in sub-networks that drive specific model behaviors. While circuit discovery has matured within Natural Language Processing (NLP), the interpretable circuits in music models remain largely unexplored. In this work, we identify a music-functional circuit within two symbolic music generative models: ChatMusician and MuPT. The music function we consider is the prediction of the terminal tonic root in a classical harmonic progression pattern $I \ldots V \rightarrow I$. We use the Auto-Emergence algorithm to extract a principal sparse sub-network as the identified circuit. Validation via causal intervention confirms that this identified circuit is a primary driver of harmonic progression behaviors in the symbolic music models. To our knowledge, this represents the first systematic circuit-discovery study conducted within the music domain. Our demo page is available at: https://ukiyo23rui.github.io/auto-emergence-demo/.

Why Multi-Source Audio Fails to Compose: A Geometric Diagnosis and Its Remedy2611.24410v1其他
展开摘要

Multi-source audio-to-image generation exhibits consistent compositional failures when audio tokens occupy geometrically displaced, semantically unanchored positions in the CLIP text-token space, where the diffusion U-Net lacks the prior to reliably attend to them or ground them in distinct spatial regions. We formalize this as the geometric displacement phenomenon and expose it through a two-level clustering analysis comparing BEATs and CLAP embeddings at the raw encoder and projected CLIP token levels. Three geometric properties emerge as important: intra-class compactness, angular alignment with the CLIP text manifold, and inter-token spatial separation. We show that CLAP, whose contrastive language-audio pretraining aligns embeddings with natural language semantics, substantially outperforms BEATs on all three properties, improving the silhouette score by more than 3x and the Audio-Image Content score as well. Building on this analysis, we introduce a multi-token extension that requires no diffusion-backbone retraining and assigns each audio source to a distinct placeholder token in the CLIP tokenizer, complemented by a mixed inference-time objective that combines Attend-and-Excite with an A* spatial separation loss to resolve residual cross-token interference. For evaluation, we construct multi-audio compositional benchmarks from VGGSound (VGG2, VGG3, and VGGN) and extend six standard audio-image metrics to the multi-source setting. Across five benchmarks and four strong baselines, our method achieves strong results on the metrics most directly tied to compositional faithfulness, with AIC improvements of up to 2.25x over the strongest baseline and FID reduced by nearly 2x on two-source generation. We propose geometric displacement as a diagnostic framework for cross-modal generation systems that project learned embeddings into a pretrained contrastive space.

Constructing Dynamic Conversations with Neural Back-channel Generation and Voice-line Scheduling2611.24086v1其他
展开摘要

We present a novel approach to the problem of combining two voice-line channels of dyadic conversational audio into a unified conversation with controllable back-channeling and turn-taking dynamics. In voice performances for the game industry, the two tracks of voice-lines are typically recorded or synthesized in isolation. Our method takes two such unsynchronized tracks and uses a neural scheduler to sequence them into a single dynamic conversation, modifying both tracks by inserting or removing pauses and introducing voice overlap as appropriate. We further propose a back-channel predictor that is able to predict opportunity for back-channels for a given conversational turn's audio and content, which the scheduler can then incorporate. We evaluate our scheduler and predictor independently and in combination by: various quantitative comparison; a qualitative and perceptual comparison to prior art and naive baselines; a compelling set of results showing animator control over the diversity, extent and nature of back-channeling and turn dynamics.

Label-Free Role-Aware Segmentation of Classroom Recordings: Recovering the Teacher from Transferable Structure2611.23609v1说话人
展开摘要

How much the teacher talks and when are among the most informative and costly-to-measure signals of teaching. Yet, classroom audio is among the most sensitive data an AI system can process: recordings capture minors and fall under GDPR, preventing raw audio from leaving the recording device. We present a teacher-student segmentation pipeline designed to meet exactly this constraint: label-free at inference, running on a recording device, and requiring no per-session calibration. The pipeline chains a lightweight voice activity detector, compactly pretrained speaker embeddings, agglomerative clustering, and a small logistic regression that assigns the teacher role based on session-relative structure rather than voice identity. We show that our structural cue transfers across classrooms, languages, and acoustic conditions. In leave-one-teacher-out cross-validation on 292 recordings from 63 teachers across English and German corpora, our pipeline achieves teacher F1 of 0.81 and 0.87, respectively, outperforming both enrollment-based baselines and a 30-second supervised enrollment ceiling. On a commodity smartphone, its performance matches pyannote, a strong neural diarization tool, while running 4.8x faster at comparable peak memory at a real-time factor of ~0.18. We also find that transcribing filtered audio only marginally increases teacher's word error rate from $0.26$ to 0.28.
