AudioLens: Multi-Perspective Speech Clustering with Reasoning Audio-Language Models

作者：Wenjun Huang, Qiaosong Chu, Tiger Shao, Pengfei Zhang, Yutong Song, Hanning Chen, Yezi Liu, Weiyi Wu, SungHeon Jeong, Ryozo Masukawa, Sanggeon Yun, Yang Ni, Jiang Gui, Mohsen Imani

语音识别评分 22speechaudioacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出AudioLens-R1，一种基于推理蒸馏和偏好优化的端到端大音频语言模型，用于根据自然语言视角对语音进行多视角聚类，并在新基准AudioLens-Bench上取得提升。

解决了什么问题

现有音频聚类依赖固定声学相似度指标或ASR文本管道，难以根据用户指定的不同视角重新组织同一音频集合，尤其当聚类依赖语言与副语言线索时。

核心方法

构建AudioLens-Bench基准；训练AudioLens-R1，端到端大音频语言模型，采用推理蒸馏和偏好优化，根据自然语言视角直接划分语音并推断簇数与分配。

主要创新点

定义音频多视角聚类任务；构建跨应用领域且评估视角内与跨视角泛化的基准；提出原生音频语言模型AudioLens-R1；使用推理蒸馏与偏好优化。

实验结论

实验表明，AudioLens-R1一致优于所有基线，整体ARI提高12.99点，V-measure提高11.62点。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

关注语音聚类、音频语言模型、多视角结构发现和语音组织的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

现有方法依赖固定声学相似度指标或基于ASR的文本管道，缺乏对用户指定视角和副语言线索的灵活处理。

对研究的启发

探索原生音频语言模型在灵活、视角条件下的语音结构发现，以及推理蒸馏和偏好优化在音频任务中的应用。

是否值得精读

提出了新任务与基准，且报告了显著的性能提升，适合关注音频语言模型与聚类的读者深入阅读。

TOP 2

CSAVocoder: A Causal Spatial Audio Vocoder Towards Real-Time Spatial Audio Generation

作者：Zhiyuan Zhu, Han Wang, Wenxiang Guo, Yu Zhang, Changhao Pan, Rui Yang, Zhou Zhao

音频生成评分 16audioaudio generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出CSAVocoder，一种因果GAN空间音频声码器，通过空间适配器和空间一致性判别器联合优化波形保真度与空间渲染，支持恒定内存的流式推理。

解决了什么问题

现有神经声码器多针对单声道设计，直接扩展到空间音频会忽略通道间线索，导致空间质量下降。

核心方法

设计因果GAN声码器，包含融合多通道梅尔谱与动态声源-听者姿态信息的空间适配器、监督通道间线索的空间一致性判别器，以及严格因果、有状态的生成器支持流式推理。

主要创新点

引入空间适配器融合姿态信息；空间一致性判别器监督通道间线索；严格因果有状态生成器实现恒定内存流式推理。

实验结论

在大型空间音频数据集上的实验表明，CSAVocoder提升了空间保真度，同时保持有竞争力的音频质量和实时性能；未提供具体量化指标。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

空间音频生成、神经声码器、实时音频处理研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

大多数神经声码器面向单声道音频，直接扩展到空间音频会因忽略通道间线索而降低空间质量。

对研究的启发

将动态姿态信息与多通道频谱融合，并通过判别器监督空间一致性，可启发其他空间音频生成任务。

是否值得精读

提出专门的空间音频声码器，关注实时性与空间保真度，若关注空间音频生成可阅读。

Fine-Tuning Whisper for Automatic Speech Recognition in Baniwa: A Preliminary Study

作者：Leonardo Duart, Tiago Fonseca, Thiago Chacón

语音识别评分 14speechspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

对Whisper Small进行微调以适应极低资源土著语言Baniwa的自动语音识别，在0.54小时语料上获得37.5% WER和7.45% CER。

解决了什么问题

自动语音识别在高资源语言上表现优异，但土著语言缺乏语音资源和语言技术，Baniwa语料极少且主要由孤立词和短句组成。

核心方法

使用来自语言文档项目的1,373条人工转写录音（约0.54小时），对Whisper Small进行有监督微调，以WER和CER评估。

主要创新点

首次为Baniwa建立ASR基线；验证多语言基础模型可适应极低资源土著语言；使用小规模语料进行微调。

实验结论

最佳模型在Baniwa测试上达到WER 37.5%，CER 7.45%，表明多语言基础模型可成功适应极低资源土著语言。

可能局限

摘要未明确说明；仅提及未来研究需更大数据集、语言特定适应策略和后处理技术。

适合谁读

低资源语言ASR、土著语言技术、语音识别微调研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

大型多语言基础模型（如Whisper）在高资源语言上取得显著性能，但低资源土著语言仍缺乏资源与技术。

对研究的启发

表明预训练多语言模型可通过少量语料微调适应极低资源语言，为其他土著语言ASR提供基线。

是否值得精读

提供了极低资源语言ASR的初步基线，适合关注低资源语音识别的研究者。

Acoustic Echo Control Based on Sound Object Identification for Suppressing Howling Caused by Complicated Acoustic Paths

作者：Osamu Hoshuyama

其他评分 12speechvoiceacoustic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出基于声对象识别的声学回声控制方法，通过默认静音并在信号不重复时放行，抑制复杂声学路径下的啸叫。

解决了什么问题

在多个免提终端共存的会议环境中，传统回声消除器仅针对设备内固定回声路径，难以控制经由终端间通信形成的非预期路径，可能导致啸叫。

核心方法

不估计回声路径，而是识别声对象，默认保持信道静音，仅当信号被判定不与最近观察到的对象相同时才允许通过/播放，从而打破回声环路，可视为经典语音切换向条件半双工的扩展。

主要创新点

将声对象识别引入回声控制；默认静音策略；打破重复声对象引起的回声环路；扩展经典语音切换为条件半双工。

实验结论

仿真显示该方法能够抑制啸叫，但与语音质量存在权衡；未提供具体量化指标。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

声学回声消除、会议音频、语音通信研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

传统声学回声消除器针对固定设备内回声路径，难以处理终端间通信形成的复杂回声路径。

对研究的启发

跳脱回声路径估计，从声对象重复角度控制回声，为复杂会议场景提供新思路。

是否值得精读

提出不同于传统回声消除的思路，适合关注会议音频与啸叫抑制的读者。

AllMusicCaps: Album Reviews as Complementary Supervision for Music CLAP

作者：Pablo Alonso-Jiménez, Xavier Lizarraga-Seijas, Xavier Serra, Dmitry Bogdanov

其他评分 11audiomusic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

利用AllMusic专家专辑评论构建监督语料，通过LLM预处理生成训练用描述，补充文本-音频对比学习（CLAP）训练，提升音乐检索与分类性能。

解决了什么问题

现有开放文本-音频对比模型多使用LLM从标签数据集或网页搜索生成描述，准确但表达范围窄，缺乏叙事线索、评价性形容词和场景框架。

核心方法

使用LLM预处理流水线从AllMusic专家评论中识别描述性音乐引文并改写为训练用描述，构建245,346条样本的语料；训练音乐CLAP并采用SigReg正则化促使嵌入空间呈各向同性高斯分布。

主要创新点

引入人类专家专辑评论作为补充监督；LLM预处理将评论转化为训练描述；重新审视SigReg正则化并展示其在分类探测与检索中的效果。

实验结论

在人类撰写描述基准Song Describer上，专辑评论监督带来最大检索增益，尤其对复杂查询；SigReg正则化改善MLP探测与文本到音乐检索；所得模型在文本到音乐检索、零样本分类和多数MLP探测任务上优于开放CLAP基线；未提供具体量化指标。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音乐信息检索、文本-音频对比学习、多模态表示学习研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

近期开放文本-音频对比模型典型地使用LLM从标签数据集或网页搜索生成描述，准确但表达狭窄，缺乏人类评论中的叙事和评价信息。

对研究的启发

利用领域专家评论作为监督信号，通过LLM预处理转化为训练语料，可改善音乐检索对复杂查询的理解；SigReg正则化也可用于其他对比学习任务。

是否值得精读

使用专家评论作为补充监督，发布了评论衍生数据集和模型权重，适合关注音乐CLAP与多模态检索的研究者。

AudioLens: Multi-Perspective Speech Clustering with Reasoning Audio-Language Models2608.25177v1语音识别
展开摘要

Audio clustering is a fundamental task for organizing rapidly growing speech collections, supporting applications such as conversational analysis and speech-driven discovery. However, existing methods rely on fixed acoustic similarity metrics or ASR-based text pipelines, limiting their ability to reorganize the same audio collection under different user-specified perspectives, especially when clustering depends on both linguistic and paralinguistic cues. We introduce audio multi-perspective clustering, where a model directly partitions speech recordings according to a natural-language perspective while inferring both the number of clusters and their assignments. To study this setting, we construct AudioLens-Bench, a benchmark spanning multiple application domains and evaluating both in-perspective and cross-perspective generalization. We further propose AudioLens-R1, an end-to-end large audio-language model trained with reasoning distillation and preference optimization. Experiments show that AudioLens-R1 consistently outperforms all baselines, improving overall ARI by 12.99 points and V-measure by 11.62 points. These results demonstrate the promise of native audio-language models for flexible, perspective-conditioned structure discovery over speech collections.

CSAVocoder: A Causal Spatial Audio Vocoder Towards Real-Time Spatial Audio Generation2608.25404v1音频生成
展开摘要

Spatial audio vocoders are able to convert mel-spectrograms produced by generative models into spatial audio waveforms. Most neural vocoders are designed for monaural audio, and direct extensions to spatial audio can degrade spatial quality by ignoring inter-channel cues. We present CSAVocoder, a causal GAN-based spatial audio vocoder that jointly optimizes waveform fidelity and spatial rendering. Our framework introduces a Spatial Adaptor that fuses multi-channel mel-spectrograms with dynamic source-listener pose information, together with a spatial consistency discriminator that supervises inter-channel cues. To meet real-time requirements, we design a strictly causal, stateful generator that supports efficient streaming inference with constant memory overhead. Experiments on large-scale spatial audio datasets show that CSAVocoder improves spatial fidelity at competitive audio quality and real-time performance.

Fine-Tuning Whisper for Automatic Speech Recognition in Baniwa: A Preliminary Study2608.26060v1语音识别
展开摘要

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) technologies have achieved remarkable performance in recent years through the use of large multilingual foundation models. However, most advances remain concentrated on high-resource languages, while indigenous languages continue to suffer from a lack of speech resources and language technologies. This work presents a preliminary study on the adaptation of Whisper for Automatic Speech Recognition in Baniwa, an indigenous Arawakan language spoken in Brazil, Colombia, and Venezuela. The experiments were conducted using a corpus of 1,373 manually transcribed recordings obtained from a linguistic documentation project. The corpus contains approximately 0.54 hours of speech and consists primarily of isolated words and short elicited utterances. The Whisper Small model was fine-tuned using supervised learning and evaluated using Word Error Rate (WER) and Character Error Rate (CER). The best model achieved a WER of 37.5% and a CER of 7.45%, demonstrating that multilingual foundation models can be successfully adapted to extremely low-resource indigenous languages. The results establish an initial baseline for Baniwa Automatic Speech Recognition and provide a foundation for future research involving larger datasets, language-specific adaptation strategies, and post-processing techniques.

Acoustic Echo Control Based on Sound Object Identification for Suppressing Howling Caused by Complicated Acoustic Paths2608.25413v1其他
展开摘要

This paper proposes acoustic echo control based on sound object identification for suppressing acoustic echo and howling in conferencing environments with complicated acoustic paths, where multiple hands-free terminals coexist in the same room. Conventional acoustic echo cancellers target fixed intra-device echo paths; however, unintended paths, for example, those formed via inter-terminal communication, are difficult to control and can lead to howling. Instead of estimating echo paths, the proposed approach identifies sound objects and keeps channels muted by default, allowing pass/playback only when the signal is judged not dentical to recently observed objects. This breaks echo loops caused by repeated reproduction of the same sound object and can be viewed as an extension of classical voice switching toward conditional half-duplex operation. Technical challenges and connections to related techniques are discussed, and a simulation shows howling suppression together with a trade-off against speech quality.

AllMusicCaps: Album Reviews as Complementary Supervision for Music CLAP2608.25244v1其他
展开摘要

Recent open text-audio contrastive models (CLAPs) are typically trained with LLM-generated captions derived from tag datasets or web search results, which tend to be accurate but expressively narrow. As a complementary source, we explore human-written album reviews, specifically expert reviews from AllMusic: they exist at scale and carry narrative cues, evaluative adjectives, and scene framing that other sources lack. Since raw reviews are too noisy for direct use as captions, we first build a caption corpus with 24,5346 samples via an LLM preprocessing pipeline that identifies descriptive musical quotes and rewrites them into training-ready captions. We find that album review supervision yields the largest retrieval gains on a human-written caption benchmark (Song Describer), particularly for complex queries that other existing caption datasets leave uncovered. In addition, we revisit the training recipe and show that SigReg regularization, which encourages an isotropic Gaussian distribution in the embedding space, improves MLP probing across classification tasks, as well as text-to-music retrieval. The resulting model outperforms open CLAP-style baselines on text-to-music retrieval, zero-shot classification, and most MLP probing tasks. We release the review-derived caption dataset and model weights to support future research.

VoiceMem: Streaming Dual-Brain Memory for Real-Time Interaction2608.26005v1其他
展开摘要

Conversational systems, such as duplex speech language models (SLMs), still lack a streaming, accurate, and empathetic memory system as their soul. We introduce VoiceMem, a simple memory architecture with a parallel informational left brain, an emotional right brain, and streaming memory I/O mechanisms. We further build a complete pipeline for memory-aware SLM training, long-horizon evaluation, and decoupled deployment with interchangeable memory backends. Experiments and real-world deployment show three advantages: i) Accuracy: under top-5 retrieval, the left brain outperforms classical systems such as Mem0 at top-200 by nearly 30 points; ii) Emotional & Personal: the right brain, with short- and long-horizon affective attribution and dual-node persona modeling, achieves state-of-the-art performance across three persona benchmarks and improves the aggregate score by 4.29 points over the previous best system; and iii) Real-Time & Cheap: VoiceMem completes retrieval in 134 ms, well within standard VAD latency, adding no extra conversational delay while maintaining high accuracy and low cost. These results show that VoiceMem provides a practical memory foundation for real-time, personalized, and emotionally aware speech interaction.

Dissonance Spectrum explicitly models perceptual frequency interactions for better music understanding2608.25621v1其他
展开摘要

Conventional music representations describe acoustic energy over time and frequency but do not explicitly expose relations among simultaneous frequency components. We introduce the \emph{Dissonance Spectrum} (DS), a nonnegative time--frequency representation that applies a tolerance-based rational pitch-relation kernel with logarithmic harmonic distance to a constant-Q spectrum and attributes aggregate pairwise interactions back to individual frequency bins. Controlled music-theory tests show strong ordinal agreement for intervals, harmonic-function connections, and church modes, and weaker but significant agreement across diverse chord voicings. DS is then encoded by a lightweight parallel branch whose zero-initialized residual projection preserves the baseline function at initialization. Across six paired training seeds in open-ended music question answering and categorical and dimensional music emotion recognition, DS obtains the highest mean on every reported endpoint relative to the unchanged baseline, a parameter-matched Gaussian-input branch, and an architecture-matched magnitude-CQT branch. These results support DS as an interpretable, complementary representation, while listener-specific perception and broader task coverage remain open problems.

A Training-Free Proactive Defense Against Partial Speech Manipulation via Self-Embedding Steganography2608.25285v1其他
展开摘要

Partial deepfake speech, where only limited segments of an utterance are synthesized or manipulated, poses a significant challenge to existing deepfake detection systems. As the proportion of spoofed regions decreases, passive detectors become increasingly unreliable, and accurate detection and restoration remain challenging. In this paper, we revisit audio steganography from a new perspective and propose its use as a proactive defense against partially deepfaked audio. In particular, we consider a self-embedding strategy in which a clean speech signal embeds a compressed representation of itself, enabling post-hoc extraction of reference content. We demonstrate how existing audio steganography methods can be repurposed to support detection of partial deepfakes through codec-based restoration.
