MIRA: A Musical Intent Refinement Agent for Aligning Text-to-Music Generation with User Intent

作者：Zekai Liu, Zhilin Wang, Xuzheng He, Yu Cheng, Yang Yang

音乐生成评分 18audiomusicmusic generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出测试时意图细化代理 MIRA，通过逐项评分和树搜索在文本到音乐生成中对齐用户意图，并构建基准 MuRA-Bench。

解决了什么问题

现有文本到音乐生成的全局文本-音频相关性评分难以捕捉欠指定提示中的隐含意图，也无法诊断乐器、结构、节奏或情绪进展等具体要求的失败。

核心方法

将意图对齐形式化为满足逐项可验证的评分表，构建 MuRA-Bench；MIRA 在测试时先将请求意图落地为评分表，再在有限预算内对黑盒生成器进行提示修订搜索，迭代生成音乐、根据评分表验证，并用反馈指导轨迹感知树搜索。

主要创新点

提出逐项验证的意图对齐框架，构建专家策划的真实请求基准 MuRA-Bench；设计测试时代理 MIRA，无需训练即可结合评分表和树搜索优化提示，提升黑盒生成器的意图对齐能力。

实验结论

在开源和商业后端上的实验显示 MIRA 改善了意图对齐，使开源生成器达到与代表性商业系统（如 Suno 和 Mureka）相当的性能。

Steerspeech: Activation Steering For Emotion Control In Generated Speech

作者：Afsara Benazir, Darius Pétermann, Felix Xiaozhu Lin, Salar Rahili

语音合成评分 17speechaudiospeakertext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出激活引导框架 SteerSpeech，通过注入低秩变换引导向量在推理时控制生成语音的情感，同时保持说话人身份和语言内容。

解决了什么问题

预训练 TTS 模型生成表现力语音，但推理时情感控制不可靠：提示和参考音频提供粗糙且不一致的控制，专门条件化和模型适应需要昂贵训练。

核心方法

对每个目标情感训练轻量低秩变换，使用多专家目标鼓励单调情感控制、保持说话人身份和语言内容、约束引导漂移，并冻结 TTS 骨干；引入两遍生成-回放管线及直通估计器，通过离散语音 token 反向传播专家监督以优化变换；推理时将目标情感引导方向与对应变换优化后注入基础 TTS 模型。

主要创新点

训练轻量低秩变换而非调整模型，设计多专家目标实现连续情感控制同时限制说话人和内容退化，提出两遍生成-回放与直通估计解决离散 token 优化问题。

实验结论

在 Qwen3-TTS 上对已见、未见和带口音说话人的客观和主观评估显示，SteerSpeech 提供更强的连续情感控制，且说话人和内容退化有限；达到基线的 1.08x-7.12x 目标情感得分，代表性情感主观上获得 78.1%-96.8% 强度偏好，高引导强度下说话人身份保持达 1.43x-1.46x。

对研究的启发

激活引导与低秩变换结合，为在不更新大模型的情况下实现细粒度属性控制提供了可参考的路径。

Tracing Inputs, Verifying Outputs: Validating Attribution in Music Generation

作者：Taejun Kim, Wonil Kim, Jongmin Jung, Hyeongseok Wi, Sangeun Kum, Keunhyoung Luke Kim, Taehyoung Kim, Dongjoo Moon, Seungsoon Park, Taewan Kim, Virginie Berger, Juhan Nam, Jongpil Lee

音乐生成评分 17audiomusicmusic generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出通过仅以音频输入条件生成并追踪输入影响，结合音乐版本识别模型 musicDNA 审计记忆，为 AI 生成音乐提供可验证的归因证据。

解决了什么问题

AI 生成音乐中难以验证哪些音乐来源对输出有贡献，输入归因需要可验证证据。

核心方法

仅用音频条件生成（无文本输入），追踪每个输出背后的输入并建立其音乐效果；在提示遵循测试和受控输入交换中评估 MixAudio 生成的 stem 在音色上遵循提示音频、和声上遵循上下文音频；同时使用音乐版本识别模型 musicDNA 审计训练数据复现情况。

主要创新点

提出基于音频输入的归因验证框架，结合输入追踪与输出分析；引入音乐版本识别模型 musicDNA 用于记忆审计，在标记池上比测试过的其他检测器具有更高精确率和召回率。

实验结论

提示遵循测试和受控输入交换表明 MixAudio 生成的 stem 在音色上遵循提示音频、和声上遵循上下文音频；musicDNA 发现输入记录外有少量复现，且在人类判定的标记池中取得更高精确率和召回率，两种评估提供互补的归因证据。

可能局限

输出仍可能复现训练数据，但摘要未明确说明其他限制

适合谁读

关注 AI 音乐版权归因、生成来源追踪与记忆审计的研究者、平台和权利方。

和已有工作的关系

摘要未列出具体相关工作，但提到与记忆检测器的比较，以及作为未来权利报告和补偿的基础。

对研究的启发

将输入归因与记忆审计相结合，为生成模型提供可验证证据的思路可推广到其他内容生成领域的版权与来源验证。

Mitigating Accent-Language Confusion in Self-Supervised Speech Representations for Language Identification

作者：Minu Kim, Jihwan Lee, David R. Mortensen, Shrikanth Narayanan

自监督学习评分 14speechspeakerself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出一个几何投影方法，仅用母语语音估计 L1 偏置方向并从自监督语音表示中去除，从而修复口音引起的语言识别混淆。

解决了什么问题

从自监督语音表示微调的语言识别模型常将非母语（L2）语音误分类为说话人的第一语言（L1），因为非母语表示位于母语目标语言和母语 L1 极点之间。

核心方法

证明非母语表示处于母语目标语言与母语 L1 之间；引入几何投影，仅从母语语音估计 L1 偏置方向，并在冻结的 LID 头之前将其移除。

主要创新点

直接在校正表示空间中消除口音引起的 L1 偏置，无需 L2 训练数据或模型适配；方法只在母语语音上估计偏置方向。

实验结论

在五个 MMS-LID 模型和非母语语料库上，该投影大幅提升 L2 口音语音的目标语言识别，同时保持母语语音预测不变。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明

适合谁读

关注自监督语音表示、语言识别和口音鲁棒性的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

摘要未列出具体文献，但涉及自监督语音表示和 MMS-LID 模型，提及与现有微调 LID 模型的比较。

对研究的启发

表示空间中的偏置方向估计与去除提供了一种无需额外标注数据的轻量修复策略，可启发其他口音或域偏移问题。

Dialect-Robust Speech Language Models with Synthetic Pseudo-Dialect Augmentation

作者：Shunsuke Mitsumori, Tomoya Mizumoto, Yusuke Fujita

语音合成评分 13speechtext-to-speechspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出通过将 LLM 生成的方言文本经由标准语言 TTS 合成伪方言语音，并在训练中引入中间标准文本预测，以提升语音语言模型在方言上的鲁棒性，且无需真实方言语音。

解决了什么问题

语音语言模型在方言上性能下降，因为数据稀缺；传统 TTS 增强需要一定量的真实方言语音，难以覆盖多样方言。

核心方法

合成伪方言语音：由 LLM 生成方言文本，通过标准语言 TTS 模型转换为语音，零真实方言语音需求；训练时增加中间标准文本预测作为语义归一化，辅助下游任务。

主要创新点

提出零真实方言语音的伪方言合成增强策略；引入中间标准文本预测作为语义归一化，桥接方言与标准语言之间的语义差距。

实验结论

在日语、德语和汉语方言的方言到英语语音翻译中，相比合成标准语音基线，伪方言增强将日语从 25.38 提升至 26.24，德语从 31.57 提升至 32.47；中间标准文本预测进一步将日语提升至 28.26，汉语从 11.67 提升至 16.37。

MIRA: A Musical Intent Refinement Agent for Aligning Text-to-Music Generation with User Intent2610.10355v1音乐生成
展开摘要

Text-to-music systems produce increasingly convincing audio, yet evaluation reveals little about whether the result matches user intent. A global text-audio relevance score can overlook the implicit intent in underspecified prompts and mask failures in specific requirements, such as instrumentation, structure, rhythm, or mood progression. To bridge this gap, we formulate text-to-music intent alignment as satisfying a per-request rubric of independently verifiable items covering both a request's explicit requirements and its implied musical intent. Scoring items individually makes evaluation diagnostic by intent source and musical dimension, rather than a single opaque score. We instantiate this as MuRA-Bench, a benchmark of real-world platform requests curated by music experts. We further propose MIRA (Musical Intent Refinement Agent), a test-time agent that first grounds a request's intent into rubrics, then searches over prompt revisions for a black-box generator under a bounded budget, iteratively generating music, verifying it against the rubrics, and using this feedback to guide a trajectory-aware tree search. Experiments across open-source and commercial backends show that MIRA improves intent alignment, enabling an open-source generator to achieve performance comparable to representative commercial systems (e.g. Suno and Mureka). Project page: https://mirareview.github.io/.

Steerspeech: Activation Steering For Emotion Control In Generated Speech2610.10415v1语音合成
展开摘要

Pretrained text-to-speech (TTS) models can generate expressive speech, but reliable inference-time emotion control remains challenging: prompts and reference audio offer coarse, inconsistent control, whereas specialized conditioning and model adaptation require costly training. We present SteerSpeech, a lightweight activation-steering framework that controls emotion by injecting steering vectors into hidden activations. For each target emotion we train a lightweight low-rank transform, using a multi-expert objective that encourages monotonic emotion control while preserving speaker identity and linguistic content, constraining steering drift, and keeping the TTS backbone frozen. To optimize through discrete speech tokens, we introduce a two-pass generation-and-replay pipeline using a straight-through estimator to backpropagate expert supervision through sampled tokens. At inference, a target-emotion steering direction is optimized with its respective transform and injected into the base TTS model. Objective and subjective evaluations with Qwen3-TTS across seen, unseen, and accented speakers show stronger continuous emotion control with limited speaker and content degradation. SteerSpeech achieves 1.08x-7.12x baseline target-emotion scores and for a representative emotion subjectively, it receives 78.1%-96.8% intensity preference and 1.43x-1.46x speaker-identity preservation at high steering strengths.

Tracing Inputs, Verifying Outputs: Validating Attribution in Music Generation2610.09637v1音乐生成
展开摘要

How can we verify whose music contributed to an AI-generated output? This paper demonstrates how input-based attribution can provide verifiable evidence of which audio sources were used in a generation and whether they shaped the output. To do so, we condition the generation solely on audio without any text input, then trace the inputs behind each output, and establish their musical effect. In prompt adherence tests and controlled input swaps, the stems generated by our generator, MixAudio, follow the prompt audio in timbre and the context audio in harmony. Yet these outputs may still reproduce training data not supplied as inputs. We therefore audit memorization with our musical version identification model, musicDNA, and find few reproductions outside the input records. On human-judged cases within the flagged pool, it achieves higher precision and recall than the other tested memorization detectors. The two evaluations suggest that input records and output analysis provide complementary evidence for attribution, on which rights-holder reporting and compensation can draw as the AI music economy takes shape. Audio examples are available at https://neutune.github.io/attr2027demo/

Mitigating Accent-Language Confusion in Self-Supervised Speech Representations for Language Identification2610.09486v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Spoken language identification (LID) aims to recognize the target language regardless of accent. In practice, however, LID models fine-tuned from self-supervised speech representations frequently confuse accents with languages, misclassifying non-native (L2) speech as the speaker's first language (L1). We show that non-native speech representations lie between native target-language and native L1 poles, causing systematic misclassification. To address this, we introduce a geometric projection that estimates an L1-bias direction solely from native speech and removes it before the frozen LID head. Across five MMS-LID models and non-native corpora, this projection substantially improves target language identification for L2-accented speech while preserving predictions for native speech. These results show that accent-induced L1 bias can be corrected directly within the representation space without L2 training data or model adaptation.

Dialect-Robust Speech Language Models with Synthetic Pseudo-Dialect Augmentation2610.09321v1语音合成
展开摘要

Speech Language Model (SLM) performance often degrades on dialects due to data scarcity. Conventional text-to-speech (TTS) augmentation struggles to cover diverse dialects as it requires a certain amount of real dialect speech. We propose synthesizing pseudo-dialect speech by converting LLM-generated dialect text via a standard-language TTS model, requiring zero real dialect speech. Additionally, we introduce intermediate standard-text prediction during training, acting as semantic normalization for downstream tasks. We evaluate dialect understanding via dialect-to-English speech translation across Japanese, German, and Chinese dialects. Compared to synthetic standard speech baselines, pseudo-dialect augmentation improves scores for Japanese (from 25.38 to 26.24) and German (from 31.57 to 32.47). Furthermore, the intermediate standard-text prediction effectively bridges the semantic gap, boosting performance to 28.26 for Japanese and from 11.67 to 16.37 for Chinese. These results suggest that our approach scales to various languages without requiring speech resources specific to each dialect.

LIFT-SE: Linguistic Inference Followed by Flow Transformation for Generative Speech Enhancement2610.09963v1语音增强
展开摘要

Generative speech enhancement (SE) is prone to linguistic hallucination when semantic constraint is unreliable under severe noise and reverberation. Moreover, approaches that generate discrete codec tokens are bounded by the quantization error of the codec decoder, regardless of token-prediction accuracy. We propose LIFT-SE, a two-stage generative framework that decouples linguistic inference from acoustic synthesis within QRes-Codec, which exposes a quantized latent and its residual-completed continuous form. The first stage predicts clean codec tokens autoregressively, conditioned on frame-aligned features from a self-supervised front-end distilled toward clean speech.
