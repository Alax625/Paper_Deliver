Self-Supervised Speech Representations for Cross-Speaker Dysarthria Detection During Awake Craniotomy

作者：Kanthila Chinmayi, Abdallah Nassib, Misy Harrison, Panheleux Celine, Saliou Vanessa, Seizeur Romuald, Dardenne Guillaume

自监督学习评分 26speechacousticspeakerself-supervised查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

在清醒开颅手术低资源语音数据中，自监督语音表示相比传统声学描述符显著提升跨说话者构音障碍检测性能，且语音表示质量比分类器复杂度更重要。

解决了什么问题

清醒开颅手术中自动检测言语障碍面临手术室录音声学干扰大、临床相关事件罕见、可用队列小且跨说话者异质等挑战。

核心方法

流程包括说话者分离以隔离患者语音，多视图表示结合手工声学描述符与多层 wav2vec 2.0 嵌入，说话者条件归一化与基于可转移性的特征选择，以及梯度提升第一阶段与神经第二阶段组成的级联分类器。

主要创新点

系统组分评估揭示了表示质量的重要性；将多层自监督表示与手工特征结合，并通过说话者条件归一化和特征选择提升跨说话者鲁棒性；提出级联分类器。

实验结论

在留一说话者交叉验证下，三个分类器 AUC 差异不超过 4.7%；用多层自监督表示替换传统声学描述符使 AUC 提高 18.2%-26.1%；分离条件特征提取和级联分类器提供额外一致增益。

适合谁读

和已有工作的关系

摘要未提及具体相关文献，仅描述现有自动化检测面临的挑战。

对研究的启发

在低资源医疗语音场景中，应优先投入可靠的语音隔离和强预训练表示，而非复杂分类器；术前校准可能进一步提升性能。

Beyond Speech Captions: Speech-Rewarded Style Planning for Conversational Text-to-Speech

作者：Shiao Zhu, Lianbo Liu, Sizhen Lyu, Yuzhe Wang, Sheng Li, Takahiro Shinozaki

语音合成评分 23speechaudioacoustictext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出 Speech-Rewarded Style Planning (SRSP)，通过冻结下游 TTS 模型并以语音令牌似然为奖励训练风格规划器，在对话 TTS 中实现更好的风格与情感控制。

解决了什么问题

自然语言风格描述作为伪标签会压缩目标声学信息，且描述保真度与对特定合成器的控制效果并不一致；经验表明语音-文本对齐对下游声学相似度预测弱。

核心方法

SRSP 使用冻结的下游 TTS 模型训练基于文本的风格规划器；给定对话历史和响应文本，规划器生成候选指令，通过组相对策略优化（GRPO）优化，以目标语音令牌的教师强制似然作为奖励。

主要创新点

直接以语音令牌似然作为奖励信号，避免文本伪标签的信息压缩；将风格规划与下游 TTS 模型解耦，优化可解释的指令规划。

实验结论

在 ISCSLP 2026 CoT-TTS 语料库英语子集上，SRSP 相比 Base LLM 和目标音频信息描述基线，语音风格和情感相似度更高，mel 倒谱失真更低；LLM 评估显示上下文适当性和参考一致性优于所有基线。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音合成、对话系统、LLM 控制 TTS、强化学习应用的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中对比了 Base LLM 和目标音频信息描述基线，但未提供具体文献。

对研究的启发

用下游任务的真实反馈（如语音似然）替代伪标签，可缓解文本描述与声学控制之间的鸿沟。

是否值得精读

值得深入阅读，因为提出了一种新的训练范式，并在多个指标上取得一致提升。

Edit Who Speaks, Control How They Speak: Global Timbre Editing and Local Instruction Control for TTS

作者：Junchuan Zhao, Chenglin Xu, Wei Zeng, Haoyang Li, Yiwen Guo, Ye Wang

语音合成评分 22speechaudiovoiceacousticspeakertext-to-speech查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

提出 EDICT 框架，通过编辑后的声学参考和段边界 KV 缓存刷新，统一 TTS 中的全局音色编辑与局部表达控制。

解决了什么问题

现有指令式 TTS 接口要么只能克隆参考语音或从文本设计新语音，不能直接修改给定参考音色；话语级表达指令对片段级变化欠指定。

核心方法

EDICT 将参考音频与结构化音色编辑结合，在编解码器令牌空间生成编辑后的参考；该参考作为冻结 TTS 骨干的共享语音锚点，允许片段级自然语言指令引导表达；在每个段边界重建 KV 缓存以刷新指令条件并保留有界声学上下文。

主要创新点

首次统一全局音色编辑与局部表达控制；利用段边界 KV 缓存刷新兼顾指令切换与声学连续性；提出 TimbreEdit-Bench 和 IntraTTS-Bench 两个基准。

实验结论

在 TimbreEdit-Bench 和 IntraTTS-Bench 上，EDICT 改进了音色编辑，并在局部指令遵循、说话者一致性和转换质量之间取得有利平衡。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

文本到语音合成、语音编辑、人机交互、可控生成的研究人员。

和已有工作的关系

摘要中提到了语音克隆和基于文本的语音设计接口，但未提供具体文献。

对研究的启发

通过编辑声学参考并维持句子级一致性，可实现细粒度多属性语音控制；段边界缓存策略可用于其他流式或分段生成任务。

是否值得精读

值得深入阅读，因为提出了统一的编辑框架和配套基准，对可控 TTS 有重要参考价值。

STEMMA: Song-to-Stem Multi-Audio Reasoning for Large Audio Language Models

作者：Hoyeol Sohn, Wonil Kim, Keunhyoung Kim, Sangeun Kum, Taehyoung Kim, Dongjoo Moon, Theerasak Charoenchob, Teeratep Weerapang, Jongpil Lee, Juhan Nam

其他评分 15audiomusic查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

STEMMA 框架利用制作来源关系构建多音频音乐问答数据，通过关系优先采样和目录标签提升大音频语言模型对歌曲间、段落间和分轨间关系的推理能力。

解决了什么问题

现有大音频语言模型和音乐问答数据集通常基于单个录音或独立采样音轨，缺乏已知的制作关系，难以进行歌曲、段落和分轨之间的比较推理。

核心方法

STEMMA 采用关系优先构建策略：先指定目标关系（如同轨同段、分轨属于哪个混音），再从目录中查询满足该关系的片段和硬负例；标签直接由目录来源确定而非语言模型生成；构建 STEMMA-Bench 和轨道不相交的 STEMMA-Instruct 训练集。

主要创新点

引入制作来源作为关系监督信号；关系优先采样克服稀疏性问题；目录确定标签避免模型生成噪声。

实验结论

在 STEMMA-Instruct 上微调两个 LALM 后，多音频推理能力提升，在直接由目录确定的结构关系上增益最大，同时保持单音频音乐理解性能。

和已有工作的关系

对研究的启发

利用真实世界实体关系（如制作来源）构建训练数据，可显著增强模型的结构化推理能力；关系优先采样适用于其他稀疏关系场景。

值得深入阅读，因为提出了新的数据构建范式和评测基准，对多音频理解有潜在推动作用。

Phonologically Informed Tokenization for German Speech Recognition: A Cross-Domain Study

作者：Christopher Witzl, Tobias Bocklet, Korbinian Riedhammer

语音识别评分 14speechacousticspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

在德语跨域语音识别中，语音信息标记化在域内与 BPE 和字符基线相当，但在域偏移且小词汇量下优势明显，且声学编码器主导错误拓扑。

解决了什么问题

德语形态丰富，音节结构可预测，但标记器选择对端到端语音识别跨域性能的影响尚不明确。

核心方法

在 Omnilingual ASR wav2vec 2.0 骨干上用 CTC 微调，比较三种标记器家族：预训练多语言字符、基于拼写的 BPE、以及由 Pyphen 音节化和字素到音素转换得到的语音信息单元；在域内朗读、方言自发、标准德语自发三个测试集上评估。

主要创新点

系统比较语音信息标记化与常用标记器在跨域条件下的表现；揭示词汇量大小与域偏移的交互作用；通过音素级混淆分析指出声学编码器主导错误。

实验结论

域内所有语音信息标记器与 BPE 和多语言字符基线在 WER/CER 上相当；域偏移下，小词汇量时音节感知标记化在方言语音（保留音节结构）和自发语音（新词词汇外）上保持领先；混淆分析显示所有标记器犯相同功能词错误，表明声学编码器主导错误拓扑。

标记器选择应结合词汇预算和部署时的分布偏移来考虑，而非追求单一最优；语音信息标记化可作为针对形态丰富语言的有效选项。

Self-Supervised Speech Representations for Cross-Speaker Dysarthria Detection During Awake Craniotomy2610.11825v1自监督学习
展开摘要

Detecting intra-operative speech impairment during awake craniotomy is essential for preserving language function. However, automated detection remains challenging because operating-room recordings contain substantial acoustic interference, clinically relevant speech events are rare, and available cohorts are small and heterogeneous across speakers. This study presents a systematic component-wise evaluation of a pipeline for distinguishing dysarthric from no-trouble speech in the DATABRASE corpus of awake-craniotomy recordings. The pipeline incorporates speaker diarization to isolate patient speech, a multi-view representation combining handcrafted acoustic descriptors with multilayer wav2vec 2.0 embeddings, speaker-conditional normalization and transferability-based feature selection to improve cross-speaker robustness, and a cascaded classifier comprising a gradient-boosted first stage and a neural second stage. Evaluation was conducted under strict speaker-independent conditions using leave-one-speaker-out cross-validation. The results show that cross-speaker performance is influenced more strongly by the speech representation than by classifier choice. The AUCs of three classifiers differed by no more than 4.7%, whereas replacing conventional acoustic descriptors with the multilayer self-supervised representation produced AUC improvements of 18.2%-26.1%. Diarization-conditioned feature extraction and the proposed classifier cascade provided additional consistent gains. These findings indicate that reliable patient-specific speech isolation and strong pretrained representations are more important than increased classifier complexity in low-resource intra-operative settings. They also quantify the potential performance gains that may be achieved through patient-specific preoperative calibration.

Beyond Speech Captions: Speech-Rewarded Style Planning for Conversational Text-to-Speech2610.11461v1语音合成
展开摘要

Natural-language style descriptions provide an interpretable interface between large language models (LLMs) and controllable text-to-speech (TTS). However, using descriptions as pseudo-labels compresses target acoustics into text, and descriptive fidelity need not imply effective control of a particular synthesizer. We empirically show that speech-text alignment only weakly predicts downstream acoustic similarity among candidate instructions for the same utterance. We therefore propose Speech-Rewarded Style Planning (SRSP), which trains a text-based style planner through a frozen downstream TTS model. Given dialogue history and response text, the planner generates candidate instructions and is optimized with group-relative policy optimization (GRPO), using the teacher-forced likelihood of target speech tokens as the reward. On an English subset of the ISCSLP 2026 CoT-TTS corpus, SRSP achieves higher speech-style and emotion similarity to target speech and lower mel-cepstral distortion than the Base LLM and target-audio-informed captioning baselines. LLM-based expressive speech evaluation further shows gains over all baselines in contextual appropriateness and reference consistency.

Edit Who Speaks, Control How They Speak: Global Timbre Editing and Local Instruction Control for TTS2610.11437v1语音合成
展开摘要

Instruction-based text-to-speech (TTS) offers control over voice characteristics and speech expression through interfaces including voice cloning and text-based voice design. Voice cloning reproduces a reference voice, whereas text-based voice design creates a voice from a natural-language description. However, neither interface directly enables users to modify the timbre of a given reference and synthesize speech with the modified voice. Meanwhile, utterance-level expressive instructions leave changes across individual text segments underspecified. We introduce \textbf{EDICT}, a framework that unifies global timbre editing and local expressive control by using an edited acoustic reference to anchor voice identity across segments. To enable synthesis with an instruction-edited voice, EDICT combines reference audio with structured timbre edits to generate an edited reference in codec-token space. This representation serves as a shared voice anchor for a frozen TTS backbone, allowing segment-specific natural-language instructions to guide expression. To accommodate instruction changes while supporting acoustic continuity, EDICT rebuilds the KV cache at each segment boundary, refreshing instruction conditioning while retaining bounded acoustic context from previously generated speech. Evaluations on our proposed TimbreEdit-Bench and IntraTTS-Bench demonstrate improved timbre editing and a favorable balance between local instruction adherence, speaker consistency, and transition quality. Audio demos are available.

STEMMA: Song-to-Stem Multi-Audio Reasoning for Large Audio Language Models2610.11884v1其他
展开摘要

Music understanding often requires comparing excerpts and reasoning about relationships among songs, sections, and stems. However, existing large audio-language models (LALMs) and music question-answering datasets typically operate on single recordings or compare independently sampled tracks with no known production relationship. We introduce STEMMA, a multi-audio music question-answering framework built around production provenance: whether excerpts originate from the same track or section, and which stems belong to which mixtures. Because such relations are sparse under conventional audio-first sampling, STEMMA adopts a relation-first construction strategy: it first specifies a target relation and then queries the catalog for excerpts that satisfy it and hard negatives that do not. Labels are determined directly from catalog provenance rather than generated by a language model from metadata. We build STEMMA-Bench for evaluation and a track-disjoint training set, STEMMA-Instruct. Fine-tuning two LALMs on STEMMA-Instruct improves multi-audio reasoning, with the largest gains on structural relations directly determined by the catalog, while preserving single-audio music understanding.

Phonologically Informed Tokenization for German Speech Recognition: A Cross-Domain Study2610.11646v1语音识别
展开摘要

German is a morphologically rich language whose syllable structure is exceptionally well-predicted by the Knuth--Liang hyphenation algorithm. We ask whether phonologically informed tokenization can serve as a competitive target for end-to-end speech recognition. We compare three tokenizer families on the Omnilingual ASR wav2vec 2.0 backbone fine-tuned with CTC: the pretrained multilingual character inventory, a data-driven Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) over orthography, and phonologically informed units from Pyphen syllabification and grapheme-to-phoneme conversion. Across 40 fine-tunes, we evaluate on three German test sets spanning orthogonal shifts: in-domain read speech, dialectal spontaneous speech, and standard-German spontaneous speech. In-domain, all phonologically informed tokenizers match BPE and the multilingual character baseline on both WER and CER. Under domain shift the picture splits along vocabulary size rather than the linguistic axis of variation: at small vocabularies, syllable-aware tokenization improves on dialectal speech, where phonetic surface forms vary but syllable structure is preserved, and stays ahead on spontaneous speech, where new word-forms violate vocabulary closure. A phoneme-level confusion analysis further shows that all tokenizers commit the same canonical function-word errors, indicating that the acoustic encoder, not the tokenizer, dominates the error topology. Our findings suggest that tokenizer choice may depend on the vocabulary budget as much as on the distribution shift expected at deployment rather than reducing to a single universal optimum.

SepGen: Multi-Stem Audio-Video Separation and Generation in a Single Model2610.11361v1其他
展开摘要

A 4D audio-visual scene comprises a video, the dynamic geometry it depicts, and the sound sources that populate it, each with its own position and trajectory. Rendering such a scene from a novel viewpoint requires that every source be available as an individual waveform, so that it can be localized in the scene and propagated to the observer before the signals are mixed. Joint audio-video generators can synthesize the video and its soundtrack, but the soundtrack is emitted as a single audio-mix in which the sources are not individually accessible.
