Alania-2: Turkish text-to-speech with instant and fine-tuned voice cloning

作者：Sezgin Saygili, Emre Kaplaner, Oncel Ozgul, Fikri San Koktas

语音合成评分 30speechaudiovoicespeakertext-to-speechspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

Alania-2是一个具有即时和微调声音克隆功能的土耳其语文本到语音系统，通过文本归一化、条件策略和推理优化在清晰度和延迟上表现优异。

解决了什么问题

土耳其语文本到语音中数字、缩写和外国名字的归一化不足导致关键信息丢失；现有系统清晰度较低；声音克隆中跳过词问题严重；推理延迟较高。

核心方法

采用488M参数VoxCPM架构，在语言模型骨干上添加扩散头生成连续音频潜变量；在18,195小时土耳其语音（90%为合成语音）上从零训练；引入文本归一化器；声音克隆仅条件于参考音频而非延续；使用低秩适配器微调；优化推理引擎。

主要创新点

发现仅条件于参考音频可将跳过词从21.9%降至1.7%；文本归一化将数字关键信息保留从0/96提升至86/96；7.6M参数模型匹配最清晰系统，表明数据比模型大小更重要；推理首音频延迟低至32ms。

实验结论

在Freya-TR-Eval上词错误率1.44%（之前系统5.99%）；文本归一化使关键部分保留从0/96提升至86/96；声音克隆跳过词从21.9%降至1.7%；20-30秒参考音频最佳；低秩适配器训练约两分钟显著提升说话人相似度；7.6M参数模型匹配最清晰系统。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

关注土耳其语语音合成、低资源语言TTS、声音克隆和高效推理的研究者与工程师。

和已有工作的关系

与作者之前的系统（词错误率5.99%）和2.3B参数模型进行了比较。

对研究的启发

训练数据质量对清晰度的影响可能大于模型大小；文本归一化对TTS准确性至关重要；声音克隆中条件策略的选择影响鲁棒性；低秩适配器可高效微调。

是否值得精读

包含实用的声音克隆发现（跳过词降低、参考音频时长）和极低推理延迟，对实际部署有参考价值。

TOP 2

Walrus Technical Report

作者：Alkis Koudounas, Osamu Take, Hayato Futami, Quentin Jodelet, Emiru Tsunoo

语音识别评分 22speechaudiotext-to-speechspeech recognitionspeech translation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

Walrus是一个8B统一语音音频基础模型，通过数据中心训练配方在多种语音和音频任务上取得领先性能。

解决了什么问题

多任务语音音频建模中缺乏有效平衡不同任务的数据策略，现有模型质量可能受限于架构而忽视数据。

核心方法

使用标准解码器仅骨干网络（初始化自Gemma 4），预测低比特率离散语音token并用轻量级token-to-waveform解码器合成音频；采用三阶段数据课程，从广泛多语言数据到多说话人、代码切换和领域特定数据；使用任务感知和数据集感知加权。

主要创新点

主要贡献是数据中心训练配方而非新架构；三阶段课程和加权策略可平衡任务并防止核心技能遗忘；在较小模型上匹配或超越更大模型性能。

实验结论

在大多数多语言、多说话人、代码切换ASR、语音翻译和音频理解基准上领先，超越同规模开放语音语言模型和专用任务模型；甚至匹配或超越4倍大的Qwen3-Omni。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

从事多任务语音和音频基础模型、数据工程、多语言ASR和音频理解的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与同规模开放语音语言模型、专用任务模型以及4倍大的Qwen3-Omni进行了比较。

对研究的启发

数据工程是多任务语音音频模型质量的主要驱动因素；课程学习和任务加权可有效训练大规模多任务模型。

是否值得精读

强调数据而非架构的实证研究，对理解大规模多任务语音模型训练有重要参考价值。

TOP 3

Earlybird: Predictive Streaming Speech Recognition in 29M Parameters

作者：Sahil Mahendrakar

语音识别评分 18speechaudiovoicespeakerspeech recognition查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

Earlybird是一个29M参数的预测性流式语音识别器，能在说话人完成单词之前进行预测，显著降低输出延迟。

解决了什么问题

现有流式语音识别器在说话人说完词后90-400ms才输出，对于语音代理等实时应用延迟过高。

核心方法

29M参数模型（4位量化16.8MB），训练时从随机截断的音频（包括词中间）学习预测所有应输出的词及接下来两个词；使用原始token概率作为置信度。

主要创新点

在词结束前预测输出，中位提前90ms；小模型实现低延迟高准确；置信度校准（ECE 3.7-5.5）。

实验结论

在LibriSpeech test-clean上中位词提前90ms预测正确；69%的词在说完前被正确预测（其他模型3-25%）；在所有六个计时测试集上最早；在八个英语Open ASR Leaderboard集上比Moonshine Tiny更准确，与Whistle大致相当。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

关注低延迟语音识别、语音代理和流式ASR的研究者与工程师。

和已有工作的关系

与发布的流式模型、Moonshine Tiny和Whistle进行了比较。

对研究的启发

利用部分词信息提前预测可大幅降低感知延迟；小模型可以通过特定训练策略实现竞争性性能。

是否值得精读

对于需要实时交互的语音应用，预测式识别提供了新颖的延迟优化思路。

MusicFlux: Flexible Conditioning for Text-Guided Music Generation and Editing

作者：Teysir Baoueb, Leonardo Boulitreau de Menezes Martins Marques, Paraskevas Stamatiadis, Ga{ë}l Richard

音乐生成评分 17audiomusicmusic generation查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

MusicFlux是一个基于预训练流匹配模型的文本引导音乐编辑器，通过两种条件化方法在编辑质量和计算效率之间取得平衡。

解决了什么问题

将图像编辑中统一的上下文方法直接应用于音乐编辑时，由于长音频序列导致计算成本过高。

核心方法

基于高效的预训练流匹配文本到音频模型；引入两种上下文条件化方法，一种优先编辑质量，另一种优先计算效率并保持原始序列长度。

主要创新点

两种条件化策略分别针对编辑质量和计算效率，且均与强基线竞争。

实验结论

实验显示两种方法均与强基线竞争，一种实现更好的编辑质量，另一种保持原序列长度并大幅提升计算效率。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

音乐生成、音频编辑和高效生成模型的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与将预训练文本到音乐模型适应于编辑任务的方法以及图像编辑中的上下文统一方法相关。

对研究的启发

在长音频序列中通过条件化设计平衡质量和效率；利用预训练流匹配模型进行音乐编辑是可行方向。

是否值得精读

对音乐编辑和高效音频生成有实际参考意义。

Emilia2: A Large-Scale Dataset for Controllable Long-Form Speech Generation

作者：Qinke Ni, Dekun Chen, Junwen Qiu, Huan Liao, Xu Li, Xingwen Han, Wan Lin, Kunyu Feng, Yuxiang Wang, Yusheng Mu, Chaoren Wang, Yingda Shen, Mingjie Chen, Haolong Zheng, Xulin Fan, Mark A. Hasegawa-Johnson, Zhizheng Wu

语音合成评分 17speechaudioacousticspeaker查看 arXiv ↗

一句话总结

Emilia2是一个229K小时的大规模多语言长篇语音生成数据集，支持多说话人、长时性和表达控制，并验证了训练模型的性能。

解决了什么问题

现有开源训练语料仅含短时示例，长形式基准每录音最多两个说话人，缺乏长时一致性、多说话人对话和表达控制的数据支持。

核心方法

从野外数据构建短句、单说话人长篇语音和多说话人对话，保留转录、说话人身份、停顿和声学过渡；提供4.77M示例的自然语言指令和933K示例的非语言发声标签；引入Emilia2-testset（23小时，最长30分钟，四个说话人，每个有参考音频）；训练AR和NAR模型。

主要创新点

大规模长时长数据集，超过五分钟示例占单说话人长篇50.0%和对话66.8%；多说话人对话占比高；引入自然语言指令和非语言发声标签支持表达控制；新测试集。

实验结论

0.6B骨干NAR模型在TTSD-Eval上取得中文CER 5.27%、英文WER 9.93%；AR模型在InstructTTSEval上与Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-VD相当（zh/en: 76.2/78.3 vs 77.1/77.9）。

可能局限

摘要中未明确说明。

适合谁读

语音合成、长篇语音生成、数据集构建和多说话人对话建模的研究者。

和已有工作的关系

与现有开源训练语料和长形式基准比较，与Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-VD比较。

对研究的启发

长时长和多说话人数据对长篇一致性至关重要；表达控制标注方式为可控生成提供新思路。

是否值得精读

作为数据为中心的语音生成研究，其数据集和测试集对后续工作有重要参考价值。

Alania-2: Turkish text-to-speech with instant and fine-tuned voice cloning2611.13845v1语音合成
展开摘要

We present Alania-2, a Turkish text-to-speech (TTS) system with two built-in voices, voice design from text descriptions, and two kinds of voice cloning. Alania-2 is a 488M-parameter model with the VoxCPM architecture, which generates continuous audio latents with a diffusion head on top of a language-model backbone. We train it from scratch on 18,195 hours of Turkish speech, 90% of which is synthetic speech produced by a larger teacher model and filtered automatically. A text normalizer rewrites numbers, abbreviations and foreign names into the words a Turkish speaker would say; without it, none of 96 test sentences with digits kept all its critical parts, such as an amount or a date, through synthesis and speech recognition, against 86 with it. On Freya-TR-Eval, a public benchmark of 495 Turkish sentences scored with automatic speech recognition, Alania-2 reaches a word error rate of 1.44%, compared with 5.99% for our previous system. On its built-in voices, it comes within half a point of character error rate of a 2.3B-parameter model at under a quarter of the size, and a 7.6M-parameter single-voice model trained with the same recipe matches the most intelligible systems we measured, which suggests that training data matters more for intelligibility than model size. We also report practical findings on voice cloning: conditioning on the reference audio alone, rather than continuing it, cuts skipped words from 21.9% to 1.7%; 20-30 seconds of reference audio works best; and a low-rank adapter trained in about two minutes raises speaker similarity well above instant cloning. Finally, we describe inference-engine changes that bring first audio down to 32 ms for a single stream. We release our evaluation harness and three Turkish speech and text datasets.

Walrus Technical Report2611.13689v1语音识别
展开摘要

We present Walrus, a unified 8B speech-and-audio foundation model handling speech recognition (including code-switching and multi-talker speech), speech-to-text and speech-to-speech translation, text-to-speech, emotion recognition, audio captioning, and general audio understanding. Our main contribution is not a new architecture but a data-centric training recipe; we use a standard decoder-only backbone initialized from Gemma 4, so that gains can be traced to the data. For speech generation, Walrus predicts low-bitrate discrete speech tokens, and a lightweight token-to-waveform decoder converts them into high-fidelity audio. The recipe is a three-stage data curriculum that moves from broad multilingual data to multi-talker, code-switching, and domain-specific data. At each stage, task-aware and dataset-aware weighting balances tasks with very different token budgets and protects core skills from forgetting. Trained on over 600M samples covering more than 20 tasks and 100 languages, Walrus leads on most multilingual, multi-talker, and code-switching ASR, speech translation, and audio understanding benchmarks, outperforming both open speech language models of comparable size and dedicated task-specific models. On most benchmarks, it even matches or surpasses the $4\times$ larger Qwen3-Omni's performance. Results suggest that data engineering is a primary driver of quality in multi-task speech and audio modeling.

Earlybird: Predictive Streaming Speech Recognition in 29M Parameters2611.14421v1语音识别
展开摘要

Streaming speech recognizers output a word only after the speaker has finished it, typically 90-400 ms later. For a voice agent every millisecond counts: the sooner the words are outputted, the sooner the agent can start a search or plan its reply. We present Earlybird, a 29M-parameter streaming recognizer (16.8 MB at 4 bits). Earlybird learns from audio cut off at random points, including partway through a word, and is asked to predict every word that is due by then in full, plus a guess of the next two words. Its raw token probabilities give a confidence for every word including the one still being spoken. On clean audiobook speech (LibriSpeech test-clean), the median word is predicted correctly 90 ms before the speaker finishes it, while released streaming models get it 90 to 400 ms after it ends. Earlybird is the earliest on all six test sets we time, covering audiobooks, TED talks, public speech, earnings calls, and synthetic speech. On the same audiobooks, 69% of words are predicted correctly before they end, against 3-25% for the other models. For words still being spoken, confidence is calibrated, with an expected calibration error (ECE) of 3.7 to 5.5. On the eight English Open ASR Leaderboard sets, it is more accurate than Moonshine Tiny on every set and roughly ties Whistle, a model of the same size.

MusicFlux: Flexible Conditioning for Text-Guided Music Generation and Editing2611.13834v1音乐生成
展开摘要

Music editing transforms existing recordings while preserving their original attributes. Recent approaches adapt pretrained text-to-music models for tasks like inpainting, event insertion, or restoration by conditioning them with context audio. It was recently proposed for image editing to unify generation and editing by treating the source image as in-context information. However, applying this directly to music is computationally expensive due to long audio sequences. In this work, we develop MusicFlux, a text-guided music editor built on an efficient pretrained flow-matching text-to-audio model. We introduce two conditioning approaches that trade off editing performance and computational efficiency. Experiments show that both are competitive with strong baselines, with one achieving better editing quality and the other preserving the original sequence length and substantially improving computational efficiency.

Emilia2: A Large-Scale Dataset for Controllable Long-Form Speech Generation2611.13614v1语音合成
展开摘要

Generating long-form speech requires models to keep content, speaker identity, turn-taking, and expressive style consistent over time. Yet most open training corpora contain examples lasting only tens of seconds and open long-form benchmarks cover at most two speakers per recording. We introduce Emilia2, a 229K-hour multilingual dataset for controllable long-form speech generation. From in-the-wild data, Emilia2 constructs short utterances, single-speaker long-form speech, and multi-speaker dialogues, preserving transcripts, speaker identities, pauses, and acoustic transitions. Examples longer than five minutes account for 50.0% of single-speaker long-form hours and 66.8% of dialogue hours. To support expressive control, Emilia2 provides natural language instructions for 4.77M examples and nonverbal-vocalization tags for 933K examples. We also introduce Emilia2-testset, 23 hours of bilingual recordings of up to 30 minutes and four speakers, each speaker paired with reference audio. We train autoregressive (AR) and non-autoregressive (NAR) models on Emilia2. Our 0.6B-backbone NAR model achieves the lowest Chinese CER and English WER on TTSD-Eval (5.27%/9.93%) among compared systems, while our AR model is on par with Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-VD on InstructTTSEval (zh/en: 76.2/78.3 vs. 77.1/77.9). These results validate Emilia2 as a comprehensive data foundation for long-form speech generation and minute-scale expressive control. Demo: https://charlesnii.github.io/Emilia2-demo/.

FaiSonic MMD: A popular music dataset with temporally aligned multidimensional annotations for multitask music analysis2611.13503v1其他
展开摘要

Temporal analysis in music information retrieval concerns the positions of musical events and structures within recordings. Joint modeling has demonstrated the value of combining musical descriptions, but existing resources differ in joint coverage and expert verification across dimensions, constraining detailed joint supervision and the analysis of relationships on the same recordings. To support multitask modeling and the study of relationships among musical dimensions in popular music, we introduce FaiSonic Multidimensional Music Dataset (FaiSonic MMD), containing 500 songs totaling approximately 32.86 hours, comprising 250 Chinese-language and 250 English-language songs. Professional musicians annotate phrases, sections, chords, and rhythm in real recordings, followed by independent review. Events and intervals across dimensions share a recording-based time reference while retaining their respective musical granularity and boundaries, allowing direct comparison across musical descriptions. We describe the annotations, collection procedure, and statistics, and establish a comprehensive multitask baseline for phrase boundaries, section boundaries, section functions, chords, beats, and downbeats. We report reference performance for all prediction targets. FaiSonic MMD provides shared expert annotations and accompanying benchmarks for temporal music analysis.

Antalia-2 Mini: A 7.6-million-parameter Turkish text-to-speech model trained entirely on synthetic speech2611.13811v1语音合成
展开摘要

Antalia-2 Mini is a single-voice Turkish text-to-speech model with 7,620,931 parameters. It reads characters and produces 48 kHz audio at 41 times real time on an Apple M5 laptop CPU (2 threads). Its training audio came only from VoxCPM2 (the teacher), about 300 times larger, cloning one synthetic clip; automatic checks kept about 1,790 h.
